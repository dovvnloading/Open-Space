# Research Notes of the AAS: drafts

Two drafts, one per result:

- `ogle-2026-blg-0058/note.tex` with `figure1.pdf`: the candidate isolated black hole.
- `neowise-nuclear-flares/note.tex`: the seven unreported nuclear infrared flares (one table, no figure).

Both are about 1,000–1,100 words (limit 1,500) with abstracts of about 130 words (limit 150). They have not been compiled here; use the AASTeX v7 template on Overleaf.

## Before you submit

1. **Read every sentence and check every number.** The AAS policy on AI use requires it, and the notes go out under your name. Each number traces to the result pages in `results/`.
2. **Check every reference in ADS.** In particular, check the full author list of Necker et al. (2025), the volume and page of Masterson et al. (2024), and the UAT keyword numbers.
3. **Fill in your details:** ORCID, affiliation and email (marked `AFFILIATION` and `EMAIL`).
4. **Get a permanent DOI.** Link the GitHub repository to Zenodo, publish a release, and replace `Zenodo DOI: XXXX` in both notes.
5. **Keep the AI disclosure** at the end of each note, and confirm it describes what was done.
6. **For the black-hole note, tell the OGLE team first.** It's a courtesy, since the result rests on their public data.
7. **Submit** through the Research Notes page: https://journals.aas.org/research-notes/

## Messages, in sending order

All four are drafts in `messages/`. Send them from your own account and fill in `[Name]` and `[Affiliation]`. Take each recipient's address from their institutional page; none is guessed here.

1. **`01-ogle-courtesy-email.md`**: tell the OGLE team before anything is public. Allow about a week for a reply before step 2.
2. **Submit the two Research Notes.**
3. **`04-atel-ogle-2026-blg-0058.md`**: Astronomer's Telegram for the black-hole candidate, which alerts observers before the 2027 bulge season. It needs an ATel account.
4. **`02-astrometry-followup-email.md`** and **`03-flare-followup-email.md`**: ask specialist teams for the decisive observations. These are strongest once the notes are published and can be cited.

Rules used: https://journals.aas.org/research-note-preparation-guidelines/ and https://journals.aas.org/author-llm-guidelines/ (both read on 15 November 2026).
