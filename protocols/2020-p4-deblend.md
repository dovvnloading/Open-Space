# A fourth condensation reported near C/2020 P4

A literature check during the Kreutz scan surfaced a separate, non-group comet with a prior unresolved report. On 2020 August 7 Worachate Boonplod reported an object near C/2020 P4 at 12:00, 12:12 and 12:24 UTC, at upper-left image coordinates (699,2), (709,8), (720,13). On September 3 Karl Battams wrote that the putative fragment lay too close to a known component for confirmation. The MPC listed three components in MPEC 2020-Q231. This is a targeted remeasurement of Boonplod’s report.

Use the LASCO C2 Orange/Clear exposures, project forward outside those three reported positions, fit the known components and compare the putative residual with an asymmetric coma or tail and the response of the camera. Test separate subsets of exposures. The prior claim is known and should receive attribution in any result. A residual unresolved from the coma warrants an inconclusive disposition.
