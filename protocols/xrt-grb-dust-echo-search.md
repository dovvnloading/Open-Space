# Swift XRT search for dust-scattered gamma-ray burst light

Search previously occupied Swift gamma-ray burst fields for delayed, expanding X-ray rings. A prompt flash scattered through a thin Galactic dust layer should concentrate photons at approximately constant angular separation squared divided by elapsed time. The observing unit is an XRT photon-counting event with a sky position, energy and time after the trigger.

Screen bright prompt bursts with available XRT photon-counting imaging. Preserve the field list and all selection changes. Use 0.7–3 keV for initial sensitivity, mask the direct point source and examine the exposure footprint. Compare the signal with azimuthal coverage, harder photons, field sources, time-shifted or shuffled events and the point-spread function. Require independently populated time intervals for a candidate. A literature search for the exact burst and dust scattering distinguishes a recovered published echo from a potentially new measurement.

This document records the initial question and acceptance tests. It contains no observational result.
