# Kreutz companion search

## Question frozen before measuring

Can coronagraph images resolve a previously unreported companion, or demonstrate two catalogued Kreutz sungrazers sharing a sky track and a measurable along-track separation? Use the SOHO Sungrazer discovery list to choose windows, identify already reported companions and recover known comets before interpreting a candidate. Record the coverage of any scan.

For a proposed new source require motion in multiple original camera exposures, reproducibility on disjoint frames, comparison with a local sky background and camera artifacts, and a search against project reports and designations. For catalogued objects compare projected tracks at common times and use independent frames where available. Source associations seen from a single viewing direction are projected. A recent physical breakup requires additional evidence. Preserve the most plausible alternative and a falsifiable next measurement for each reported result.
