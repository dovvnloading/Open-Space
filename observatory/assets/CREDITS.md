# Observatory artwork

Open-Space created these illustrations with OpenAI ImageGen for the public observatory. The images give visitors a visual introduction to each subject. Each investigation links to its measurements and observing records separately. The artwork is distributed under the [repository license](../../LICENSE) to the extent applicable.

| Asset | Art direction |
| --- | --- |
| `mars.webp` | A copper Mars globe lit from the upper left, with dark albedo markings, craters and a canyon. |
| `solar.webp` | Filaments arching above the solar limb in an amber extreme-ultraviolet-inspired palette. |
| `galaxy.webp` | An inclined spiral galaxy with violet arms, dust lanes and an ivory core. |
| `dwarf.webp` | An imagined cold brown dwarf with banded indigo clouds and an icy blue limb. |
