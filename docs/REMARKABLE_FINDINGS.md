# Remarkable findings

**DESI DR1, Gaia DR3 1717410430709663616 — evidence level: spectroscopically supported candidate, single epoch (binary motion untested).** Five stellar absorption lines support a corrected heliocentric RV of +501.74 ± 4.65 km/s. The inferred Galactocentric speed is 697 km/s (691–703 km/s) on the reference distance scale: all 4,096 draws exceed escape speed in McMillan 2017 and Gala 2022, but a published higher-mass halo binds all 4,096 reference draws. The 256-draw backward orbit ensemble crosses the disk 7.40 kpc from the Galactic centre about 10.9 Myr ago, with no reference orbit approaching within 1 kpc of Sgr A* or 5 kpc of the moving LMC centre. **No Sgr A* or LMC-centre ejection is established**, and neither a >1,000 km/s black-hole ejection nor an LMC black-hole mass constraint was found. A second independent RV epoch is essential to test the systemic velocity; discovery priority remains open after a finite 21-source literature comparison. [Measurements, controls and origin tests](../results/dr1-dr19-unbound-stars/README.md)

**DESI DR1 optical SETI — evidence level: independently reproduced stellar-template false positive.** Seven repeat-exposure narrow-line triggers clustered at the Li I resonance doublet near 6709.7 Å in a blind search. Before opening a separate sky field, the search predicted the same effect in new stars. Five stars in that field reproduced it on three independent nights each. Across all 137 held-out stars, 89 showed a positive template residual at lithium at ≥3σ when the available exposures were combined; neither prespecified comparison wavelength produced one. The release's fitted absorption is systematically deeper than the observed feature. All 12 blind triggers in these initial fields coincide with a known transition.
