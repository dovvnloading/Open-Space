# Remarkable findings

**Perdita cleared its nominal known-moon mask in two independent JWST visits.** The evidence combines multi-epoch astrometry with an independent published image identification. A compact source in 153 NIRCam integrations was a median 0.50 arcsec from JPL's URA184 Perdita prediction in February 2025 and 1.17 arcsec away in October. On the favored 387-revolution circular fit its radius is 76,418.1 km, and NASA's labeled February image identifies the source as Perdita. The observing team had previously reported a qualitative prediction discrepancy. This search measures its size at both epochs and demonstrates how a known moon can pass a two-visit search that uses nominal ephemeris masks.
