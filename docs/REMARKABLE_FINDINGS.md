# Remarkable findings

**DESI DR1, Gaia DR3 1717410430709663616 — evidence level: spectroscopically supported candidate, single epoch (binary motion untested).** Five stellar absorption lines support a corrected heliocentric RV of +501.74 ± 4.65 km/s. The inferred Galactocentric speed is 697 km/s (691–703 km/s) on the reference distance scale: all 4,096 draws exceed escape speed in McMillan 2017 and Gala 2022, but a published higher-mass halo binds all 4,096 reference draws. The 256-draw backward orbit ensemble crosses the disk 7.40 kpc from the Galactic centre about 10.9 Myr ago, with no reference orbit approaching within 1 kpc of Sgr A* or 5 kpc of the moving LMC centre. **No Sgr A* or LMC-centre ejection is established**, and neither a >1,000 km/s black-hole ejection nor an LMC black-hole mass constraint was found. A second independent RV epoch is essential to test the systemic velocity; discovery priority remains open after a finite 21-source literature comparison.
