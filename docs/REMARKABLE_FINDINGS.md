# Remarkable findings

**Perdita crossed the nominal known-moon masks in independent JWST observations in 2025 and 2026.** The evidence combines multi-epoch native-detector astrometry, a frozen-trajectory follow-up and an independent identification in the discovery team’s labeled image. The source was a median 0.50 arcsec from JPL URA184 in February 2025 and 1.17 arcsec away in October 2025. A new blind search in two October 2026 visits recovered a source at Perdita’s radius about 1.9 arcsec from nominal, and a third visit confirmed it. The median projected longitude residual reached −19.7° across four 2026 exposures. Earlier 2023–24 images give residuals of order one degree. The observing team previously reported a qualitative discrepancy. These measurements quantify the offset and show how an ordinary catalog mask can admit a known moon through a multi-visit discovery search.
