# Frozen search for separated emission in GRB 261115A

The observing unit is a single public Fermi GBM trigger, 816473458 / bn261115924, registered at 2026-11-15 22:10:53.44 UTC. This is a targeted screen in one event. The frozen primary question is whether a temporally detached, count-rate excess outside the known prompt interval appears at the same time in the two best-facing NaI detectors and passes a third-detector and directional check. The search was specified before reading the SPECTRUM count columns or examining plots.

## Selection and time coverage

[Fermi GCN 46299](https://gcn.nasa.gov/circulars/46299) places the burst at RA 5.9°, Dec −31.1°, with 2.13° statistical uncertainty. [GECAM-B GCN 46305](https://gcn.nasa.gov/circulars/46305) reports multiple pulses, T90 of 4.0 +3.5/−0.5 s, and its spectral interval corresponds to approximately [−0.69, +3.81] s on the Fermi clock. Energy bands and sensitivity differ. The prespecified Fermi screen excludes [−3, +8] s around this prompt event. Its pre and late intervals are [−32, −3] and [8, 40] s. The known-event positive control integrates complete native bins within [−1, 4] s. Independent off-event time controls [−106, −77] and [77, 109] s diagnose false activations outside the bracketing background. The time coordinate is each CTIME bin's TT mission elapsed time minus its TRIGTIME, and no crossing bin is split. Scans use complete contiguous native 64 or 256 ms bins at nominal widths 0.256, 0.512, 1.024 and 2.048 s with an adjacent same-cadence guard on each side.

The TCAT direction and spacecraft axes combined with the NaI normals from [Meegan et al., Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/702/1/791) select n9 (16.7°) and n6 (34.2°) for the primary statistic, n7 (44.1°) for a separate detector check, and n4/n5 (120.3°/122.5°) for directional diagnostics. Geometry is an approximate trigger-time proxy for count response. The detectors share spacecraft, the same burst and potentially particle backgrounds, and distant viewing angles do not eliminate scatter. Fixed pulse-height channels 2–4 span approximately 26–296 keV; the exact detector-specific boundaries are retained. Coverage excludes nonzero QUALITY, bins outside GTIs, and exposure fractions below 0.90. All selected detectors must have the same native time grid.

## Test and falsification

Compare every window separately with the two disjoint sidebands [−72, −36] and [44, 72] s. Use each detector's own summed live time; require at least 90% live exposure over each sideband. For an on-window count X and sideband count B, conditional on X+B use the upper binomial tail with probability E_on/(E_on+E_side). This is an exact count test for equal constant Poisson rates and conservative if the candidate's baseline rate is no higher than the particular reference rate. The maximum of the two sideband p-values avoids dependence on selecting a favorable background. The maximum over n9 and n6 requires both to pass and needs no independence between their p-values for this intersection rule. Multiply by the number of primary pre plus late windows, including all widths, capped at one. The threshold is family-adjusted p ≤ 0.01. A candidate must also show the same-window p ≤ 0.01 in n7 and avoid p ≤ 0.01 in either n4 or n5. Any above-threshold time-control activation using the signal-sized trial correction flags the background model for review before interpreting a candidate. All windows and quality coverage are deposited. The known prompt interval is reported without borrowing it to set the threshold.

The reference comparison does not model a local rise above both sidebands, count-rate-dependent systematics, correlated particle backgrounds, source response or dead-time effects beyond provided exposure. Report rates by sideband and subinterval and the fixed time and direction diagnostics. [Fermi caveats](https://fermi.gsfc.nasa.gov/ssc/data/analysis/GBM_caveats.html) describe rate artifacts near cadence changes. This is a conservative, limited screen for separated count excesses, with no search inside the prompt guard, beyond the frozen time windows, below 256 ms, above 2.048 s, or in other energy channels. An empty screen applies only to tested windows. It is not a fluence upper limit or a claim that the burst had no precursor or extended emission. A viable candidate would need a second spacecraft and response- and background-aware modeling before an astrophysical claim. [Established GBM precursor search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.03246) and [official targeted search](https://fermi.gsfc.nasa.gov/ssc/data/access/gbm/targeted_search/about.html) provide prior methods.
