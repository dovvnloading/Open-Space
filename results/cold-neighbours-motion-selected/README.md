# Motion-selected cold neighbours of the Sun

**Search in progress. No new object, measured distance, or survey depth has been established.**

This investigation starts from blind infrared source selection. Previously identified ultracool dwarfs serve only as recovery controls. The scientific target is a previously unreported late-T/Y dwarf within 20 pc, with special attention to distances below 5 pc and temperatures below the estimated temperature of WISE 0855. Catalogue matches, sky coverage, selection losses, and the evidence for each surviving trajectory will be recorded here as they are measured.
