# Motion-selected cold neighbours of the Sun

**Three sources pass the bright infrared-motion screen in 84.80 deg². Two are published objects. The third, CWISE J182532.09+671906.2, has zero qualifying unTimely W2 centroids within 1.5 arcsec of its predicted track. The other two have 16 and 17 matching epochs.**

The three disjoint 3°-radius cones are centred on the Euclid deep fields. Their combined 3,601,560 CatWISE2020 rows include 1,905,431 W2 motion-fit S/N ≥ 8 measurements. Of 11,299 rows in the loose colour and motion prefilter, 658 pass the colour and significance screen. Three pass the additional W2 ≤ 16.5 Vega and image-fit quality requirements. The geometric cones extend outside the Euclid Q1 imaging footprint. This is a catalogue screen, with no inferred completeness in distance, temperature or sky volume.

See the pilot selection and exact TAP queries in [pilot/](pilot/). The input manifest contains SHA-256 checksums. The bright follow-up covers CWISE J181125.34+665806.4, CWISE J182532.09+671906.2 and CWISE J041358.20−475038.1. The WISE 0855 control has been recovered in 21 NEOWISE visits and in original SPHEREx DR1 images. The parallax becomes poorly constrained if free scan-direction offsets are fitted. See [the astrometric fits](control0855/fit.json), [the DR1 band fluxes](spherex/wise0855_bands.csv), [Gaia and Q1 queries](counterparts/bright_matches.json) and [the literature crosschecks](catalogues/pilot_matches.csv). Of 658 pilot rows, only the two published dwarfs meet the multi-epoch W2 track criterion. A native-image test using 128 2014/2023 frames rejects persistent emission at the third bright source’s reported motion and flux. See [image difference and control positions](heldout1825/README.md).

Using the best available observation-epoch positions for the controls, the primary CatWISE selection recovers 31 of 41 Y-classed UltracoolSheet entries with catalogue parallaxes ≥50 mas. Thirty pass the bright cuts. This includes both controls within 5 pc. WISE 0855 appears in three qualifying CatWISE entries. The initial J2000-only association gave 25/41 and missed WISE 0855 because its propagated position was displaced by 3.19″ from the 20 pc census prediction. The [epoch comparison](controls/epoch_matched.csv) preserves this distinction. A separate [W2-only trajectory search](fast/README.md) recovered WISE 0855 in all 16 visits without a CatWISE seed. The wider sample and motion-first links are undergoing additional checks.
