# A search for cold neighbours of the Sun

**No previously unreported late-T or Y dwarf is validated by this search.** In 84.80 deg² around the Euclid deep fields, two of 658 red CatWISE motion entries reproduced their tracks across five or more W2 epochs over at least five years and beat stationary and rotated tracks. Both are published dwarfs. A separate 4,235.10 deg² bright-source screen and a motion-first search found no new source with a secure cold spectrum and a parallax placing it within 20 pc.

The 24 wide caps are disjoint from the pilot cones and from the W2-only fields. The wide and pilot footprints together span 4,319.90 deg² at different selection depths. The 43 motion-first circles lie on tiles that contributed a CatWISE selection to the first search. The W2 linker searched all qualifying epoch detections inside each circle. The WISE 0855 control circle was deliberately centred near its trajectory and contributes no blind sky area.

Of the 988 wide entries without a literature alert, **zero** met the independent 2019–2024 NEOWISE single-exposure motion criterion. There were 363 with no W2 S/N ≥3 detection within 5″ of the predicted track, 620 with sparse or inconclusive evidence, and five borderline associations with pre-existing stationary sources. Those five were selected by the single-exposure test and differ from the five selected by the six-epoch coadd test. The T8 control in the same wide sample passed. ## Astrometry, pixels and spectra

- **WISE 0855** was recovered in 21 NEOWISE visits over 9.95 years. The fit gives µα cos δ = −8.125 ± 0.014″/yr, µδ = +0.654 ± 0.018″/yr and relative parallax 434 ± 44 mas under a common scan calibration. Free scan-direction centroid offsets inflate the parallax uncertainty to 1,630 mas. That degeneracy matters for any proposed nearby object with WISE-only astrometry. The independent W2 linker recovered its trajectory over 16 visits without a CatWISE seed. [Fit, comparison and native images](control0855/README.md)
- **SPHEREx DR1** forced PSF photometry used 1,113 distinct target–image measurements from 1,112 original products at nine trial or control tracks. Two duplicate WISE 0855 cutouts were removed before aggregation. Of the distinct measurements, 919 passed the field-of-view and pixel-quality tests. WISE 0855 has −0.2 ± 21.6 µJy at 3.20–3.45 µm and 596.4 ± 40.5 µJy at 4.40–4.62 µm, using visit-jackknife errors. The broad methane-band depression and 4–5 µm maximum are recovered. Five empirical late-T/Y templates and 1,025 redshifted galaxy/AGN alternatives were tested on common samples. The blind leads produced no secure subtype, individual CO/CO₂ identification or temperature. For J192049.57+111222.6, changing the fitted sky from a plane to a quadratic surface changed the red-window flux from −300 ± 89 to +83 ± 95 µJy. [Images, templates and replay](spherex/NOTES.md) · [Six-track spectra](spherex/dr1_spectra.png) · [Three additional tracks](spherex/additional_spectra.png)
- **The most persistent blind W2-only link** appeared in 14 epochs. The detections switch between components of a stationary blend. In 96 native W2 exposures the predicted motion residual is −0.55 and −0.61 local MAD units in the two scan seasons. Injecting a source at the claimed brightness and motion at the same blend produces +27.4 and +28.8. Two further links with hundreds of catalogue detections were also explained by stationary counterparts. [Fourteen-epoch image test](fast/blend2746/README.md) · [Fixed-source tests](fast-alternatives/README.md)
- **Asteroids and artifacts** were tested using elapsed time, scan direction, single-frame contamination and Solar System flags where available, split exposures, source-centroid histories, and native W2 images for the leading trajectories. Repeated detections in the catalogue can belong to a different source at each epoch. CatWISE and unTimely share some underlying photons. NEOWISE images from later years provide the time holdout. ## How far this result reaches

The CatWISE primary cuts recover **31 of 41** nearby published Y-class controls, including both with nominal distance ≤5 pc. Thirty pass the bright-tier cuts. An initial match propagated from the UltracoolSheet J2000 coordinates recovered 25. Substituting near-epoch census astrometry where available corrected six associations, including WISE 0855. It enters the selection in three CatWISE fragments. [Per-object recovery and radius checks](controls/README.md)

An independent check of the held-out single-exposure criterion recovered 11 of 29 literature-alert positions in the wide sample. It recovered 3/3 brighter than W2=14, 6/9 at 14–14.5, 2/6 at 14.5–15, and 0/11 at 15–15.5. This is a reference cohort, with possible positional associations, and measures a different stage from the 41-object Y-control test. [Single-exposure sensitivity](reference-batch/README.md)

The pilot and bright-cap results are **bounded selection nulls**. The deep six-epoch W2 test recovered 24 of the 29 wide literature-alert positions. Of the 1,017 wide entries, 1,012 had three or more epochs whose local sensitivity estimate reached the catalogue W2 flux. For the pilot, only 191 of the 658 trials had at least five epochs whose local four-sigma sensitivity estimate reached the catalogue W2 flux. The other trials have weaker exclusions. For an object with the measured W2 luminosity of WISE 0855, the expected W2 magnitudes at 5, 10 and 20 pc are about 15.66, 17.17 and 18.67. The wide W2 ≤15.5 cut reaches only about 4.6 pc for that luminosity before losses to motion, confusion or masking. At 20 pc the 0.3″/yr motion threshold also excludes objects with tangential speeds below 28.4 km/s. The known controls and local image injections do not calibrate an all-sky effective volume. This search sets no space-density limit and leaves open the possibility of a colder or closer neighbour.

## Reproduction

Run the following from this directory with Python, NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Astropy, Matplotlib and Pillow. The component READMEs describe the image reconstructions and the archive queries. The frozen CatWISE responses and cropped unTimely detection tables allow the pilot selection and association scores to be reproduced offline.

~~~bash
python verify.py
python pilot/unpack_inputs.py
python pilot/select_catwise.py
python pilot/score_tracks.py
python wide/select_wide.py
~~~

Exact URLs, ADQL or cone parameters and input SHA-256 digests accompany each measurement series. The investigation uses the NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive, WISE/NEOWISE, CatWISE2020, unWISE/unTimely, ESA Gaia DR3 and Euclid Q1, SPHEREx DR1 spectral images (DOI 10.26131/IRSA678), CDS/VizieR, the cited published compilations and the SWIRE template library. [SPHEREx DR1 data release](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/SPHEREx/docs/overview_dr1.html)
