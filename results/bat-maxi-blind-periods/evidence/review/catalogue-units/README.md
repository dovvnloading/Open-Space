# Orbital-period units in two LMXB catalogues

The Fortin Period and Avakyan Porb VOTable fields declare d and d. Among 98 common objects with positive numerical periods, 87 Fortin-to-Avakyan ratios fell within 5% of 24, 1 within 5% of one, and 10 outside both intervals.

The match requires an exact normalized primary or alternative name, positions within 60 arcseconds, and a unique association in both directions. Coordinate signs are preserved. There were 241 reciprocal name-and-position matches before requiring positive periods. The [full comparison](source-comparison.csv) includes the flags and original period-reference bibcodes.
