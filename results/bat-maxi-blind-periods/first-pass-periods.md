# Periods retained with the original formal-error weights

The first blind pass retained three periods. Each agrees with a published orbital period. The source names listed here entered the literature review after independent detection in BAT and MAXI.

| Source | BAT period in days (one standard error) | Prior orbital period in days | Publication |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 4U 1909+07 (X1908+075) | 4.40023 ± 0.00021 | 4.400 ± 0.001 | [Wen et al. (2000)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2000ApJ...532.1119W/abstract) |
| IGR J18483−0311 | 18.5492 ± 0.0021 | 18.5482 ± 0.0088 | [Jain, Paul & Dutta (2009)](https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.4563) |
| XTE J1855−026 | 6.07424 ± 0.00018 | 6.07413 ± 0.00004 | [Coley, Corbet & Krimm (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.03051) |

The first-pass source records, folds, amplitudes, false-alarm probabilities and literature queries are available at the [frozen first-pass revision](https://github.com/dovvnloading/Open-Space/tree/af5953ee0a38d31fcfb07197834dcbb3447064de/results/bat-maxi-blind-periods/evidence).
