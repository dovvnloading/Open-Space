# Acqua Alta tower, northern Adriatic Sea, November 6–7, 2025

The two NASA PACE matchups lie over a shelf depth of approximately 19 m in the ancillary elevation field. The field station is about 15 km from the Venice lagoon and sits in optically complex water. Absolute reflectance fell across the green and red bands. In the water measurements Rrs560 fell from 0.00668 to 0.00374 sr⁻¹, Rrs620 from 0.00217 to 0.000597, and Rrs665 from 0.00145 to 0.000422. OCI recorded declines of 36%, 56%, and 54% at those wavelengths. For wavelength-linear interpolation in log reflectance, ΔC560/620/665 was −0.174 in OCI and −0.337 in the field. Both 5×5 satellite boxes retained 25 pixels. Reported spatial CV was 0.102 and 0.0644 and QWIP was +0.0450 and +0.0596. Field-to-satellite separations were 3 and 79 minutes, respectively. Solar zenith was 61.8° and 63.5°. These conditions and the offshore field measurements support a real local change. They leave uncertainty in the satellite's absolute magnitudes, the source of suspended material and the shape of the full spatial footprint. ## Historical check

AERONET-OC V3 Level 1.5 daily mean normalized, bidirectionally corrected water-leaving radiances provide a second temporal aggregation at the same platform. The same nominal wavelengths (560, 620, 667 nm) are available for a comparison in 2017–25. Fixed band-dependent normalization constants cancel for temporal changes in log curvature. Daily ΔC was −0.18676, with a low value persisting on November 8. The Oct 7–Dec 7 windows in 2017–24 contain 151 exact one-day increments. Five were at least as negative, most recently November 4, 2024 (−0.21680). Overlapping intervals and selection from the original screen make this a descriptive comparison. The Level 1.5 field archive has provisional calibration. The same physical radiometer contributed to NASA's fixed-platform matchups, whose field rows are labeled V4 Level 1.5. [AERONET-OC data service](https://aeronet.gsfc.nasa.gov/print_web_data_help_v3_seaprism.html)

The November 7 daily endpoint had C = −0.4914. Across earlier seasonal days whose 560 nm normalized radiance fell within 15% of its value, the median was −0.4975 and 18 of 33 had an equally low or lower curvature. The table also reports 10% and 20% matching windows. Matching on a wavelength in C makes this a descriptive optical-state comparison. It shows that the low endpoint shape fits the historical local distribution for similarly green-bright water.

## Discharge, wind, waves and currents

The daily mean discharge of the Po at Pontelagoscuro declined from 1,422 m³/s on November 5 to 1,241 on November 6 and 1,118 on November 7. These were measured upstream of the delta. Timing at the tower also depends on plume paths, lag and smaller nearer rivers. The November 6 district report identifies ordinary 30-day streamflow-index conditions. Its latest observation covers November 5. The daily values in the CSV come from the subsequently published monthly table. [ARPAE November 2025 bulletin, p. 37, Table 1](https://www.arpae.it/it/temi-ambientali/meteo/report-meteo/bollettini-mensili/bm_202511.pdf) · [Po District weekly bulletin](https://www.adbpo.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/aggiornamento-settimanale_20251106.pdf)

At Venice Marco Polo airport (LIPZ, about 25 km from the platform), the near-overpass wind reports changed from 4 kt from 060° (November 6, 11:20 UTC) to 8 kt from 070° (November 7, 11:50 UTC). The daily median reported wind rose from 3 to 8 kt. These airport observations and strengthening short northeast waves are consistent with an offshore wind-driven transport change. [Iowa Environmental Mesonet METAR archive](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/download.phtml)

At the nearest sampled sea grid cell (45.291664° N, 12.541672° E), Météo-France wave model output at the nearest clock hour changed from 0.12 m significant height, 2.60 s and 060° to 0.42 m, 3.05 s and 057°. Modeled surface current increased from 0.2 km/h toward 180° to 0.4 km/h toward 207°. Modeled sea level at the same times was −0.32 and −0.39 m relative to mean sea level. The wave and current directions use different conventions, from for waves and toward for current. These are coastal model grid cells with limited spatial resolution. The airport wind is a direct observation. The city archive lists tower wind/tide but the downloaded response was an HTML challenge, so no observed tower tide or wave height entered this comparison. Wave model heights and periods do not by themselves establish sediment mobilization at the tower. [Wave and current model documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/marine-weather-api) · [Venice municipal tower archive](https://www.comune.venezia.it/it/content/3-piattaforma-ismar-cnr)

The observed strong reduction in green-to-red reflectance is compatible with removal, dilution or advection of particle-rich coastal water. Wind-induced resuspension, river forcing and biological changes are documented parts of the regional optical variability. The simultaneous red-band declines and similar earlier seasonal events support treating this as a follow-up case. The station measurements do not identify particle mineralogy, distinguish advection from settling or resolve phytoplankton composition. ## Reproduction and credit

Run `python aaot_followup.py raw --out reproduced` from this directory. The source script requires Python 3.12, NumPy, pandas and curl. Use `--matchup PATH` to reuse the NASA release downloaded for the main matchup screen. The source manifest identifies each query and the bytes used. Open-Meteo response metadata includes server generation time, which can change the raw file digest even for identical numerical fields. `spectra.csv` uses sr⁻¹, with field wavelengths left blank where unavailable. `modeled_marine_hours.csv` uses UTC, metres, seconds, degrees and km/h. `airport_wind.csv` preserves the raw METAR strings and knots. Only the Po PDF table required manual transcription. The reproducer verifies the digest before emitting that extract.

The PACE mission, OBPG, SeaBASS, AERONET-OC AAOT PI Barbara Bulgarelli and collaborating teams provided the radiometry. AERONET-OC requests offering the site PI co-authorship for research use. ARPAE Emilia-Romagna provided river discharge, airport METAR operators and Iowa Environmental Mesonet provided wind reports, and Copernicus Marine, Météo-France and Open-Meteo provided modeled marine conditions.
