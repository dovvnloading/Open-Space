# Independent Sentinel-3 checks and river discharge

The Copernicus Marine GlobColour daily, 4 km OLCI-only product combines Sentinel-3A and Sentinel-3B. The observables are fully normalized, binned remote-sensing reflectances in sr⁻¹. Native centers are 443, 490 and 560 nm. The log-linear curvature at 490 nm is `log(Rrs490) − (70/117) log(Rrs443) − (47/117) log(Rrs560)`. The nominal wavelengths approximate the bands' different spectral response functions. Times are UTC product days. A daily 4 km cell can differ from the PACE footprint in both overpass time and area. Contains modified Copernicus Marine Service information, accessed 2026-11-16. [Product and DOI](https://doi.org/10.48670/moi-00280) · [Source dataset](https://stac.marine.copernicus.eu/metadata/OCEANCOLOUR_GLO_BGC_L3_MY_009_103/cmems_obs-oc_glo_bgc-reflectance_my_l3-olci-4km_P1D_202207/dataset.stac.json)

## Southern California

The nearest OLCI cell to USC SeaPRISM is centered at 33.56250° N, 118.10416° W, 1.27 km from the platform. Curvature decreased by 0.445 on March 11–15 and 0.298 on April 10–15, 2024. For March, 18 of 255 paired cells within 50 km declined by at least 0.25, and the median change was −0.00445. In April, 26 of 104 paired cells met that magnitude threshold and the median was −0.0906. The denominators include only cells with positive values in all three bands on both endpoint dates. Nominal areas use spherical grid-cell areas and describe the sampled cells, with no interpolation through clouds. `olci_stations.csv` contains the endpoint bands and 3×3 summaries. `olci_paired_cells.csv` gives paired changes within 125 km, and `olci_spatial.csv` gives cumulative summaries by radius. The `grid_cells` denominator includes land.

The seasonal comparison uses other years, the same interval length and a ±15-day window around the start date. A 3×3 neighborhood must have at least five same-cell temporal pairs. Across nine comparison years, 12/93 March and 12/75 April neighborhood changes were at least as negative as the respective event. For the single central cell, the counts were 0/89 and 8/72. Candidate selection, overlapping comparison periods, spatial autocorrelation and cloud-dependent coverage preclude interpreting these counts as independent-trial significance tests. `olci_seasonal_reference.csv` and `olci_seasonal_summary.csv` retain the reference samples and event denominators.

At the Santa Ana River gauge in Santa Ana, the approved daily mean flow fell from 500 to 6.56 ft³/s over the March window, following 669 ft³/s on March 7. In April it rose from 2.72 to 34.6 ft³/s, reaching 76.0 ft³/s on April 14. The gauge is upstream of the mouth. These data cannot resolve transport time, plume location, suspended particles or colored dissolved matter at the platform. Runoff and nearshore plume transport are established regional explanations. [USGS gauge](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-11078000/) · [Southern California Bight field observations](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csr.2009.06.011)

## Florida

`olci_footprints.csv` pairs each screened PACE position with the nearest OLCI grid cell on each date. It retains native reflectances, the two curvature values per sensor, center coordinates, center separation, and a sensitivity flag. `olci_footprints_summary.csv` computes quantiles and Pearson correlations only from pairs observed by both sensors. The sensitivity subset requires 0 < Rrs < 0.05 sr⁻¹ at the three blue/green OLCI bands, Rrs560 > 0.00005, 0 ≤ Rrs665 < 0.0015 and Rrs665/Rrs560 < 0.5 at both endpoints. The eastern Florida May 13–17, 2025 decline and May 17–21 rebound both lose their magnitude in OLCI. Of seven May 2024 Gulf cruise matchups in the NASA validation viewer, all three May 22 stations had OLCI coverage and all four May 24 stations lacked coverage even in 3×3 neighborhoods. [NASA matchup viewer](https://seabass.gsfc.nasa.gov/validation-viewer/)

## Additional stations and the northern Adriatic

Five additional PACE and field candidates were checked on the OLCI grid (`other_station_checks.csv`). Casablanca's surrounding red curvature moved in the opposite direction. Irbe's 3×3 blue curvature change was small and opposite to the field change. The California August 2025 interval had no paired OLCI colors within 50 km. The Section-7 platform in the western Black Sea had a positive station change of 0.425 in March 2026. Within 50 km the median was 0.0556. Three of 33 comparison intervals had a 3×3 median at least as large as Section-7's 0.372. For these five platforms every historical 3×3 interval has the same lag, a start date in the ±15-day calendar window and at least five valid matched cells. The counts select the tail in the direction of the observed OLCI neighborhood change.

At the Acqua Alta tower off Venice, 620 nm log-curvature relative to 560 and 665 nm fell by 0.114 in the nearest OLCI cell from November 6 to 7, 2025. The decline was 0.139 in the median of six paired neighbors. One of 61 historical 3×3 intervals had as large a decline. The regional sensitivity analysis used nominal 4 km footprints with wet ETOPO1 centers. Within 50 km, 268 cells sampled 4,050 km², of which 3,400 km² showed declines. The median change was −0.0826. Using all available autumns outside 2025 in the OLCI archive, one-day control periods starting within ±15 calendar days were evaluated on the same wet cells. At least half of the 268 event cells had to be valid on both reference days. Two of 58 historical changes exceeded the matched event decline, beginning October 25, 2019 and October 30, 2026. `aaot_regional_seasonal.csv` reports both medians on each exact intersection. Historical samples overlap temporally and their coverage depends on cloud conditions.

The all-band data in `aaot_spectral_context.csv` show a red-brightening pulse on November 6. From November 5 to 6, median paired Rrs620 increased 34.4% and Rrs665 increased 34.0%. From November 6 to 7, these bands fell 26.4% and 26.0%, respectively, while Rrs560 fell 9.0%. The direct November 5–7 comparison on 262 cells gives Rrs620 +0.59%, Rrs665 −0.39% and a median curvature change of +0.0108. The linear 681 nm line-height statistic uses a wavelength-linear continuum with 665 and 709 nm anchors. Its median change was −0.000015 sr⁻¹ while the logarithmic 681 nm curvature increased. Spectral curvature alone does not resolve pigments, sediment or organic detritus. Bank depth, particle transport and seasonal analogs warrant particular attention. The AAOT is a known coastal front approximately 15 km from the lagoon in shallow water. [Prior optical observations at the tower](https://doi.org/10.1175/2008JTECHO619.1)

`aaot_masks.csv` examines red brightness and depth. Among cells with Rrs665 changing by ≤20% of its initial value, the 50 km median was −0.0462 (69 cells). It fell to −0.0121 among 226 near-constant-brightness cells within 100 km. The bottom depths of all wet cells contributing within 100 km were 1–44 m. No 50 m or 100 m deep cell center was available anywhere in that radius. Bathymetric depth alone cannot establish optical bottom contamination. For the bathymetry test each OLCI cell is assigned the closest ETOPO1 center. [NOAA bathymetry service](https://upwell.pfeg.noaa.gov/erddap/griddap/etopo180.html)

## Reproduction

Run with Python 3.10+, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib and the system `libblosc` library, with HTTPS access to Copernicus Marine, CloudFerro, NOAA and USGS. `OLCI_CACHE` can point to a directory for reusable compressed blocks. Scripts write tables alongside the scripts. The receipt CSVs give URLs, byte lengths and SHA-256s of the retrieved inputs. Quantities can change with upstream reprocessing. Blank or `nan` entries are missing data.

```sh
python olci.py
python check_footprints.py /path/to/footprints.csv
python check_stations.py
python aaot_sensitivity.py
python aaot_spectral_context.py
python plot_aaot.py
```
