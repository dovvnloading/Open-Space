# NEOWISE two-season difference photometry

No Phan track passed a repeated positive-flux test in the 2023–2024 W2 imaging. The W2 brightness limit is in Vega magnitudes. It applies to point sources with the adopted moving PSF. At W2 = 14.0 we recovered 240/256 injections from a separate, finer grid and at 15.0 we recovered 35/256. Each magnitude was tested at the same 256 positions. Twenty-four native single exposures in three visits enter this measurement. The dates, selected rows, image/mask URLs, masks and checksums are in the acquisition ledger. [IRSA image service](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/ibe/docs/wise/neowiser/p1bm_frm/) · [Instrument calibration and masks](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/WISE/docs/release/NEOWISE/expsup/sec4_2a.html)

## What was tested

The Phan search fixes both historical directions, varies their ranges and the IRAS effective date, and projects each solution at the midpoint of each W2 exposure. A geocentric observer approximates low Earth orbit here. Of 24,132 bound templates, 24,118 met the mask, cadence and subtraction requirements. Each native frame uses a PSF-sized Gaussian filter and a broader Gaussian background filter normalized by their unmasked support. The profile-fit bad-bit mask is 1504706175. Each visit compares the same sky coordinates in another visit with large predicted parallax displacement. A position needs at least two valid science frames and two reference frames. All usable visits must exceed 3 control-scatter units and the mean must exceed 5. A total of 415 out of 512 locally displaced control tracks passed the masks and none passed the threshold. Their local correlations preclude a survey-wide false-alarm probability.

Model planets have a sampled, normalized 6.4 arcsec FWHM Gaussian profile. The image zero point converts counts to W2 Vega magnitude. The script applies the same masks, filtering and interpolation to the native image-plane injection as to the image, then combines signal and background before the nonlinear visit median. It includes potential reference subtraction. It omits source photon noise, real PSF wings and changes to pipeline masks. Each fake is tested on the nearest search track in the joint visit coordinates. The 256 Phan injections draw from a finer grid with a different spacing. Physical parameters receive computational grid weights. The model does not weight by a planetary population or measured centroid likelihood. A grey reflected-light model with the optical grid's albedos predicts W2 roughly 17.3–22.3 Vega (solar absolute W2 near 3.27); this search is too shallow across that range. Methane-dependent atmospheric emission at 3–5 μm requires separate spectral models. [Solar WISE calibration](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/797/1/55) · [Fortney et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.07424)

For each ACT peak we search a ±60 arcsec square of common sky offsets from its published best-fitting motion on a 4 arcsec grid. Rounded velocities and distances can create errors outside that square. For each Chen source and the Rowan-Robinson source we select eight directions around a circular orbit at 650 or 225 au respectively and repeat that small-offset test. Those eight cutouts leave most of the continuum untested. Bounds for the wider prior grid are in the adjacent other directory. A missing or mask-ineligible field has no sensitivity estimate.

## Replay

From this directory, install NumPy, SciPy, Astropy and pyerfa. Run acquire.py with --cache /path/to/cache --download and --targets Phan, ACT or other, one selection per call. Next run repair.py --cache /path/to/cache followed by field names to retry failed cutouts using their public original images. Run search.py --cache /path/to/cache followed by the same names. Run pack.py to reproduce the compact ledgers. The per-field acquisition ledger fingerprints both the raw SIA response and input pixels, including the original transport checksum when a fallback was used. Filter programs, date limits, and eligibility are encoded in the scripts. Re-querying a live archive can change a result; compare the hashes. Compacted JSON lines contain a whole per-field ledger per line. Survey credit goes to NASA/JPL-Caltech and the NEOWISE team.

## W1 and additional candidates

The same 24 Phan exposures were searched independently at 3.4 µm using the W1 image and mask, 6.1″ model FWHM and the W1 image zero point. All 24,132 central-position templates passed the masks, and none met the repeated-signal threshold. In the independent 256-draw injections, 252 recovered at W1=15.0 Vega, 223 at 16.0, 100 at 16.5 and one at 17.0. W1 and W2 share acquisition dates. Their detection efficiencies have not been multiplied.

All 20 published ACT peaks have eligible pixels in the two-year W2 search. Across twenty 961-node trials, 9,411 offset templates passed the visit requirements (245 to 961 per field), and none triggered. The ±60″ range describes only a common sky offset along each published motion; the paper supplies no orbit covariance for these peaks.

For Chen 1, Chen 2 and Rowan-Robinson, all 792, 792 and 504 discrete circular hypotheses, respectively, were projected on the eight sampled image fields per target. After taking the union by physical orbit ID, 221, 219 and 29 distinct orbits were eligible and no orbit triggered. At W2=14 Vega, recovered counts were 206/792, 204/792 and 26/504. At W2=15 the counts were 0/792, 0/792 and 3/504. The eight directions with ±60″ offsets also explore positions between or outside the physical model nodes. In the westward Rowan-Robinson field two such offsets triggered. The exact physical grid and the additional offset search are recorded separately. Use w1.py /path/to/cache after the Phan W2 inputs. For the additional ACT and Rowan-Robinson cases, more_frames.py --cache /path/to/cache followed by ACT4_01, ACT4_04 and each RR field selects up to ten distinct scan IDs per season. The extended search retains seasons with five or more input exposures. The compact acquisition ledger records the exact selections. Run search.py a second time with --circular-grid for each of the 24 Chen and Rowan-Robinson fields before pack.py. Sampled directions and field acquisition coordinates are in other_fields.py.

Both Rowan-Robinson offset triggers fail native-image point-source vetting across all three visits. Neighboring bright stars include a Gaia long-period variable and a diffraction-contaminated WISE detection. A JPL check using WISE spacecraft states returned no known objects.
