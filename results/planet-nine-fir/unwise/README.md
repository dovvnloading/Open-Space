## unTimely catalogue screen of the Phan pair

**None of the 3,617 model trajectories matched a nonpersistent, quality-selected W1 or W2 source at two distinct coadd midpoints.** Across both bands, 61 model/epoch associations remained at one epoch apiece after rejecting detections that recurred at the same sky position. These associations involve 14 unique catalogue entries, so nearby model nodes often point to the same measurement. All 34 catalogues and the time index were checked against the recorded SHA-256 hashes. The W1/W2 catalogues contain 1,704,924 rows from 17 visits, with midpoints spanning 2010-06-28 through 2020-12-04. The code propagates the conditional Phan grid to each catalogue's MJDMEAN and checks membership against the 2048 × 2048 TAN tile WCS in its FITS primary header. Each trajectory falls in the tile at 16 or 17 visit midpoints. The tile index reports no zero-coverage pixels in the selected catalogues, with at least 10 input frames per tile pixel. These footprints do not establish source sensitivity at a specific location. [Epochs, geometry and per-visit counts](epochs.csv)

We select positive PSF fluxes at S/N ≥5, PSF-weighted good-pixel fraction ≥0.8, source flux fraction ≥0.5, zero `flags_unwise`, and at least three contributing frames (`nm`). We keep `flags_info` and fit diagnostics with each retained match. No `primary` cut is needed in a single tile. A source is classified as persistent when another entry in the **same band** occurs within 3″ at two or more other visits, using a looser S/N ≥3, good-pixel fraction ≥0.6 and at least two frames to find counterparts. Pointing and crowding can hide a real mover near a persistent source. The 32 translated controls offset all positions on each sampled trajectory together, using 16 azimuths on each of 60″ and 120″ circles. They preserve the time ordering and use the same quality, geometry and recurrence tests. The remaining either-band association rate for the unshifted trajectories is 0.104%. The control rates range from 0.047% to 0.185% with a median of 0.097%. The controls and nearby orbit nodes are correlated; these rates are an empirical comparison, not independent p-values. [Matching code](match.py)

MJDMEAN is the midpoint of the earliest and latest contributing exposure, **not** a measured source-specific flux-weighted epoch. The modeled sky displacement from MJDMIN to MJDMAX ranges from 22.2″ to 76.5″ across the grid. A relaxed check also matches against the trajectory throughout each tile's coadd interval, sampling the arc at roughly 1″ spacing with the same 3″ matching radius. It finds 495 either-band model/visit pairs and zero trajectories with at least three distinct matched visits. A moving source can smear across multiple WISE resolution elements and fail extraction from this coadd. The study has no measured moving-source completeness, limiting W1/W2 magnitude, or orbit exclusion fraction from unTimely. Its catalogue dates end in 2020. The separate later NEOWISE exposure work is needed for 2021–2024. [unTimely data and catalogue definitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14327)

From this directory, run `python catalogue.py /path/to/cache` to fetch the official index, tile listing and catalogues, then `python match.py /path/to/cache` to repeat the screen. The latter checks the receipts and uses the `orbits/` sibling. An independent re-download can compare the original committed receipts with newly returned data.
