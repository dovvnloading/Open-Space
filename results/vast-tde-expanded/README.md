## Primary provenance and executable proposal

The frozen CSV bytes are available at [author repository commit d8b7b571](https://github.com/dlangis/TDECat/commit/d8b7b571dec01b65f357db37c6d0ca68b852fb79), dated 2026-04-29; the scientific release is [Langis et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202554882). TDECat is a literature compilation, described by its authors as mostly optical; do not assume every row is independently optically selected or spectroscopically confirmed. The CSV omits these fields. Object-by-object classification, original references and separate honourable mentions need an audit. The date rule and all 134 dispositions remain unchanged.

AT2020nov is already a studied nuclear TDE candidate: [Earl et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12991) report optical double-peaked Balmer lines, a 300-day X-ray rebrightening and a roughly quiescent host, while its X-ray properties resemble AGN in some respects. This does not eliminate low-level AGN activity or establish the origin of any radio source. Its radio and VAST published-list history remain pending; lack of a found paper does not establish novelty.

VAST DR1 describes a 2.5 arcsec absolute astrometric term, added in quadrature to the catalog averaged positional error; it is a conservative crossmatch convention, not a guaranteed Gaussian 1σ. The 30 arcsec metadata query was only an inclusive neighborhood search. Adopt the paper convention and documented optical positional uncertainty in a prospectively reviewed association gate, preserving all alternatives and their provenance. The three tabulated nearest sources are <1 arcsec away; their second-nearest catalog sources are >139 arcsec away. Catalog isolation does not rule out an uncataloged contaminant.

`epoch-with-sbid.csv` adds image pointings and scheduling-block IDs from the 936444-byte official image table (SHA-256 70ce692b3c8d067d53e248b6265420a26880147bd7bc15327930880bd8395195); no flux values are in this table. A proposal groups images linked by successive gaps of at most 24 hours into one session, requires distinct IDs, and reserves the last two sessions. For 2020nov, the proposed heldout sessions contain IDs (9841,9844) and (10114,10117); both members of each pair are same-session, not independent heldouts. For the two known controls, proposed sessions are (9767), (10040) and (9870), (10143), respectively. `proposal-synthetic.py` demonstrates grouping and a constant-baseline counterfactual only on artificial data; it does not fix a significance threshold or handle calibration covariance. Local-controls calibration, covariance, flux eligibility, nondetection handling, physical classification and the published 126/170 crossmatch remain required before root review and any flux access.

## Pre-flux review packet

The proposal operates on all 103 date-eligible parent rows; only the three metadata associations have a catalog light curve. The other 100 remain no-match/unknown coverage, and the 31 date exclusions remain visible. AT2018hyz and ASASSN-15oi are known radio-positive controls. AT2020nov is a known TDE candidate with a prior radio source, not an unreported radio discovery: [Earl et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12991) cite VLA program 20A-372 detections 111, 228, 246 and 869 days after discovery, interpreted by Cendes et al. as likely host star formation. Those cited epochs precede the 2023 VAST sequence; this literature check did not inspect VAST flux values. Source separation or a radio rise alone cannot prove association with the TDE.

The VAST paper's Table 2 contains 126 names on PDF pages 14–16. The extracted names and coordinates in `vast-table2-names.txt` were crossmatched using only names/positions: no one of the three source positions is near the 126 published sources. The additional 44 rejected members of the 170 candidate set are not identified in that table. Its full-sample eta/V selection uses all epochs and remains unread, since it would leak holdout information. This check does not establish priority. The primary PDF SHA-256 is `da0d9a2b0e3add16b79c46ed023fddf6b6a3993e20c4c2cfbd1b94fb7963c92e`.

`proposed-packet.json` records metadata hashes, image/SBID splits and a sealed holdout flag; `packet_guard.py` verifies the packet and uses synthetic data only. `control-metadata.csv` records up to 200 nearest sources within 1 degree, more than 60 arcsec from any parent position and more than 30 arcsec from the 126 published variable positions, with at least four common image IDs. The resulting counts are 145, 172 and 200. The full-image membership used for this initial availability cut is metadata only. Stable controls must be selected on development flux alone, not from all-epoch eta/V or heldout values.

The proposed one-candidate screen is a constant, per-session early baseline; the two latest distinct sessions are reserved. A candidate crosses a conditional rise screen only if both heldout session values exceed the frozen baseline by at least three estimated standard errors. The two known controls are diagnostics, not additional discovery trials. With calibrated Gaussian marginal errors, a union bound gives a one-sided null probability at most 0.00135 for the one candidate even with correlated sessions; this is conditional and not a calibrated astrophysical significance. `packet_guard.py` simulates an idealized independent-Gaussian null and a +4σ injection, while `development_prototype.py` tests holdout-ID rejection and the constant-fit/gate calculations. The toy does not model forced-flux bias, correlated field calibration, beam changes, shared baseline covariance across all sources, stellar/AGN variability or finite-sample control selection.

Before source flux access the operator must review and freeze a complete calibration and execution implementation: define per-image valid flags and PSF/beam cuts; combine same-session overlapping fields without treating them as independent; select at least 20 stable controls with adequate development-only measurements and quantify a field calibration floor; propagate shared session and baseline covariance; preserve negative forced measurements and missingness; verify an image-level baseline and confusion; and predeclare failure handling and sensitivity. Current control metadata are not measurements, and the toy prototype does not implement or validate these requirements. No real source or control flux has been read.
