# Expanded optical TDE parent — metadata only

This revises the 30-event pilot in `../vast-tde-metadata/`; it does not replace or revise the original selection. Before reading radio fluxes, the author-published [TDECat CSV](https://github.com/dlangis/TDECat) was selected as an independently compiled optical TDE parent and a discovery date strictly before 2023-06-01 was specified. The 134 original rows and aliases are retained unchanged, 103 meet that date, and 31 later-discovered rows remain in the output as exclusions. The source authors document separate uncertain candidates in `honourable_mentions.pdf`; its exact original bytes are preserved at the D: campaign root, SHA-256 f79dc9823fcbf596de7d5756d828125eb1fc61add844cb16e6670779fa5a26b1. Those additional candidates are not silently treated as confirmed and need a separately frozen analysis if included. The parent CSV SHA-256 is cfda3e614c8f950daf693d469122a9019e5b41add186e69dbbc16d768e718a4a; it is distributed by its authors under CC BY-NC 4.0. TDECat is an incomplete compilation; the catalog CSV does not itself provide per-object spectral classifications, which must be verified in primary references before science testing.

A metadata-only nearest-source search against VAST DR1 source positions, with 30 arcsec used only for inclusive triage, returned 3 possible matches among the 103 and 100 without a catalog source. Neither 30 arcsec nor a catalog association establishes ownership. The match table retains nearest and second-nearest distances and catalog position errors; missing catalog matches do not mean a radio nondetection or confirm footprint coverage. No coordinates among the 134 parent rows lie within 1 arcsec of each other, but alias/host deduplication still needs literature review. All 134 rows remain in `expanded-metadata.csv`.

- AT2020nov (ZTF20abisysx), candidate VAST source 14043503, separation 0.724 arcsec, 22 image rows of which 14 are forced. The scientific status and radio history remain unverified. An empty search result is not novelty evidence.
- AT2018hyz, VAST 13894502, 0.905 arcsec, 11 image rows; a known radio-bright control in [RACS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12097) and [earlier follow-up](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10435685).
- ASASSN-15oi, VAST 14481388, 0.390 arcsec, 11 image rows; a known delayed-radio control ([published monitoring](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-021-01300-8)).

For AT2020nov the final two individual image IDs 10114 and 10117 are only about 14 minutes apart on 2025-05-12; they are not two independent visit epochs. The preceding March pair is also separated by about 15 minutes. Specify observation-session grouping and any held-out epochs before viewing flux, and account for overlaps and forced measurements. Image IDs and times for all three associations are in `epoch-metadata.csv`. Only source ID, source position/error and count metadata, plus measurement source, image ID, time and forced flags, were accessed. No radio flux, cutout image pixels or held-out measurements have been examined.

The original ASKAP paper's 170-candidate gate and 126 real-source list need direct crossmatch before assigning discovery status; its supplemental DOI currently redirects to the article instead of the machine table. For off-catalog positions, confirm image-footprint coverage, beam, noise and forced-photometry availability from metadata before proposing image retrieval. The 2024 RACS study and other prior radio TDE work require an object-by-object audit. Before any flux access, freeze a protocol with source matching and astrometric verification, session grouping, local calibration controls, sky/background and imaging checks, source-history and AGN/scintillation alternatives, sensitivity and multiplicity. Do not choose numerical gates after inspecting source outcomes.
