# Preflare fading in the AT 2023ugy host

The host was already brighter in 2014–2015 than in 2021–2022, well before the reported 2023 tidal disruption event. Median NEOWISE W1 dimmed by 0.328 mag, W2 by 0.437 mag. Gaia G independently dimmed by about 0.20 mag across these windows. The source was selected after comparing preflare light curves. This is a measurement of the unresolved host. Its physical cause remains open. Epochs, replay, literature checks and controls are being assembled.
