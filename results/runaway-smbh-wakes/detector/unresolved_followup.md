# Unresolved Euclid VIS segments: tests needed to decide them

These are the **32 `weak or confused` entries** of the 68 individually inspected positive segments in [`review_rows.csv`](review_rows.csv). They are **not discoveries**, physical-class identifications, or an upper limit on wake incidence. Several may be parts of the same sky structure. The proposed observations are not yet completed. A result consistent with a wake would still require an identified host, a common redshift and exclusion of stars, chance alignments, disks, streams and jets.
