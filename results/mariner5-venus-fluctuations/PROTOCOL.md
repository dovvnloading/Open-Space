# Recovering the two Venus bow-shock crossings from Mariner 5 frame statistics

Frozen 18 November 2026 on main aa234103 before reading the restored magnetic field columns. The PDS released the fine-time-scale archive on 2 October 2026. The original mission report assigns the inbound and outbound events approximately −156 and +18 minutes from closest approach at 17:34:55.3 UT. The inbound marker was supported by a large field-strength increase and a density change. The outbound marker was supported by reduced magnetic fluctuations and recovery in plasma velocity. Published plasma measurements use a roughly five-minute sampling cycle, and the event labels reflect both instruments. They provide approximate reference times. [PDS announcement](https://pds.nasa.gov/datasearch/subscription-service/SS-20261031.shtml) · [NASA SP-190, Figure 2-27 and text](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19720013159/downloads/19720013159.pdf) · [Bridge et al. 1967](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.158.3809.1669)

## Test

- The observing unit is an archived MAG frame. Use the native encounter time from the HOUR column and compare the added UTC column to it at recorded precision. Exclude any nonfinite row or negative magnitude or standard deviation. Report duplicates, gaps and alternate time support. The encounter frames have three unevenly spaced vector samples in the restored product and a nominal 50.4 s duration. The first paper describes five samples per 50.4 s at Venus. Keep that discrepancy visible and avoid assigning subframe timing or identifying a wave frequency.
- Compare field magnitude B with the square root of the sum of squared component deviations Sx, Sy and Sz as a rotation-invariant measure of within-frame vector variability. Treat SB, the standard deviation of the magnitude, as a separate diagnostic. For a split between adjacent valid frames with no more than 80 s between frame times, score the difference between before and after medians. Each side uses frames between 2 and 8 minutes from the split and requires at least five. The magnitude score is the absolute log ratio of the two positive medians. The variability scores use the absolute log ratio after adding a fixed 0.2 nT floor to both medians, roughly the documented digitization uncertainty. Retain the signed change and medians.
- For each reference time, search all splits within ±10 minutes using one maximum per channel and retain ties with the earliest split. A match requires the maximum within ±5 minutes and the expected signed change. Require a magnitude increase at ingress and a drop in vector variability at egress. Report the magnitude maximum at egress using the same time and control thresholds in either direction. Also score windows fixed at the exact approximate reference with no optimization. Report all channels and explicit sample counts.
- Apply the same ±10 min scan to 03:00, 09:00, 15:00 and 21:00 UTC daily on October 1–15 and October 22–November 5, 1967. These 120 cruise blocks are defined by dates outside encounter before inspecting any values. Use the 50.4 s rate with three usable samples per frame and documented bad-frame flags excluded. Require zero IJ, IR, MF6, MF11 and MF12, and ordinary MF10=0. The positive control also permits the roll flag MF10=3. Exclude the representable endpoints and nonfinite vector components. Average the three raw magnitudes and derive the square root of the sum of component population variances. Verify and report flag handling from the product guide. Compare peak scores against equally searched controls. A channel counts as unusually strong if its maximum exceeds the 95th percentile of eligible control maxima and at least 60 control blocks are eligible. Report empirical ranks and calendar-day counts without treating blocks as independent trials. Apply same-frame gap and coverage requirements. Report the effect of multiplying encounter dispersions by sqrt(2/3), which would convert a sample standard deviation for three readings to a population standard deviation.
- Repeat the local encounter score with 3–12 minute side windows (at least eight frames), and with a one-frame deletion of each influential before/after frame. For deletion, hold each candidate split fixed even when deleting a bracketing frame and count cases whose strength and direction criteria survive. Report if detection depends on a window choice. Same-day comparison anchors every 24 minutes beginning E−264 through E+48, excluding any whose ±10 min search plus 8-minute window touches the ±10 min interval of either reference. Report this separate comparison without conflating the sheathed region and independent cruise.
- June 14 Earth bow shock near 12:23 UT is a separate check of a known large change, if the raw cruise record covers it. Score 2–8 minute windows with at least 20 high-rate frames per side and at most 25 s between bracketing frames. Retain spacecraft rolls for this check and use raw magnitude and variability only. The geometry, mode and orbit differ and this positive control receives no Venus ranking. Never call an unrelated cruise peak a planetary shock.
