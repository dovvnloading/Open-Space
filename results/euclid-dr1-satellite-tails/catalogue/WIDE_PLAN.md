# Blind search beyond the four named dwarfs

Secondary analysis fixed after the four anchored-field tests and before inspecting other positions in the parent tiles. Use all four parent catalogues. Exclude the region within six published half-light radii of the targeted dwarf. Search on a 12 arcsec grid at position angles spaced by 22.5°. In each 80 × 20 arcsec rectangle count stellar-colour tracers from the same filter used in the anchored catalogue analysis. Use equally long flanking regions on each side, separated by a 10 arcsec gap. Compare the fraction of tracers among quality VIS detections inside and in the flanks to mitigate spatially varying depth. Require support in both longitudinal halves, representation on both sides and in each flank, and a complete geometric aperture within the tile. Randomly assign catalogue rows to two disjoint halves before scanning. Select up to three separated positions per field using the discovery half, evaluating the same frozen positions and orientations in the other half. Use a one-sided Fisher exact test of tracer enrichment in that half with Bonferroni correction for twelve possible leads. Pixel dependence, survey selection and foreground variation are limitations of the test. A catalogue excess requires confirmation with imaging and literature checks. Splitting catalogue rows does not produce an independent telescope exposure.
