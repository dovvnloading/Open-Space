# Outer debris in four Euclid DR1 dwarf fields

Frozen on 2026-11-16 before catalogue or pixel screening. The unit of study is the sky outside each published dwarf core. This is a targeted pilot with four fields, selected because Gatto et al. explicitly discuss an individual nearby galaxy in projection. It has no population-wide denominator. Table 2 supplies the centre, semi-major half-light radius, ellipticity and position angle. Table 1 supplies the adopted distance. Table 3 and section 5 discuss association uncertainties. Both northern fields are included regardless of host membership. The published dwarf itself, its ellipticity, and its projected host relationship are prior knowledge. [Gatto et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16025)

Search elongated diffuse emission outside two published half-light radii. Separately search the catalogue of point-like, stellar-colour sources in the elliptical annulus from two to six half-light semi-major axes. In the catalogue test, compare opposing sectors with random orientations of individual stars under a smooth elliptical dwarf plus uniform contamination model. Repeat the scan over position angle in every null realization, correct across four fields, and examine the field in translated control apertures. Use the probability of the weaker of two arms via the smaller sector count. A one-sided excess cannot by itself establish paired tails. Record objects passing photometric cuts and all trials, including failures. Image injections and catalogue-level additions probe different stages of selection.

For any feature passing the search, assess independent bands, available exposures and ground-based images at its frozen position. Test masks, PSF wings, cirrus, catalogue counterparts, smooth bound light and foreground/background alignments before assigning tidal origin. A nondetection gives no population-wide limit. Existing sky-subtracted stacks may attenuate diffuse light that was present before processing.
