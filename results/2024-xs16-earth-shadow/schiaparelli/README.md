## Schiaparelli saw 2024 XS16 less than three minutes before modeled first contact

The ESA NEO Coordination Centre publishes full-size display images and calibrated FITS headers for six 1-second exposures from Schiaparelli Observatory (MPC 204). The headers identify CCGRUC2, the original discovery designation of 2024 XS16. The exposures started at 18:24:02.2 through 18:24:27.7 UTC on 11 December 2024. The final shutter closure at 18:24:28.7 precedes modeled first contact at 18:27:24.7 by 176 seconds. The final exposure midpoint is about 59 seconds later than station 204's last listed MPC position. Individual frames may contribute to the observatory's astrometry; this is an independent instrument from L34.

We projected the JPL Horizons topocentric ICRF coordinates (observing code 204) onto each TAN–SIP solution at mid-exposure. JPL positions were interpolated between 18:24 and 18:25 UTC. The location moves by about 73 pixels over the six images. A three-pixel-radius Gaussian-weighted aperture at each fixed position (sigma 1.5 pixels), with a local median in a 7–12 pixel annulus, captured positive signal in all six exposures. In 8-bit display levels the mean excess was **77.0**. The largest of 296 nearby tracks translated by 24–100 pixels was **23.0**. These correlated control tracks measure local image contrast. The public render includes image stretching and clipping, precluding calibrated flux estimates. It establishes the pre-eclipse presence of the moving target.

A date-only search returned 481 station-204 archive records in 17:30–20:00 UTC. The 18:24:00–18:30:00 window returned only the six target exposures. A search from 18:30:00 through 18:40:41 returned zero. The file at 18:40:43 was aimed at A11fGbM (declination +62.5309°), and the file at 19:46:14 at A11fFZo (+11.4514°). These pointings supply no brightness limits for 2024 XS16. The calibrated FITS download on ESA's site requires a registered session.

Run `python check_station204.py` here with NumPy, SciPy and Pillow to obtain the six ESA display JPEGs by their original links, verify each SHA-256 and repeat the fixed-position measurements. The full Horizons response, FITS WCS terms and per-frame timestamps are included. The ESA archive search form exposes the raw header for each filename in the manifest. The WCS metadata are sufficient to reproduce the projection. The original display files are referenced by URL and checksum.
