# Five additional large-source fits

**The ρ = 15 solution for KMT-2024-BLG-1762 requires more source light than the measured flux calibration supports.** KMT-2025-BLG-1797 has a genuine independent OGLE brightening, but the OGLE data disfavor the fitted KMT profile. KMT-2025-BLG-2489 is a localized, nonrepeating two-color brightening whose modeled source egress fell after the observing season. KMT-2024-BLG-1152 has full coverage at two sites and is significant in I, but its expanded binary trial fits better. KMT-2024-BLG-3413 has weak positional recovery and stronger signals on neighboring stars. None provides a measured angular Einstein radius or a confirmed unbound lens.

## The DIAPL light scale for 1762

For a uniform source transited at its center the maximum excess magnification is √(1 + 4/ρ²) − 1. At ρ = 15 it is 0.008850. The 2024 nominal profile has peak differential flux of 674.72 counts in Chile and 650.94 in South Africa, implying at least 76,242 and 73,555 source counts respectively. The third site has insufficient signal to test this requirement.

We measured the DIAPL count scale using 2024 events KMT-2412, 0273, 1694, 1096 and 3131 on the same BLG31M CCD. KMT-2412 is on the same BLG31M0105 subfield. We matched each released pySIS I measurement to positional DIAPL within 2.6 minutes, took night medians, and compared on-event and off-event flux contrasts. The ratio of ΔDIAPL to Δ[10⁻⁰·⁴⁽ᴵ⁻²⁰⁾] transfers a calibrated I = 20 flux into DIAPL counts. We bootstrapped the matched nights in each group together. We retained scale estimates only when both contrasts were positive in >95% of bootstrap resamples.

The same-subfield event gives 3,349 counts at I = 20 in Chile and 1,370 in South Africa. Taking the **largest** 84th-percentile estimate across the five same-chip calibrators gives 3,699 and 5,981. Even at those generous scales, the proposed source requires **20.6 and 12.3 times** the catalog's total I = 20.00 light. The alternative catalog I = 20.65 makes the mismatch larger. Different locations and blending introduce variation in the transfer, which is traceable across calibrators in the saved table. [Scale-transfer measurements and source requirements](diapl-scale.json)

Refitting the 2024 positional data alone, after binning into 234 site/nights and using independently fitted site baselines, yields point-lens fits with tE around one month. The PSPL costs less than 2 in χ² relative to the best tested FSPL after limiting each site's source flux to the generous I = 20 estimate, and also when that limit is doubled. Changing the grouping, empirical scatter and bound changes the numerical χ² relative to the all-year event-file analysis. This comparison establishes a viable ordinary 2024 brightening and illustrates why the shape-only ρ = 15 fit should not be interpreted as a radius measurement.
