# No validated new occulting body in 42 bright TESS white dwarfs

At the 1.40794-day period of WD 1856+534 b, the blind search returned no period-compatible trigger among the **39 white dwarfs in the frozen catalogue extension**. It recovered **1,896 of 1,950** randomly phased transiting injections of radius 11.2 Earth radii into their measured 200-second aperture fluxes. The pre-specified, pointwise **95% upper bound on the fraction with a transiting companion is 11.13%** for those stars and that model. It gives no upper bound below 100% on the host fraction for isotropic orbits. [Frozen extension](EXTENSION.md) · [Trial ledger](extension/extension_recovery_trials.csv.gz) · [Limits](extension/extension_limits.json)

For a secondary giant-companion model with periods distributed uniformly in log period from 0.25 to 3 days, the three independent Sector 109 stars bring the total to 42. The survey recovered 2,035 of 2,100 injections. Counting both stars with compatible triggers as possible hosts yields a 20.13% transiting-host ceiling. The geometry-weighted sensitivity is only 1.68 effective stars. Neither a general planet-host limit nor a debris occurrence rate follows from this experiment. [Population model](PERIOD_POPULATION.md) · [Results and possible hosts](extension/population_limit.json)

The [independent referee replay](REFEREE_REVIEW.md) verifies these pointwise bounds, the input hashes, sample decisions, positive controls and optical ledgers. A periodic-orbit limit **conditional on transit** does not constrain the fraction of systems with randomly inclined, non-transiting planets or the occurrence rate of irregular debris.

## Limits and pending checks

- The 11.13% bound applies to a circular, opaque companion of the stated radius and period among the 39 bright, crowding-qualified extension stars. The hydrogen-atmosphere masses and radii, nominal Gaia/TESS flux ratios, and random orientation **conditional on transit** enter the recovery model. The four pointwise experiments do not jointly constrain a continuum of sizes or periods. [Four-cell recovery table and statistical methods](EXTENSION.md)
- The 0.08–7-day FFI detector uses 2–120-minute windows and seeds its period search from individual dips. A calibrated rate for faint or crowded targets, low-amplitude signals detectable only by stacking, or time-variable debris would require a separate selection and recovery experiment. The Sector 110 trapezoid injections measure sensitivity in one visit. [Search protocol](PROTOCOL.md) · [Single-dip recovery](sector110/tic55997020/single_dip_recovery.json)
- No simultaneous ground-based exposure verifies the quality-excluded FFI events. Sparse historical folds have uncertain phase after years of extrapolation. The two compatible stars remain counted in the continuous-period upper bound. The three-star Sector 109 cohort includes a known extremely low-mass binary with uncertain catalogue parameters. Removing it weakens that cohort’s fixed-period transiting-giant bound to 88.8%. [Binary-excluded calculation](sector109/ffi_binary_excluded.json)
