# Scope and measurement decisions

The all-sky inventory starts with every Gentile Fusillo EDR3 main-catalogue entry satisfying P_WD > 0.75 and G < 14. The inferential cohort is the subset landing on a Sector 109 science detector with a unique TIC identity, measurable photometry and a modelled target fraction of at least 0.80 throughout the specified crowding checks. Sector 109 is the most recent complete sector with working TESScut cutouts in this acquisition. Controls are separate. The parent roster and field selection were chosen before searching their fluxes.

The independent unit is the star. Four pointwise experiments use companion radii of 1 and 11.2 Earth radii crossed with periods of 0.25 and 1.40794 days. Each trial draws orbital phase uniformly over the period and inclination conditional on transiting. The search runs blind to injected periods. Count phase- and period-matched recoveries, preserve gaps and dilution, and use per-star detection probabilities. The comparison to the unmodified curves must count every compatible trigger whose origin remains uncertain. Isolated dips do not yield a debris population limit. Absolute occurrence also depends on geometric transit probability. These fixed circles in radius and period do not represent a period distribution.

The supplemental search takes all released 20- and 120-second target-pixel visits for the Sector 109 crowding-qualified stars, and checks released Cycle 5 onward FFI coverage for those same stars. Supplemental products and positive controls are excluded from the Sector 109 occurrence calculation. Select the brightest Sector 109 qualified target also present in the partially released Sector 110 for a direct full-frame aperture check on its latest listed exposures. Report its window and usable timestamps separately.

## Catalogue extension

Before acquiring or opening the remaining fluxes, extend the four calibrated experiments to the all-sky parent outside all nine Sector 109 geometric targets. Require a unique TIC ID match with a consistent epoch-corrected position, positive finite catalogue mass and gravity, and the same per-visit extraction and crowding cuts. Select one latest geometric complete sector in Sectors 55–108 for each star and record acquisition failures. The CCD, aperture and flux may differ between visits. Apply the same detection and trial-matching rules. Report the extension separately and retain compatible triggers in the occurrence numerator. Catalogue hydrogen-atmosphere fits are a modelling assumption, particularly in binary white dwarfs.
