# Two candidate multi-apparition identifications

A common six-component gravitational orbit connects 2007 VE138 to 2026 VV15, and a second connects 2017 RB15 to 2026 RG39. These are candidate identifications of already discovered objects. The MPC Designation Identifier returned distinct primary designations on 2026-11-15. The discovering surveys retain credit for the detections.

| Designations | Positions | Stations | All-position radial RMS | Held-out stations | Held-out positions | Held-out radial RMS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 2007 VE138 and 2026 VV15 | 110 | 25 | 1.142 arcsec | 595, J95 | 14 | 1.146 arcsec |
| 2017 RB15 and 2026 RG39 | 62 | 13 | 0.926 arcsec | F51, I52 | 20 | 1.984 arcsec |

The measured right ascensions and declinations come from the MPC ADES archive. Each site holdout uses an independent refit from a catalog seed. Both discovery arcs enter the full-orbit fit. The held-out measurements test a solution fitted to the other stations, conditional on the pair having been selected from catalog orbits. The authoritative catalog association remains with the MPC.
