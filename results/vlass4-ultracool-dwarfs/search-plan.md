# Fourth-epoch radio search for ultracool dwarfs

Starting point: Open-Space main at 0ec1fdb32cfbdecf216a5168e9e4290aadffeabc. The open-PR listing was empty during the initial audit. The results directory and branch names were screened, and the separately reported helium-accretor and hierarchical-multiple work was excluded.

Question: Does VLASS epoch 4 contain a compact radio detection at the epoch-propagated position of a spectroscopically classified ultracool dwarf, including objects without a published radio counterpart? Search the released UltracoolSheet positions against the available epoch-4 sky coverage. Resolve close components as one association when the radio beam does not distinguish them. Inspect the image and neighboring sources, quantify the shifted-position background, compare historical epochs, and search for prior reports before advancing a candidate. Catalog position alone is insufficient to establish a physical counterpart.

Input releases: [UltracoolSheet 2.1.0](https://zenodo.org/records/15802304), [NRAO VLASS](https://science.nrao.edu/vlass/). Earlier all-sky radio work: [Radio emission from flaring stars and brown dwarfs](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202555032).
