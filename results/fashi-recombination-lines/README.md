# Four Galactic nebulae in the FASHI extragalactic H I catalogs

Four consecutive hydrogen recombination lines from the Galactic H II region Sh 2-235 appear as four separate H I sources in the published FASHI DR2 catalog. The entries span 56.2 to 298.1 Mpc under the catalog's 21-cm interpretation. Interpreting their frequencies as H167α, H168α, H169α and H170α puts them within 2.3 km/s of the independent H91α/H92α velocity for Sh 2-235. Their largest pairwise separation is 88.2 arcsec, approximately half a FAST beam. The inference concerns the source identifications in the releases. The nebula and its recombination emission are already known.

The same search identifies a three-transition system in Sh 2-156 (IC 1470) in DR1 and two-transition systems in Sh 2-206 and Sh 2-297 in DR2. DR2 retains one of the three IC 1470 lines. DR1 and DR2 overlap observationally.

Primary data: [FASHI DR1](https://nadc.china-vo.org/res/r100875/?lang=en), [FASHI DR2](https://nadc.china-vo.org/res/r103421/?lang=en), and the [WISE Catalog of Galactic H II Regions](https://astro.phys.wvu.edu/wise/).
