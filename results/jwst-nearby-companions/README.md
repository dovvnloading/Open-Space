# Public JWST companion search around nearby stars

**No uncatalogued planet or brown-dwarf companion passed validation in the ten stellar fields searched.** The three field sources highlighted below each have an independently identified archival counterpart. Nine additional white-dwarf-field peaks matched stationary positions between JWST epochs. Two additional NIRCam peaks around Ross 619 fell below threshold when we changed the reference stars. The sensitivity measurements apply to the footprints, filters, separations and recovery rules below.

## Referee audit and replay boundary (2026-11-17)

An independent, standard-library-only [referee test suite](../../tests/test_jwst_nearby_referee.py) recomputes the published numerical statements from **frozen intermediate CSV and JSON outputs**, rather than trusting the prose. Run python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_jwst_nearby_referee.py' -v from the repository root; the same tests run in CI. They check 9,056 exposure filesets, 2,653 visit/filter groups, 240 programs, 331 candidate hosts and 214 title-screen leads; all 14 Pan-STARRS detections (nine nights), yielding median 0.252157″ fixed-sky and 11.133507″ host-following residuals for L 788−34; G29−38 Spitzer 4.5 µm centroids at 0.069706″ and 0.055268″ from fixed sky versus 10.003″ and 7.834″ for a host-following location; GJ 205's 0.051029″ Gaia relative-position residual; both reference-dependent Ross 619 peaks; and 4+5 stationary matches plus seven unmatched peaks in the white-dwarf controls. They recompute **all 30 NIRCam 95%-recovery levels** from 11,880 injections and all 12 GJ 205 MIRI 95%-recovery levels from 6,336 injections, plus the G29−38, Ross 154 and white-dwarf thresholds from the trial tables.

**Replay boundary.** The FITS pixel arrays and the externally hosted MAST/Spitzer bytes are not committed. The offline tests check receipt page counts and predicates, SHA-256 syntax, public-rights flags *where supplied*, fixed manifests and internal arithmetic. They do not independently prove that URLs still serve identical bytes or that the original image fits are free of bias. The deposited science scripts verify byte hashes when downloading; a clean-machine pixel-level re-execution remains a distinct check. MAST response SHA-256s are *historical* query receipts, not evidence that subsequent live search results have identical bytes. A blank rights flag on an off-axis calibration entry is missing metadata, not a claim that the calibration file is public.

## Remaining scope and limits

This is a selected ten-field experiment. The full inventory includes science targets, references, calibrations and archive associations. The nearby sample can miss stars with incomplete Gaia astrometry, short observations outside the supplementary programs, proposer misclassifications and serendipitous stellar fields. Program-level publication links do not establish whether a specific field was searched. Individual legacy searches and image coverage for other hosts require review.

Completeness depends on band, field, angular separation, stellar-flux normalization, and which azimuths were observable. NIRCam injections measure the blind detection stage before independent-library vetting. The two-epoch MIRI white-dwarf trials include the motion criterion. The seven unmatched white-dwarf peaks and the two reference-dependent Ross 619 peaks have no validated astrophysical classification. No survey-wide upper bound on planet occurrence, universal mass limit, or bound companion follows from these measurements.
