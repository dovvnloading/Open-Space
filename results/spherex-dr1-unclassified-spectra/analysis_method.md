# Residual ranking and sensitivity

The primary statistic measures how strongly a localized feature departs from a fitted spectrum. Its scale is heuristic. The second ranking uses distances to other sources in the same field and uses no class labels. Neither ranking establishes the nature of a source.

## Eligibility and comparisons

Each spectrum retains finite strict-mask fluxes with positive empirical blank-aperture scatter and center-pixel NHITS greater than zero. The two blank mission channels (51 and 52, one-based) are excluded. The primary screen requires at least 35 channels across at least three detectors and flux at least three times the blank scatter in at least eight channels. A separately reported NEP screen requires at least 15 measured channels in total, both D2 and D5, and the same eight-channel source-detection cut. The local search requires enough contiguous same-detector sidebands to define at least one filter. No channels are filled.

1. **Templates.** The model bank includes solar-metallicity Castelli–Kurucz atmospheres at nine temperatures (3500–12,000 K), log g values of 2.5 and 4.5 where available, and four foreground extinction values. Where a Gaia temperature exists the fit admits the two nearest tabulated temperatures. The 25 Polletta SWIRE galaxy, starburst and AGN templates use the catalog redshift when available, otherwise zero. Four warm blackbody continua are included for dusty sources. Input flux densities are converted from relative Fλ to relative Fν. The fitter averages each published spectral template over 11 samples inside each recorded wavelength interval under a top-hat response. The extinction grid and warm-dust continua are evaluated at channel center.
2. **Continuum and features.** A positive amplitude and two iteratively downweighted updates fit each candidate continuum. The winning model minimizes summed Huber loss at transition 2.5. A nonnegative least-squares fit then adjusts signed profiles for H recombination, PAH, H₂O vapor and ice, CO gas, ice and overtone, CO₂, CH₄, NH₃ and CH₃OH at that model's redshift. Table entries in the script give the approximate positions and widths. The effective per-channel scale is the quadrature sum of the empirical blank scatter and 2.5% of a five-sample median-smoothed source flux. These profiles model likely alternatives without establishing molecular identification. Gravity/metallicity coverage, temperature coverage, YSO geometry, time variation, line ratios, blends and bandpass variation remain limited.
3. **Peak ranking.** At every testable center fit Gaussian profiles of sampled FWHM one and three local channel spacings. Project a weighted linear slope and intercept out of each filter before scoring it. The respective windows require complete sidebands extending four and six channels on both sides inside a single detector. Take the source's largest absolute score, retaining the sign, exact channel and width. The separate median absolute residual describes broad model mismatch across all retained channels. The peak statistic does not search detector ends, gaps or arbitrarily broad features. Correlated channels and extraction systematics preclude a Gaussian false-alarm interpretation of a score of five.
4. **Independent shape ranking.** Normalize each source by its median positive flux, apply an asinh transform with softening 0.1 after normalization, and compare absolute differences on jointly measured channels. The distance averages the median and 90th-percentile difference. Average the distances to the three nearest *other* sources. Require at least 35 jointly measured channels for each primary comparison, or 15 in the two-detector comparison. Missing-channel patterns and flux-dependent noise can influence this metric.
5. **Fixed-channel check.** For each held-out source and filter center/width, use the other sources' median and scatter (1.4826 times the median absolute deviation, floored at one). Report original and strongest adjusted residuals with at least ten other testable sources. Count same-sign residuals crossing three at the original center and width. Shared stellar features, channel calibration and local sampling can all contribute to recurrence.
6. **Injection and recovery.** Add sampled Gaussians before template selection to each real eligible spectrum. Choose up to two valid distinct centers per detector and width for each source using a fixed seed. Reuse each center for positive and negative features with peak amplitudes 0.10, 0.25, 0.50 and 1.00 times the fitted continuum. Hold the measured channel mask and estimated noise scale fixed, refit all continuum and nuisance-feature parameters, and scan all filters. A recovery requires the strongest residual to pass five, have the correct sign and localize within one channel on the same detector. A location already meeting the criterion in the uninjected spectrum counts as a failure, preventing credit for coincident shams. Repeated signs and amplitudes share source spectra and placements. Recovery fractions measure the spectral screen at eligible detector interiors, conditional on this sample. They do not include catalog selection, pixel extraction losses, visit confirmation, or the broad shape ranking.

## Replay

From this result directory, regenerate templates with `python prepare_templates.py` if the compiled grid is absent. This checks the raw inputs against `templates/sources.json`. The following commands use Python with NumPy, pandas and SciPy. Template regeneration additionally uses Astropy.

```sh
export OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1
python analyze.py --out nep_full
python analyze.py --two-band --out nep_two_band
python analyze.py --input wide/spectra.csv --redshifts wide/catalogs/model_redshifts.csv --out wide/full
python channel_check.py nep_two_band
python channel_check.py wide/full
python redshift_check.py wide
```

The per-run JSON records inputs, hashes, seed and counts. The CSVs retain source eligibility, both ranks, per-channel fits, all tested centers, sham and injected trials, recovery by width/sign/detector, and held-out field comparisons. A missing same-width redshift transfer means the predicted channel lacked a testable filter with the required sidebands.
