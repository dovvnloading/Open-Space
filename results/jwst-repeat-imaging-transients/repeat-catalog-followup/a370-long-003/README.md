# Abell 370 trigger a370_long-003 is a known BAL quasar

The position (39.96591521°, −1.59954268°) falls **0.079 arcsec from L1, the dusty broad-absorption-line quasar in SMM J02399−0136**. The ALMA continuum coordinates are 02:39:51.815, −01:35:58.39 (Table 1 of Vidal-García et al. 2021). The independent Pilot-WINGS MUSE spectrum of source 605 is 0.073 arcsec away and gives **z = 2.79800, quality 3**. This component lies in the known gravitationally lensed galaxy group at systemic z ≈ 2.8. The search therefore excludes this trigger from new-supernova, TDE and lensed-star candidates. Compact nuclear variability is consistent with the known AGN. These data do not establish the physical origin of every flux change. [ALMA position and spectroscopic classification](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stab1503) · [MUSE Table A1](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/J/MNRAS/514/497) · [All matched positions](published_counterparts.csv)

The catalogued F150W flux already rose from 19.23 ± 0.04 µJy in December 2022 to 27.45 ± 0.04 µJy in December 2024. Two January 2026 observations give 27.54 ± 0.04 and 26.44 ± 0.04 µJy. The quoted catalogue errors exclude saturation and flux-calibration systematics. The high state therefore predates the 2026 epoch. The F200W catalogue flux changed from 19.14 ± 0.03 to 29.94 ± 0.04 µJy between December 2022 and late January 2026.

We queried all 76 ESA ObsCore Level-3 NIRCam image products whose published footprint contains this exact sky position. Of those, **all 51 public products as of 2026 November 16 supplied source catalogues**, and 36 contained a centroid within 0.15 arcsec. A footprint that contains a coordinate can still include internal gaps. All 25 other products had future archive release dates. The August 2026 JW05324 repeat becomes public in February 2027. [Full per-visit photometry, mask and source hashes](all_epoch_catalogs.csv) · [Release dates and product identifiers](l3.csv)

We also measured 64 individual public detector exposures across the baseline and independent repeat visits. The earlier source cores contain saturated or unusable pixels in several bands. In 2022 the median count of saturated pixels within 0.07 arcsec is 4.5 in F150W and 2 in F277W, compared with zero in January 2026 in both bands. Fully usable 0.12–0.18 arcsec annuli gave median F200W fluxes of 2.50 and 3.02 µJy in the two epochs and F277W fluxes of 4.74 and 5.31 µJy. The corresponding F150W annular medians were 1.44 and 1.42 µJy. Core-excluded flux changes depend on the filter and aperture. The catalogue amplitude alone cannot measure an additional transient reliably. DQ masks, aperture coverage and SHA-256 hashes of the detector byte ranges are recorded per exposure. Relative astrometry with nearby reference sources gives displacements of 5–36 mas across six filters; the directions vary between bands. CANUCS DR1 object 2101748 is 0.105 arcsec away and has a formal photo-z of 0.933 (16–84% interval 0.928–0.937). The survey sets USE_PHOT=False and flags deblending and ACS photometry. The HFF ASTRODEEP fit to source 530 at 0.107 arcsec gives 1.599 (95% interval 1.093–1.749). Neither catalogue supplies a spectrum for its entry. The coincident MUSE spectrum supersedes the incompatible photometric estimates. Separate cone searches returned no entry in Gaia DR3 (10.8 arcsec), Chandra CSC 2.2 (12 arcsec), the HEASARC XMM-Newton catalogue (15 arcsec), FIRST radio (12 arcsec) or the HEASARC Million Quasars table (8 arcsec). This directly matched literature identification illustrates the incompleteness of those selections. The source catalogue and detector scripts use NumPy, pandas, SciPy and Astropy. From this directory run `python lightcurve.py`, `python native_wings.py` and `python registration.py` against the supplied immutable visit lists. Detector fluxes are fixed angular, locally background-subtracted apertures, in µJy. The annular flux fields require every contributing pixel to have finite science and positive finite error and no DO_NOT_USE/SATURATED flag. The general aperture fields allow 2% masked area and are marked with explicit coverage. Catalogued aperture-corrected fluxes and native finite-aperture fluxes have different normalizations.
