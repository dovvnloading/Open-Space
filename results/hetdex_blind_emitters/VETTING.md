# Spatial tests and candidate vetting

A catalogue entry identifies a source observed by HETDEX. A new claim would concern its measured properties. All positions are ICRS degrees. HETDEX observed wavelengths are in air. Fluxes fitted to the spectra are integrated erg s⁻¹ cm⁻². The cube aperture measurements retain the native scale of 10⁻¹⁷ erg s⁻¹ cm⁻². Aperture radii and separations are in arcseconds.

## Extended emission

The first spatial search pairs line detections within a shot using their measured sky coordinates and wavelengths. Both knots must have catalogue S/N ≥7, separations of 4–14 arcsec, a wavelength difference ≤5 Å and a representative source observation. A pair can be a blend, satellite residual or foreground galaxy. Group axes and optical galaxy sizes alone do not establish Lyα extent.

The second search draws up to 256 catalogue LAE observations per survey field at a catalogue S/N ≥9 and z of 1.88–3.52, using random seed 91841. A source can enter without a second knot. This arm samples catalogued line sources and cannot count diffuse-only objects absent from the observations table.

The follow-up uses masked, dither-combined PDR1 cubes. A seven-bin (14 Å) narrowband image is continuum-subtracted using the adjacent two seven-bin windows, separated from the line core. Every nonzero quality bit is excluded. The local sky is estimated at 9–20 arcsec. The central 1.5-arcsec aperture and the extension statistic are compared to identical measurements in disjoint offset spectral windows in the same cube. At least 12 usable control windows are required. Reported empirical sigma uses the median absolute deviation. The tail fraction includes one pseudocount and cannot establish Gaussian 5-sigma tail probabilities from a few dozen controls.

For two knots, fit two Moffat profiles with beta 2.5 and the observed seeing and subtract their interpolated wings. The test integrates residual emission along the line between their centers, farther than 1.3 seeing FWHM from either center and within 0.75 FWHM of the connecting axis. The central-source test integrates the one-profile residual in the annulus from 1.5 to 3.0 seeing FWHM. Positive evidentiary requirements include a ≥5-empirical-sigma value at the source and in the relevant extension statistic, clean controls, repetition of the extension in a distinct shot, and evidence for the line identity. Imperfect seeing, profile mismatch, an unrecognized neighboring source and spatial resampling can all mimic extended emission. Source counts depend on the knot, point-source, repeat and surface-brightness selection.

## Spectroscopic and archival checks

- Joined source and representative-detection fields include the HETDEX redshift method, class, fibre ID, seeing, strongest-fibre weight and broadband association where available. Source ID, shot ID and detection ID identify each extraction. Repeat tests fit an integrated Gaussian at the measured wavelength with a local continuum in the original data. A second reduction of the same shot supplies no independent verification.
- Forced measurements at foreground-line hypotheses look for corroborating low-redshift lines. Single-line catalogue assignments and individual companion coincidences remain review flags. Secure foreground assignments, convincing multiple optical companions and catalogue AGN identifications remove a lead from the discovery pool.
- The independent DESI follow-up VAC uses a 1.25-arcsec positional radius. Secure visual redshifts are compared to the trial redshift. SPARCL provides additional DESI and SDSS/BOSS spectroscopy. An independently detected line at the nominated wavelength or distinct HETDEX shot addresses cosmic-ray and single-fibre interpretations. A dither-combined cube does not separately expose each fibre or exposure. A secure independent spectroscopic redshift or corroborated high-z line pair addresses foreground identifications.
- All eight He II systems in Table 1 of Mirza Khanlari et al. (2026) are checked by detection identifier or position and redshift. The LAN catalogue provides published resolved-source decisions, radii, redshifts, photometry and Lyα equivalent widths. A prior published extreme EW or resolved morphology is recorded as a recovery of that property. A nearby continuum object or an ordinary HETDEX LAE identification alone does not establish the rare property was already reported.
- Ranked leads receive 3-arcsec NED, SIMBAD and Legacy Surveys queries. The ledger retains separations, types, redshifts/bibliographic fields, query URLs, service outcomes and response hashes. HSC DR2 r-band cutouts and the HETDEX imaging associations supply additional continuum constraints. Photometry reports units, aperture and uncertainties. Noncoverage and service errors carry no flux limit. WISE colors are calculated from Tractor forced W1/W2 AB fluxes. A competing AGN interpretation is flagged only for a well-isolated, unmasked bright counterpart (W2 ≤15 Vega) whose W1–W2 lower two-sigma bound exceeds 0.8 Vega. Blended or faint WISE colors do not classify a source. [Stern et al. (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/753/1/30). Catalogue redshifts of uncertain provenance cannot independently confirm the HETDEX measurement.
- Each release reserves up to 32 direct external follow-ups per channel. The blob slots are split equally between the two spatial arms. The imaging-led EW search receives up to 32 additional slots with its own ranking. It requires a conservative independent photometric EW lower bound and fails if the spectrum excludes that EW even at three sigma. Ranking favors independent line repetitions and independent DESI observations, then selection strength. Rows outside the follow-up budget remain unresolved. Stored dispositions count leads, including different observations, hypotheses and reductions of the same object.
- Line widths and UV metal constraints screen for ordinary broad-line or UV-bright AGN. A narrow-line spectrum, lack of a broadband association, and an empty catalogue cone cannot establish the absence of an obscured nucleus. Any qualifying source requires physical follow-up before claims about stellar populations or black holes. A peak with no match at the HETDEX catalogue redshift still admits Lyα at another redshift throughout the entire band.
