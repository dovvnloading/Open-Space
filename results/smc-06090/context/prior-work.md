# Prior-report check for OGLE-SMC-ECL-06090

Checked 13 November 2026, US Eastern time. Identifiers used included OGLE-SMC-ECL-06090, OGLE-SMC-ECL-6090, SMC121.3.2046, SMC738.16.542 and Gaia DR3 4686449235706841984. Catalog coordinates are 01h 24m 15.06s, −73° 19′ 33.4″ (J2000). Searches combined the IDs, aliases or coordinates with “apsidal motion”, “apsidal”, “eclipse timing”, “eclipsing binary” and “precession”. A SIMBAD coordinate search used a five-arcsecond radius, and the resulting aliases and five bibcodes are in simbad6090.txt.

The OGLE-III and OGLE-IV remarks lists contain no entry for this star. Those releases and Gaia do catalogue the binary itself. The historic OGLE-III catalog paper explicitly reports 32 objects with noticeable apsidal motion. The accompanying remarks do not mark 6090. A text/target-list comparison also found no identification of 6090 in the SMC apsidal-motion studies of Hong et al. (2015, 27 targets; 2016, 90 targets), Zasche et al. (2014, 18 targets), or Zasche et al. (2019, 21 targets). The 21 SMC systems in Juryšek et al.'s inclination-change study were also checked. This search found no prior report of the timing reversal for this star. A different alias, an unindexed source, an unexamined catalog or unpublished work could alter that assessment.
