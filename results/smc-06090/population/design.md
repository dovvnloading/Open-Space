# Comparison population chosen before measuring its eclipse timings
With the target behavior already known, take every OGLE-IV SMC non-contact binary with I within 0.75 mag of 18.225, orbital periods 0.75–1.50 times 1.1696719 days and both catalog eclipse depths at least 0.15 mag. Include the target. Do not select on measured timing changes. Use OGLE-IV 2010–2014 (HJD before 2457000) and 2022+ (HJD from 2459700), plus the original OGLE-III two seasons groups at HJD 2453500 for a separate test of the reversal where a catalog crossmatch exists. Fit the eclipse centers and widths independently in each window; include the out-of-eclipse second harmonic. Require ≥40 good points, ≥5 different nights within 0.06 cycle of each fitted minimum, plausible non-boundary depths/widths/centers and maximum per-epoch formal separation uncertainty 0.015 cycles. Use each target's OGLE-IV period/epoch in both surveys. Report absolute OGLE-IV early-to-late shift relative to comparison stars; report the count of reversal-amplitudes at least as large in both intervals, allowing either sign orientation. This measures comparative rarity among observable analogues, not a search-corrected p-value or population incidence. Publish all attempted measurements, quality failures and thresholds; show sensitivity to quality thresholds.
