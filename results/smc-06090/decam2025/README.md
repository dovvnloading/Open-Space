# 2025 DECam observations cover a predicted egress

Archive metadata identifies 27 unique pipeline-calibrated science exposures near the field center on 2025 October 24 UTC, archived under proposal 2025B-285894. Each has two reductions. The NOIRLab archive's detector-level coordinate query returns 22 of the 27 at the position of OGLE-SMC-ECL-06090: 11 N501 and 11 N540, matched on HDUs 27, 28 or 34. This overlap is the archive footprint test; full calibrated pixels are needed to confirm usable target photometry on each exposure. In the response to the advanced-search query every October image has release date **2027-04-16**. Attempts to retrieve the HDUs returned HTTP 403. As of November 13, 2026 (US Eastern), this is a metadata-only result.

The first N540 midpoint was 03:45:29.729 UTC (300-second exposure), and the first N501 midpoint was 03:55:07.877 UTC (800 seconds). The last N501 midpoint was 07:32:15.211 UTC. Catalog phases corrected to HJD UTC are 0.53248, 0.53820 and 0.66711. The empirical opposite-motion fit to the OGLE photometry predicts the secondary center at phase 0.52859 and a modeled Gaussian width of 41.1 minutes on that date. The first two exposure midpoints fall 6.6 and 16.2 minutes after the conditional center, and the final image 233.3 minutes after. These timestamps could cover the egress and out-of-eclipse continuum; the model puts the central instant before the first exposure. Filter-dependent depths, long integrations, plate astrometry and comparison-star stability would need to be accounted for in measuring an egress. There is no DECam flux measurement or time of minimum in this analysis.

The successful NOIRLab coordinate request was `https://astroarchive.noirlab.edu/api/sia/vohdu?POS=21.06275,-73.32594444&SIZE=0.0001&format=json`. The full detector-level response can be retrieved at that URL. selection.json retains unique v1 science-image entries on the five selected October/November nights. The October release-date query POSTed meta.json to `https://astroarchive.noirlab.edu/api/adv_search/find/?limit=200&sort=md5sum`; meta-final.json contains the 54 reductions, their start/mid/end UTC timestamps and release dates. On October 24 the SIA file_dateobs matches the catalog dateobs_center. Source images are credited to the observing team and NSF NOIRLab Astro Data Archive. [Archive](https://astroarchive.noirlab.edu/) · [NOIRLab SIA/HDU documentation](https://astroquery.readthedocs.io/en/latest/noirlab/noirlab.html)

Run `python assess.py` with NumPy and Astropy to recreate phases.csv. Inputs include the position query and the OGLE-only fit parameters in model.json, copied from the all-photometry opposite-motion solution in the alternatives investigation. The phase calculation uses the CTIO site, the specified target coordinates and the built-in solar-system ephemeris. It estimates HJD on the UTC scale, preserving the UTC timestamp; it does not claim a measured eclipse. Photometric data used to fit the predicted motion were collected by OGLE.
