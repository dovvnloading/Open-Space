# AT2022fpx and IC240307A remain a coordinate-bounds overlap

AT2022fpx is 14.249° from the best-fit direction of IC240307A. The source lies within the broad RA/Dec extrema of this short, partially contained track. Its brightest sampled post-discovery W2 visit precedes the alert by 57.472 days. The magnitude difference from the preceding W2 visit is only 0.0125 mag. The source passes the ±180-day screen, with no public native region in this analysis to establish membership or randomize the alert. The TDECat source list was inspected after the primary modern search. This pair has no calibrated significance or claim of novelty. [Supplemental screen](supplement_tdecat.csv) · [IceCube circular](https://gcn.nasa.gov/circulars/35898) · [W2 visit data](../../gammair/tdecat/w2_visits.csv)

The original neutrino circular contains an inconsistent month in its body; the archived notice, event ID, arrival timestamp, publication date, and subsequent two circulars identify March 7. IceCube's separate two-day follow-up gave p=0.75 for additional track events. LAT reported no significant new excess at the **neutrino best fit**. This is a different position from AT2022fpx. [IceCube follow-up](https://gcn.nasa.gov/circulars/35905) · [Fermi/LAT follow-up](https://gcn.nasa.gov/circulars/35912)

The source is an independently studied extreme coronal-line emitter. Its published optical/UV peak is near MJD 59783, about 593 days before the neutrino, and its earlier X-ray rebrightening appears in the source's 2022–23 multiwavelength campaign. The frozen source photometry covers seven optical and infrared facility/filter combinations within ±180 d of the alert. The nearest ATLAS orange-filter measurement was 4.76 d earlier and the nearest ZTF g/r measurements were about 6.9 d earlier. Source-specific optical/X-ray activity at the time of the neutrino has not been established. A finite search on 2026-11-17 for the AT2022fpx and IC240307A identifiers together, including GCN/ATel, located the separately published source and alert histories; those sampled reports did not identify the pair.
