# Frozen analysis plan

Frozen at the first commit on this branch, before crossmatching. Publication and event cutoff 2026-11-17T12:09:02Z. Archive retrieval may occur later. Record the actual latest entry, release and censoring for each product. Scope includes archival IceCat tracks and all public Gold/Bronze track alerts from 2024-01-01 through cutoff. List any intervening catalogue gap. Use transient events with reference epoch from 2024-01-01 through cutoff for the new search. Earlier epochs are reserved for named controls. Use UTC, ICRS, inclusive windows and one physical alert per run/event. Mask the four named control sources from discovery. Do not select EM objects on the basis of a neutrino follow-up.

## Frozen search

| Family | Eligible EM events and reference epoch | Allowed neutrino time minus reference epoch |
|---|---|---|
| Optical | Classified TDEs or explicitly classified nuclear flares in survey/TNS records. Use documented optical maximum if available, otherwise discovery. Merge aliases and report anchor kind. | 0 to +365 d |
| X-ray | Independent EP or Swift X-ray transient triggers, including GRBs if in the frozen trigger catalogue. Use first published independent trigger, deduplicate the same episode. Exclude pointed follow-up and unreconciled identities. | −1 to +7 d |
| Gamma-ray | Fermi LAT LCR 7-day fixed-index bins. Use good converged detected bins, TS ≥9, positive flux and uncertainty, flux/error ≥3. At least 10 qualifying bins from mission start through cutoff. Select bins ≥3× median flux of qualifying bins. Merge adjacent selected bins, anchor on brightest bin midpoint, earliest in a tie. | −7 to +7 d |
| Mid-infrared | Independently selected published WISE/NEOWISE flare catalogue with identifiable peak epoch. Use the author's primary band/peak or W2 if both bands have peaks and no band is preferred. | −180 to +180 d |

Use latest applicable released event-specific revision at cutoff, including public retractions and supersessions. Prefer the collaboration's updated archival localization when available; document the hierarchy among map products. Apply the collaboration-calibrated event-specific 90% contour. Compare a point with its native HEALPix threshold/mask, or published spherical polygon with minor great-circle edges. Use a circle only if explicitly defined as the 90% region by the publisher. RA/Dec extrema, approximate equal-area circles, and a probability approximation of an official likelihood contour cannot qualify. Log unmatched geometry and source support. Use EM catalogue positions as point estimates; flag large EM localization errors for candidate-level robustness. Use run/event IDs and verified aliases for deduplication, allow disjoint observing epochs of the same source.

## Controls and multiplicity

Observation unit is a unique alert. For each of four families record (1) the number of distinct alerts with one or more matches and (2) the largest inverse published 90%-region area among matched alerts, zero if none. Area specifically measures localization specificity, and the single-pair ranking can tie. An association requires independent corroboration and its own multiplicity-controlled test. Population excess alone cannot establish an individual source.

Generate 9,999 randomizations per null with NumPy PCG64, seeds 20261117 (RA) and 20261118 (time). In each RA trial independently rotate each alert and its entire region around the celestial pole by a uniform offset, keeping times and declinations. In each time trial permute event timestamps within UTC calendar year, calendar quarter and alert tier (Gold, Bronze, other); retain positions and regions together, keep singletons fixed. Hold the EM catalogues, frozen epochs, selection and family masks fixed. Compute the upper-tail empirical p=(1+exceedances)/(1+trials) for both statistics, counting ties against significance. Multiply each p by eight (four families × two statistics), cap at one, and require both nulls to pass, using the larger corrected p. Keep eight even when a family has no usable data. Report null quantiles, exceedance count, number of movable events, sky/season or pointed-selection caveats and unresolved cross-survey duplicates. No inference for a missing family, a neutrino-selected sample, or an incomplete or degenerate null. If RA exposure is incompatible with uniformity or shuffled strata do not support the test, withhold calibrated interpretation pending exposure modelling.

Treat corrected p<0.01 under both nulls as follow-up only. Discovery level requires one-sided global p≤2.87e-7 under both nulls, checked survey/detector selection, identifying a specific source after all trials, independent corroboration and prior-art search. Initial MC cannot resolve this threshold. Escalation requires a predeclared fixed sample size sufficient to bound the global tail under both nulls (or an independently validated tail method), plus published-vs-revised localization robustness, optical/X-ray/gamma/IR light curves and survey coverage. Record source aliases, literature, GCN and ATel search dates and receipts for new pairs. A new pair, a significant population excess and identification of a neutrino source are distinct claims.

## Named controls and completeness

Audit TXS 0506+056, AT2019dsg, AT2019fdr and AT2019aalc against the original claimed localization, contemporary timing and later IceCube maps. Record exact event ID, counterpart position, temporal anchor, angular separation and native-contour membership for each available localization. Atmospheric and chance events remain possible. Report failed, uncovered and historical-only controls individually. Do not modify thresholds to recover them. Synthetic injection and permutation checks exercise implementation separately. Freeze every usable observational payload with SHA-256 or a versioned upstream file checksum, record download failures, and distinguish a descriptive subset calculation from a full survey result.
