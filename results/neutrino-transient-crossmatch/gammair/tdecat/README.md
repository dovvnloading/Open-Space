# Published TDEs with infrared flares

Langis et al. (2026), section 4.4, identify these 18 optical TDEs as having observed infrared flares. Their GitHub photometry supplies 403 W2 visits with at least three usable WISE or NEOWISE measurements. Seven sources have their brightest measured post-discovery visit in 2024. For six the mission ended with the source still at its brightest sampled visit. The remaining source, AT2022fpx, has a bracketed maximum on 2024-01-09 (MJD 60318.851). Its W2 medians in the three successive visits are 12.3375, 12.325 and 12.440 mag. The first two are close, so the peak time within that broad plateau is uncertain. An observed maximum supplies a sampling epoch. The intrinsic maximum may occur between visits or after the final measurement.

The sample uses all 18 authors' IR identifications independently of neutrino positions. The sample was added after the initial archival MIR search, so treat it as supplementary. Preserve the right-censoring column when testing these epochs. The maximum is derived from the authors' photometry. The method selects W2 measurements with finite positive magnitude error corresponding to signal-to-noise of at least three, groups visits separated by more than 30 days, and takes the median date and magnitude of every visit with at least three detections. It picks the brightest median after optical discovery. No all-sky rate can be inferred from this optically selected population.
