# Fixed ephemeris test of Sector 108

Use every entry in `eumemic/exoplanets` candidates/ephemerides.csv at commit e2eee928fd621f75c1621f6b4206d34bb222ae59 whose TIC occurs in the official Sector 108 two-minute SPOC light-curve manifest. Fit no ephemeris or duration to the holdout. Use quality zero, finite PDCSAP_FLUX, central 80% of predicted duration, and baseline windows from 0.8 to 2 durations on both sides. Estimate local flux by interpolation between medians in the two baseline windows. Require at least 65% nominal coverage in each window. Clip only the top and bottom 10% in averaging the in-event relative flux (flares and cosmic-ray residuals). Combine event depths with equal weights. Repeat with SAP and a narrower local baseline. Report covered opportunities and individual event depths. Compare with 199 equal-spaced phase offsets, excluding offsets within 2.5 durations of true events; these overlap, so their ranks are descriptive. Report both time halves, and the five whole-sample outcomes irrespective of sign. Detector and astrophysical false positives remain possible. Evidence is for repeatability in a new epoch. Freeze these choices before examining any Sector 108 light-curve flux values.
