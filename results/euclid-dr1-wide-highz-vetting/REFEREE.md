# Independent referee decision — Euclid DR1-Foundation wide high-z search

**Decision: PASS for a transparently caveated catalogue-screening measurement; FAIL for any validated new quasar, calibrated probability or completeness claim.** No remarkable-findings designation. The result can be archived/merged as a *measured, inconclusive* data screen, provided its README explicitly retains every caution below.

## What passed independent replay

The separate [referee.py](referee.py), rather than calling the scanner/aggregator's selection methods, audited the committed publisher index SHA-256, **32 batch output hashes**, exact **768/768 partition index order**, full **888,152,165-row** publisher total, **462,500** independently retested stored strict-selected records, **62,358** pathological J−H tail entries, known-control receipts, and the **478-object** derived diagnostic cohort. Each batch contains its selected-source receipts and source-object metadata. GitHub Actions replay passed and emitted [output/referee.json](output/referee.json) with status `INDEPENDENT_RECEIPT_REPLAY_PASS_NO_DISCOVERY_CLAIM`. The rounded >3σ ground-optical threshold must be evaluated after the aggregate's three-decimal SNR reporting; the first referee attempt exposed this rounding mismatch and was corrected in a subsequent short, receipt-reusing run. All full-scan partition checkpoints were retained.

**Important measurement/geometry distinction:** the HATS cells geometrically cover **3,107.065 deg²**, whereas the release has a **nominal ~1,900 deg²** Wide Survey imaging area. Neither is a measured clean effective area; the 3,107 number is only HATS-cell union area, so no survey sensitivity/completeness or absence constraint follows.

## Scientific objections / interpretation gates

1. **Control failure is material.** There are 120 frozen z>6.5 known quasars, of which 24 are catalogue-matched within 3 arcsec; **3/24 = 12.5%** pass the unchanged strict cut. This is conditional catalogue-matched recovery, not completeness across a verified imaged footprint. The z>7 matched subset is **3/8**, still not representative.
2. **The borrowed NISP shape filter is not a validated QSO point-source test.** Only **1/24** matched known quasars falls in its adopted narrow diagnostic band; **0/3** strict-recovered controls does. A rank-selected sample of 478 may be heavily nonrepresentative. VIS POINT_LIKE_PROB is inapplicable to VIS dropouts.
3. **The numerical Q/dwarf model is not calibrated.** An arbitrary quasar prior (0.001, alternative 0.01), 24+14 selected controls, 0.32-mag KDE bandwidth, poor leave-one-out Brier score **0.629861**, missing galaxies/blends/artifacts, and unverified selection function preclude field posterior interpretations. The very red highest-rank objects are outside its empirical colour support; do not extrapolate confidence to them.
4. **External-vetting failure is reported rather than hidden.** Of 24 objects queried, all have at least one external-service error. CatWISE has just one matched W1/W2+motion object; other unWISE/CatWISE rows are empty or errors. Gaia empty cones are not proof of stationary objects. SIMBAD, DESI, SDSS and ESO query errors, plus incomplete MAST metadata, do not constitute spectrum or literature clearance.
5. **Images remain unverified.** The top eight SIA locations yielded metadata; bounded VIS/NISP FITS cutout attempts returned HTTP 400 and zero saved science pixels. Detector ghosts, persistence, edges, blending and other artefacts were not checked at the exposure-pixel level; no object is image-cleared.
6. **Prior-work and population constraints are open.** The March/July 2026 control lists are dated, not a complete November-known census. The illustrative numerical LF integration predicts **18.710** for z=7–9 to J_AB≤23 over the nominal 1,900 deg² (with a distinct **7.6** Yang area-scaled benchmark) and cannot be compared to this raw catalogue or ranked sample without masks and injection tests. There is no new confirmed source or valid null upper bound.

**Publication-level verdict:** do not advertise 478 quasars, a credible highest-z candidate, a photometric discovery, a QSO/dwarf purity, or a 1,900-deg² completeness measurement. A **reproducible full public MER catalogue screen with an explicitly unresolved follow-up shortlist** is a defensible repository contribution. Public spectroscopy and pixel-artefact checks would be new work needed for scientific discovery claims.
