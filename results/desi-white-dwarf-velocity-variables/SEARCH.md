# DESI DR1 white dwarf velocity screening

2026-11-15 UTC. The exploratory screen uses repeat exposures of catalogued DA stars, requires blue and red cameras to change in the same direction, and ranks the weaker of the two arm significances. The strongest object, WD 1013−010, is a published compact binary. The next two, WDJ042155.26−132531.35 and WDJ023809.44+273329.79, show catalogued |ΔRV| of 246/260 and 270/261 km/s respectively (blue/red). Each of Hα, Hβ, Hγ and Hδ changes in the same direction in an independent reduction of those same exposures. Cross-survey and literature assessments are in progress. These are measured changes in line-of-sight velocity. No companion type, orbital period, or mass has been established.

Inputs are Swan et al. DESI DR1 catalogue v1.0 (Zenodo record 23453342) and its Warwick individual-exposure spectra. The associated data and the complete screen will be included with the final disposition.
