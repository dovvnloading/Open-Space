The closest catalog sources lie near/inside aperture scales and must be considered in a deblend. Historical W2 magnitudes cannot be directly subtracted from SPHEREx narrow channels: epochs, spectral responses, variability and extended morphology differ. Position agreement of the bright target-centered signal does not by itself exclude blends.
