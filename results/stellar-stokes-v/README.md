# Circularly polarised radio emission: published stellar sources, no new validated discovery

**Result (17 November 2026): no new circularly polarised emitter or planet-linked burst was validated.** Cross-matching public, signed Stokes V catalogues recovered **21 distinct V-LoTSS DR2 sources** and **46 distinct RACS-low2 source records**, all already present in those published catalogues with stellar identifications. They produce 35 and 91 target–source pairs because Gaia, CNS5, host and binary/component entries repeat physical objects. **Two confirmed planet hosts have previously catalogued V detections: GJ 1151 (V-LoTSS) and COCONUTS-2 A = L 34-26 (RACS-low2).** A separate VLASS image search found **zero ≥5σ events in 11 independent non-host positions and 88 offsets**. These are catalogue recoveries and a scoped null test, **not new discoveries or evidence that the planets produce radio emission**.

## What was actually covered

The input census contains 48,809 Gaia DR3 stars within 50 pc (parallax ≥20 mas, S/N ≥10), 5,908 CNS5 entries, 372 L/T/Y dwarfs with usable coordinates/motion, and **4,862 confirmed-planet hosts** (4,480 with matched Gaia proper motions; **382 do not have validated epochs/motions**). These are four overlapping samples, not four independent populations. Source-catalogue searches **do not measure sensitivity at every target absent from the source list**. Restored image *footprints* and images actually sampled for local V noise are different denominators.

The listed 5×RMS values are **local detection thresholds, not upper limits for these already detected sources**. Do not compare integrated LOFAR flux with ASKAP peak brightness as though the instruments had identical beams or bands. No per-host 5σ **nondetection** limit was derived for the 4,477 footprint-only ASKAP hosts or the unobserved hosts: the required forced V/I pixel cutouts, local noise and leakage corrections are missing. Published SRSC measurements for five V-observed hosts provide prior reports, not new homogeneous noise/limits. In the **11 non-host VLASS** positions where local V was *actually measured*, 5σ V sensitivities are **0.892–1.045 mJy/beam**, with a largest absolute on-target excursion of **2.79σ**. The CNS5 277 alias has a different inherited coordinate epoch and is retained as an **alternative**, not a second dwarf or an independent trial; nearby Gaia components inside one beam are likewise not independent. ## Disposition of every catalogue match and false associations

[`match_dispositions.csv`](validation/match_dispositions.csv) classifies **every one of the 126 on-target catalogue pairs** (35 V-LoTSS plus 91 RACS-low2). Each source record has a published V catalogue identity and stellar association, so all 126 are `published` **at the radio-source level**; zero qualify as new-and-unvalidated sources, and no observed pair was established as a chance alignment. This is **not** 126 independent stars/detections: 21 + 46 distinct *survey records* (not necessarily distinct astrophysical stars across surveys). RACS entries landing near multiple Gaia sources have an explicit unresolved-*component* caveat; they cannot be used to claim V from every one of those individual components. V-LoTSS source **i Boo** is in the accepted V catalogue but lacked a name overlap with the paper's Table A.2: it is not a newly discovered source. **Outside selected footprint** is separately tabulated for 384 hosts; `footprint_only` means *no flux classification*, not a detected source or a validated nondetection. [Per-source classifications and caveats](validation/match_dispositions.csv) · [Disposition counts and file SHA-256](validation/disposition_receipt.json)

- **V-LoTSS:** 0 matches in eight offset catalogues per sample, versus 1/20/14/0 unshifted matches. Empirical false-pair estimate **0 per unshifted sample search**; a zero-count Poisson 95% upper mean is **0.374 false pairs per sample search** (= −ln 0.05 / 8).
- **RACS-low2:** 0 matches in 32 offsets per radio record per sample, versus 1/51/39/0 real pairs. Empirical estimate **0 per unshifted sample search**; corresponding zero-count Poisson 95% upper mean **0.094 pairs per sample search** (= −ln 0.05 / 32).
- **VLASS pixels:** 0/88 independent offset positions pass |V| ≥ 5 local MAD-RMS, versus 0/11 independently counted target positions; maximum control |SNR| is 2.18.

These rotated, nearby offsets are **not fully independent random sky trials** and their upper means are heuristic, not a calibrated all-sky false-discovery rate. Close binaries, source selection, source leakage, small sample sizes, and multiple targets sharing a synthesized beam remain important. The control null does not itself prove that every individual target association or polarization mechanism is correct. ## Completed tests, unresolved tests and reproducibility

**Completed:** input samples frozen with source query URLs and SHA-256s; observation-epoch astrometry applied where present; released V catalogue sources and matched target pairs enumerated; RACS-low2 local catalogue V noise included for detected sources; V-LoTSS signed catalogue fluxes and errors retained; 16,503 selected ASKAP V product polygons intersected with all hosts; all 12 accessible VLASS V images inspected with paired I cutouts, local median/MAD RMS, native-pixel measurements and beam-separated eight-point controls; published counterparts cross-checked against Yiu et al. and the [Radio Stars Catalogue](https://radiostars.org/); one nearest TESS SPOC 120-s sector examined for each of six dated host/radio cases. **None of those six optical light-curve sectors overlaps the radio epoch** (nearest gaps ≥103 days), so optical excursions cannot validate the radio burst or activity state at that time. [TESS checks](validation/tess_activity.csv) · [TESS query and FITS checksums](validation/tess_receipt.json) · [Published-source receipt](validation/known_receipt.json)

**Pending / outside evidence:** unlock and download full DR3 Stokes products (the three tested archives returned HTTP 202); forced local Stokes V **and I** imaging at host positions inside RACS/VAST polygons, with quality, beam, primary-beam, instrumental-leakage and per-epoch checks; repeat/independent V epochs for any prospective new source; component-resolved radio astrometry for close Gaia pairs; simultaneous optical/radio or sufficiently precise orbital ephemeris testing before attributing emission to a planet. The frozen planet-period file is an inventory, **not** an orbital-phase detection. No all-sky event-rate or nondetection constraint is inferred from these source-selected and partial-image releases.
