# PUNCH two-image development comparison

Two 2026-11-01 PUNCH CTM v0l mosaics were compared in an authorized descriptive development run. The independently reproduced calculation retained 9,674,839 and 9,646,666 native eligible pixels, and 7,215,505 common pixels out of 16,777,216. The 64×64 block-mean difference contains 1,949 finite blocks. [Grid and receipts](execution/) document the calculation; the figure clips colors at the 98th percentile of absolute finite block values. This is not a front detection, physical flux change, significance test or absence claim.

The exact executed code is [development.py](development.py), with [manifest](execution/manifest.json), [authorization](execution/authorization.json), [result](execution/development.json), [command](execution/command.json), [execution receipt](execution/execution.json), [console log](execution/run.log), and [independent SciPy replay](execution/independent_check.json). Independent pixel mapping agreed to 1.51e-28 in the grid. It uses the same two observations, not independent photons. Astropy emitted a FITSFixedWarning for CROTA; retain this in the log. Raw images remain external at the paths and checksums in [acquisition receipt](execution/receipt.json). The planned 00:16 image was neither acquired in full nor decoded and no held-out prediction was made. The earlier [identity-WCS preflight code](execution/preflight-bffa92b8.py) found mismatching serialized WCS headers before array access; different observation timestamps contributed, so this is a failed identity check, not evidence of misregistration. The later reviewed angular reprojection revision was used for the actual run.

The packed FITS uncertainty extension is reciprocal fractional uncertainty despite its copied brightness BUNIT. The reader unpacks sigma=data/packed and uses finite positive decoded values and a conservative four-pixel stencil. This is not a validated quality mask; shared stellar/background subtraction, WFI seams, observer parallax and correlated interpolation remain unquantified. Observer thresholds are engineering gates, not physical uncertainty bounds. No additional images or target-pixel diagnostics are used. The numerical 64×64 grid is retained as produced; the rendering is descriptive.

# Original metadata-only feasibility record (before development run)

Question: can an outward-moving white-light front selected from disjoint early observations predict its location in a later, previously unread exposure? The observing unit is a front, not a pixel or a mosaic tile. A failure to find the predicted front, given valid coverage and a predeclared noise/registration tolerance, falsifies that particular propagation prediction; it does not prove the absence of a CME. Brightness is line-of-sight scattering, not a direct density measurement. Stationary starfield, detector-frame structure, seams, changing background and projection are alternatives.

Three 2026-11-01 v0l CTM mosaic products at 00:00, 00:08 and 00:16 are frozen access proofs, not an event-selected sample. Their actual FILE PROVENANCE tables list the nine distinct CR1/CR2/CR3 level-1 parent files; independently queried parent headers establish 24-second exposures and nonoverlap across epochs. See provenance.json, parent_headers.json and receipts.json. Mosaic windows and parent identities do not prove independent systematic errors. Four-spacecraft TELESCOP header is generic: v0l excludes NFI. Exact byte-range hashes are receipts, not full-file hashes. At that metadata-only stage no science pixels were decoded. Range responses can contain bytes beyond the FITS header; none were interpreted.

Preliminary design: reserve 00:00 and 00:08 for development and 00:16 for a prospective prediction, with later epoch unopened until an explicit separate authorization. Prior to any image access, a reviewed executable protocol must fix detector-specific ROI, celestial and instrument-frame masks, geometric mapping, background model and its independent inputs, valid footprint/quality coverage, allowed speeds, sign, tolerances, search trials and controls. A known CME (e.g. 2025-05-31) is an external positive calibration, not proof this November interval contains a front. Build synthetic moving-front and detector-fixed/star-fixed injection and registration controls. Parent overlap, shared preprocessing backgrounds, missing independent comparison, incomplete masks or inadequate coverage make the experiment inconclusive. One 8-minute separation and three epochs may be inadequate: do not tune or backfill after image inspection. L3 is excluded until exact parent and background windows are shown disjoint.

No fixed ROI or scientific threshold was defensible from those metadata. Therefore that stage was not an authorization for image access. guard.py only checks the selected metadata identities and temporal separation and refuses all science phases. This is an explicitly staged planning record. Version-specific WFI photometry, registration, masks, L1/L2 background lineage, actual ROI coverage and independently covered coronagraph geometry still require proof. SOHO/LASCO is a potential comparator; neither coverage nor geometry for this interval is established. Catalog/event history for any eventual candidate must be recorded before a novelty assessment.
