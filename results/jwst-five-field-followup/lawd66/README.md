## Astrometry, colors and counterparts

- Every 6-MADσ local maximum inside each image’s usable annulus enters the search, even if absent in the first epoch or missing from the pipeline source catalog. Ninety-two of the 93 individual detections preferred fixed sky to the host in at least one same-passband epoch with ≥0.22″ host displacement. All nine of their distinct sky positions repeat. For each peak, the nearest source for a hypothesis had to lie within 0.10″, with a ≥0.08″ advantage over the alternative. We checked the full set of 14 off-visit image comparisons per trigger, including cross-band nearest neighbors, using a wider comparison region to retain boundary-crossing objects. A 0.10″ complete-linkage cut groups repeated detections. [1,302 comparisons](tracks.csv) · [Source groups](sources.csv)
- The single-epoch peak was 3.274″ from LAWD 66 on April 21, 2025, at registered coordinates 257.863643°, −14.799260°. F164N forced flux was 0.802 ± 0.133 µJy. No other visit’s catalog contains a source within 0.8″ of either tested position. At the host-following location, subthreshold March 2024 and September 2025 apertures gave 0.595 ± 0.156 and 0.499 ± 0.189 µJy, while March 2025 gave −0.149 ± 0.135 µJy. Its simultaneous F277W measurement is consistent with background. Field crowding, azimuth-dependent residuals, or a transient can affect single-epoch peaks. Its origin remains open. [Forced fluxes and independent catalog distances in all 16 images](unmatched_forced.csv)
- For each detection, we measured both bands with the merged archival aperture routine. A three-sample-radius aperture is normalized to the same-epoch JWST pipeline total flux for the host. The reported errors combine the scatter of nearby apertures at the same separation with a 5% relative term. We compared the measured flux pair to the full Sonora Bobcat cloud-free planet/brown-dwarf grid across its three tabulated metallicities at the Gaia distance, retaining the nearest grid point and its two-band chi-square per peak. A masked F277W measurement for one F164N trigger prevents that visit’s cross-band fit. Every distinct persistent source has a usable two-band measurement in at least one visit. The minimum chi-square supplies a conditional color/brightness comparison, without a unique temperature, gravity or mass determination. [Photometry, noise and model comparisons](peaks.csv) · [Model release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5063476)
- The 45″ field queries returned 52 Gaia DR3 entries, 174 Pan-STARRS DR2 mean objects, one SIMBAD entry (LAWD 66), and no NASA Exoplanet Archive PS row. The ESA HST footprint query returned zero overlapping observations. We compared every peak to Gaia positions at the JWST epoch with catalog proper motion where available and to the host-following locus at Gaia's reference epoch. For PS1, the host-following prediction uses each object’s mean epoch. The nearest fixed-sky and host-following catalog IDs and distances are given separately. Cone completeness, blending and catalog depths limit these checks. Nine repeated objects have a direct JWST astrometric test, including those missing optical counterparts. ## Reduction and evidence

1. We paired all 16 PUBLIC MAST Level 3 mosaics with their pipeline catalogs, retaining MAST numeric observation IDs, exposures, URIs and raw query responses for programs 2692, 4519 and 6584. STScI cloud object listings supply sizes, change dates and multipart ETags. The 32 verified products total 5,363,177,794 bytes. Narrowband pupils F164N and F323N set the effective passbands of the F150W2/F164N and F322W2/F323N combinations. [Input manifest and receipts](inputs.json)
2. We identified the bright nearby white dwarf using the DR3 proper motion, independently centroided it on each calibrated mosaic, and resampled each native SCI/WHT cutout at 0.031″ or 0.063″. To register the common field, we used outlier-clipped translation fits to compact sources 7–50″ from the host, with the first F164N image defining the reference. Each other mosaic retained 105–194 ties with 0.006–0.016″ radial RMS. Host-relative positions include the observed parallax as well as proper motion. Single-epoch aperture and Gaia extrapolations have additional WCS, parallax and blend uncertainties. [Epoch metadata](epochs.csv)
3. The adapter calls the merged white-dwarf search’s sky grid, difference-of-Gaussians response, local-maximum finder, subpixel refinement, injection iterator and fixed/background classifier. Local medians and MADs are measured in radial bins. A visit-specific point-spread function comes from an isolated, pipeline-compact field star. We use its tapered 21×21 stamp scaled by the same-visit catalog host/reference flux ratio. For each tested ratio and position, we add its filtered response to the real data before applying the blind detection threshold. For paired trials we also require the same host-motion decision used on the real positions. Zero-signal controls prevent a pre-existing recovery from counting. Each epoch and each baseline have discrete curves, with no interpolation, global mass limit or all-azimuth completeness assertion. [Reproducible reducer](reduce_lawd.py)

## Replay

Install Python with NumPy, SciPy, pandas and Astropy. From the repository root set FIELD to this directory and CACHE to an empty writable data directory. The archived model table is fetched on first use and verified against the existing campaign hash. External queries can be replayed offline using their deposited responses.

```sh
python "$FIELD/fetch_lawd.py" . "$CACHE"
python "$FIELD/field_archives.py" .
python "$FIELD/reduce_lawd.py" . "$CACHE"
```

The limits apply to point-like sources at the stated passbands, sampled radii and eligible angles, for the tested nearly constant projected offset. The measurement uses archive-calibrated Level 3 pixels. Exposure-level reprocessing, archival images absent from the footprint query, separations below 2″ or above 5″, appreciable orbital curvature and any variability between epochs fall outside these constraints. A weaker or varying source can remain unclassified. No object passed the co-motion selection for discovery validation.
