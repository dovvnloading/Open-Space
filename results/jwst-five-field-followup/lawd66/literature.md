# LAWD 66 publication screen

The target is LAWD 66, L 845−70, WD 1708−147, or LTT 6847. A known microlensed background star is expected within a fraction of an arcsecond. The object in the 2023 HST single-white-dwarf mass measurement was LAWD 37. The accompanying NASA announcement separately identifies the LAWD 66 JWST campaign and its first 2022 observation. [NASA/STScI announcement](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/hubble/for-the-first-time-hubble-directly-measures-mass-of-a-lone-white-dwarf/) · [McGill et al.](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stac3532)

Bramich (2018) gives Gaia DR2 IDs 4139531467491239680 (lens) and 4139531467491232000 (background source), the lens proper motion (278.156, −273.661) mas yr⁻¹, parallax 44.128 mas, and a predicted August 2024 event with G = 20.778 for the source. The 1.37 mas predicted deflection is relevant to sub-mas astrometry. Catalogue positions can shift by much more in unresolved, blended images. [Bramich, Table A.4, event ME30](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2018/10/aa33505-18.pdf)

Farihi, Becklin & Zuckerman (2005) already included WD 1708−147/LTT 6847 in their infrared low-luminosity-companion survey (Table 1). Its listing flags Steward and IRTF observations. Survey-wide limits cannot be assigned to individual radii around LAWD 66. Farihi et al. (2024) report optical carbon features in this specific star and discuss the distinct planetary-mass companion of WD 0806−661. [Farihi et al. 2005](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0506017) · [Farihi et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stae1068)

The target and purpose of JWST GO 2692 are explicit in the Cycle 1 list. Searches on 2026-11-17 used all four aliases with JWST, companion, brown dwarf, infrared imaging and microlensing, plus programs 2692, 4519 and 6584, in the journals, arXiv and STScI pages indexed by web search. No target-specific JWST bound-companion claim was identified in that screen. This is a dated search result; papers using field names or other source identifiers can be missed. A source-by-source positional literature check is separate. [STScI Cycle 1 GO, program 2692](https://www.stsci.edu/jwst/science-execution/approved-programs/general-observers/cycle-1-go)
