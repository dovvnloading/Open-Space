# HD 21997 and HD 202628

HD 21997 has no F444W threshold crossing in the 2.5–5.5 arcsec search annulus. HD 202628 has two, at 3.91 and 4.13 arcsec. Both disappear when the identical science integrations are reprocessed with a different reference star. The primary reference produces a deep negative feature whose shoulders pass the locally background-subtracted matched filter. Neither peak establishes a point source.

| Field | F444W date | Science integrations | Reference dithers | Search coverage | Positive / negative crossings | 95% recovery at 2.65 / 5.35 arcsec |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| HD 21997 | 2024-02-05 | 5 | 9 | 99.67% | 0 / 0 | 1.58 / 0.74 µJy |
| HD 202628 | 2024-06-02 | 9 | 5 | 99.64% | 2 / 8 | 2.48 / 0.93 µJy |

Each field contributes 2,376 single-source injection trials, with six radii, 36 angles and 11 brightnesses. The published parent code uses independent subsets of five F444W standard-star dithers for the detection and injected PSFs, a relative mask-transmission map, small-sample annular thresholds and a negative-peak control. Its explicit injection before reference fitting agrees with the local calculation to better than 2e−13 in amplitude for both targets. The curves in [measurements](measurements/) measure F444W point-source recovery in a single detector visit. The approximate contrast denominator comes from an isophotal blackbody extrapolation from 2MASS Ks (2.159 µm, 666.7 Jy zero point) to 4.433 µm. The input temperatures of 8300 and 5750 K give 0.720 and 1.748 Jy respectively. Stellar SED, variability and disc excess affect the relative contrasts. Absolute flux recovery does not use this denominator. [2MASS calibration](https://www.ipac.caltech.edu/2mass/releases/allsky/doc/sec6_4a.html)

In the alternate-reference experiment, HD 25945 replaces all five HD 190580 dithers. At the two HD 202628 peak pixels, S/N changes from 5.44 to 0.31 and 8.16 to −0.50. Within two detector pixels of either peak there are zero crossings with HD 25945. The absolute residual at each original peak was negative. [Reference test](measurements/HD202628_reference_test.csv)

## Publication screen

The search on 2026-11-17 covered target names, JWST and companion/coronagraph/point-source terms, the GO 2538 and 3989 programs, and the earlier review's object-linked titles. Search coverage depends on indexing and does not establish priority.

- HD 21997 (HIP 16449, HR 1082). Subaru/HiCIAO SEEDS section 4.3.2 reports zero candidates in the January 2013 data. Rameau et al. surveyed dusty systems with VLT/NaCo. The 2026 Spirit of Lyot conference already reported JWST/NIRCam and MIRI disc imaging for HD 21997 with a broad dust distribution. A dust detection or excess cannot establish a point source. Other object-linked studies include the Meshkat et al. debris survey, ISPY, Gemini/GPI polarimetry, the MLOCI methodology, a two-epoch study of β Pic and gas imaging of HD 131835. These entries alone do not identify a companion in this field. [SEEDS](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/153/3/106) · [Rameau](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2013A%26A...553A..60R/abstract) · [Bendahan-West conference program](https://conference.ipac.caltech.edu/SpiritofLyot6/page/agenda) · [ALMA](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.5069)
- HD 202628 (HIP 105184). Ferrer-Chávez et al. 2026, the JWST-related SIMBAD lead 2026ApJ..1010L..19F, mentions HD 202628 only in §7 as an eccentric-ring comparison. Their detected planet orbits RX J0534.0−0221. HST/STIS had already imaged the ring. Faramaz et al. 2019 Table 4 gives ALMA sources S1–S4. S1 was discussed as possible circumplanetary material with a background alternative. Its existence is previously reported and its interpretation was unresolved in that study. ISPY lists this target in Table D.1. An earlier transit-feasibility study considered a background object that the projected disc would cross. [Ferrer-Chávez](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20748) · [HST](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.2078) · [ALMA + HST](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.04162) · [ISPY](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020A%26A...635A.162L/abstract) · [Background transit prediction](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stt2471)

## Public archive and reproduction

The 0.012-degree MAST footprint cones and frozen product-list responses are in [receipts](receipts/). The NIRCam association members explicitly distinguish one deep science visit per field from PSF reference dithers. For HD 21997, a single public F1140C Level 3 mosaic mixes September 2023 and January 2024. The auxiliary search uses separate PSF-subtracted visit cubes before mosaicking. For HD 202628, both F1140C rolls are from June 2023. GO 9828's October 2026 HD 202628 F2550W images have MAST release dates of 2027-10-17 and do not contribute a public epoch to this test.

From the repository root set the variables below to the field directory containing this README, the merged analysis and a writable cache. Use Python with NumPy, SciPy and Astropy.

```sh
field_dir=results/jwst-five-field-followup/hd21997-hd202628
merged=results/jwst-nearby-companions
cache_dir=./jwst-two-field-cache
out_dir=./jwst-two-field-measurements
python "$merged/nircam/nircam_fetch.py" --manifest "$field_dir/inputs.csv" --inputs "$cache_dir"
python "$field_dir/reduce_f444w.py" "$merged" "$cache_dir" "$out_dir" HD21997
python "$field_dir/reduce_f444w.py" "$merged" "$cache_dir" "$out_dir" HD202628
python "$field_dir/check_reference.py" "$merged" "$cache_dir" "$out_dir"
python "$field_dir/auxiliary.py" "$merged" "$cache_dir" "$out_dir"
```

The blind F444W tables treat every threshold crossing as a trigger. Motion comparisons at two wavelengths require an independently recovered centroid and relative registration. A missing counterpart at another wavelength cannot determine motion. Atmospheric grid fits to signed flux proxies on subtraction residuals have no physical interpretation.

## Auxiliary imaging and source checks

The auxiliary search applies the parent MIRI difference of Gaussians, peak finder and subpixel refinement to the F200W KLIP mosaics and the individual F1140C PSF-subtracted rolls. It uses a 6-MAD cut in 0.5 arcsec radial bins, excludes a five-pixel invalid-data neighborhood and excludes locations within 0.37 arcsec of either MIRI quadrant boundary. These thresholds and footprints differ from the primary F444W search. Each HD 21997 MIRI visit searched 58.3 square arcsec. The F200W and F444W footprints cover about 66.5 and 75.2 square arcsec. Counts below refer to detections in individual products; the same detector artifact can recur.

| Field | Auxiliary positive peaks | Result of independent checks |
|---|---|---|
| HD 21997 | 1 F200W, 2 MIRI | The F200W mosaic patch exceeds 200 local MAD but reaches at most 1.53 MAD in two native RDI reductions with different reference stars. Both MIRI peaks are near detector (151,76) at sky positions more than 9 arcsec apart. A feature recurs there in the HD 202628 field. |
| HD 202628 | 10 F200W, 2 MIRI | Nine F200W peaks cluster near the negative reference imprint. All ten reach less than 4.4 MAD with an alternate reference. Peak 10 is 2.5 MAD in that reduction. One MIRI peak recurs at detector (151,76). The other has 6.12 MAD in one roll, with no qualifying peak in the other roll or common-position match at a distinct JWST epoch. Its identity remains open. |

The two motion hypotheses use Gaia DR3 proper motion and parallax with the Earth barycentric ephemeris as an observer approximation. The JWST–Earth parallax difference is below 1 mas for these two stars. Cross-filter relative registration is limited by the adopted mask centers and the absence of a robust grid of tie objects. The expected separation of the two tracks is only 0.003–0.011 arcsec across HD 21997’s date pairs and 0.240 arcsec for HD 202628. No pair from different calendar dates lies within 0.35 arcsec of the same host-relative position. The nearest such pair is 2.52 arcsec apart for HD 21997 and 1.16 arcsec for HD 202628. The two NIRCam filters are simultaneous. - All 17 triggers have forced flux or coverage records. A 296-row solar-metallicity cloud-free Sonora Bobcat grid with free nonnegative scale tests the available co-moving-position fluxes in F200W, F444W and F1140C. Nondetections retain signed flux. The proxy apertures adopt 60% encircled energy and local angular controls, with an added 20% flux-dependent error. They lack position-specific coronagraphic throughput and absolute inter-filter registration. Weak fits cannot assign temperatures, masses or establish a companion. ## HD 202628 MIRI peak in visit 007

At (+1.18,+3.06) arcsec, the fixed-position F1140C flux proxies are 3.04 ± 0.59 and 2.00 ± 0.63 µJy in two same-day rolls. The pipeline response reaches 6.12 and 3.39 local MADs. Odd/even subsets return 5.33/5.19 in the first roll and 3.08/3.83 in the second. Native classical RDI of those science integrations returns 3.66 and 4.25 using the program's HD 201865 reference. Substituting nine HD 22333 dithers gives −0.77 and 1.89 (odd/even −0.86/−0.97 and 1.68/2.13). Some positive flux persists in the second roll, but its significance depends on the subtraction and these rolls cannot test common motion. [Reference and split tests](measurements/MIRI_roll_tests.csv)

For the co-moving prediction in 2024 the F200W/F444W flux proxies are −0.05 ± 0.07 and +0.07 ± 0.04 µJy. At the stationary prediction they are +0.06 ± 0.08 and +0.14 ± 0.05 µJy, below the blind cut. No 2024 centroid distinguishes the tracks. The free-scale Sonora fit at the co-moving position has a minimum weighted residual sum of 9.98 for three bands (best grid point 300 K at log g 3); both the error model and throughput limit its interpretation. At the 2018 ALMA epoch the forced responses are 2.34 MAD for stationary and 3.04 MAD for co-moving, in a 0.89 arcsec beam. These data do not validate either interpretation. ### Injection recovery at the MIRI peak’s separation

At 3.283 arcsec, 28 of 72 equally spaced sky angles satisfy the coverage and model transmission cuts in **both** June 2023 rolls (41 in each roll individually). The first tested incident flux recovered at ≥95% of jointly eligible angles in both rolls was **15 µJy using the designated native RDI reference** and **20 µJy using the alternate reference**. All 28 angles passed at those fluxes. At 10 µJy the respective counts are 9/28 and 0/28. The measured feature’s two aperture proxies (3.04 and 2.00 µJy) are below the reproducible high-completeness regime of either independent native reduction. The tests give sensitivity conditional on the sampled angles at this radius and the empirical source morphology. They do not measure a mass or the probability that the observed feature is real.

The template uses flight observations of the unocculted HD 2811 standard on the same F1140C mask (GO 1536). Ten of the 16 dither positions have enough room for the standard 19.1–27.06 pixel sky annulus under the conservative edge cut and a finite aperture check. All four quadrants contribute. The merged CALSPEC band integrator gives 46.504 mJy from the HD 2811 model and SVO response. The STScI aperture-corrected large-aperture estimates range from 98.3% to 102.2% of that prediction. Each shifted empirical PSF is normalized to incident flux, matched to detector quadrant, and scaled by the pinned spaceKLIP F1140C scalar transmission relative to the standard’s location. At the observed position the roll transmissions are 0.905 and 0.968. Significant PSF-shape variations near quadrant boundaries are a residual uncertainty even after excluding transmissions below 85%. [Standard checks](measurements/F1140C_standard_checks.csv) · [Instrument PSF calibration](https://jwst-docs.stsci.edu/jwst-mid-infrared-instrument/miri-performance/miri-point-spread-functions) · [Photometric calibration](https://jwst-docs.stsci.edu/jwst-mid-infrared-instrument/miri-performance/miri-zero-magnitude-flux-densities-aperture-corrections-and-color-corrections) · [Transmission model](https://github.com/spacetelescope/spaceKLIP/blob/ff2484ea2be71929a3c8404e8b3eca567dc47206/spaceKLIP/resources/transmissions/JWST_MIRI_F1140C_transmission_webbpsf-ext_v2.fits)

At each trial the parent injection routine repeats the local 6-MAD peak search with baseline noise fixed, requires a peak within 0.20 arcsec and excludes pre-existing threshold peaks. Both-roll recovery requires detection at the same sky offset. The 13-pixel-radius template lies outside the native RDI fit annulus. A full 5 µJy injection into every native integration before refitting the references confirms the linear calculation to <2×10⁻¹⁵ MJy/sr in both rolls/reference choices. At the feature’s exact position, the minimum *added* flux needed for detection in both rolls is 7 µJy with HD 201865, and 20 µJy with HD 22333. Adding flux to the already detected position in visit 007 of the pipeline residual cannot qualify as a new recovery. A separate post-KLIP injection branch reaches 27/28 jointly at 10 µJy; its injections bypass the original KLIP processing. ## Limits and pending work

The six-radius contrast curve applies to F444W over 2.5–5.5 arcsec at the six sampled radii. It does not cover the central mask, entire field of view, a fixed companion mass, or joint recovery across all three filters. Deep, independent, same-band astrometry would be required to classify HD 202628’s remaining one-roll MIRI peak. A future F2550W detection could test the position but would still require an astrometric and colour analysis. The companion-level claim remains a scoped null in F444W. HST pixel tests for these same triggers are documented in the [campaign archive section](../archives/).
