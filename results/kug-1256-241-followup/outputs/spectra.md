Searches center on the 2MASX nucleus. A 10-arcsec inventory radius and a 3-arcsec association cut apply when target coordinates are available. Name-indexed NED products can duplicate survey spectra. A DESI stack spans the exposure MJD range recorded in the timeline.

The fit uses a local linear continuum and a common narrow-line velocity and width, with fixed [O III] and [N II] doublet ratios. The alternate model adds one Gaussian Balmer component with FWHM between 1,201 and 14,129 km/s. “Broad favored” requires ΔBIC ≥ 10, integrated-flux signal-to-noise ≥ 5 and an interior broad solution. The formal errors use pixel weights and residual scatter. Stellar absorption, Fe II, aperture differences and flux calibration affect comparisons. An inconclusive fit gives no disappearance limit. Continuum windows are 50 Å wide in the rest frame and include host light. Integrated line fluxes in the CSV use the native flux-density scale times observed Å. Rows on the same night or repeated between NED and a survey can represent the same exposure.

An independent broad-line transition requires distinct epochs with comparable line sensitivity. Catalog spectral types and multiple reductions of one epoch cannot establish one. Searches are limited to responding public holdings.
