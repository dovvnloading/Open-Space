ROSAT and eRASS1 establish the long-baseline soft X-ray change. The XMM slew is intermediate. The catalogue interval for this target's eRASS1 scans falls in December 2019. The Swift catalogue mean covers two sparse visits. Later eROSITA emission establishes continued activity.

The table reports observed 0.2–2.0 keV energy flux in erg cm⁻² s⁻¹. A band projection uses the previous survey's absorbed power law with photon index 2 and column 3 × 10²⁰ cm⁻². It multiplies the released observed energy flux by the ratio of absorbed energy integrals. Instrument response and spectral assumptions of the native catalogue persist. Statistical errors exclude model uncertainty. The X-ray-only measurements cannot separate luminosity and absorption changes.
