## Coverage and selection

The public ALeRCE TAP archive returned 41,630 broker object IDs with at least two detections and first catalogued detection in the 16-day UTC window. All 16 per-discovery-date CSV bodies matched an independent COUNT query. Their 90,769 rows have unique candids. The local screen retained 75,719 positive detections with uncertainty at most 2.5 / (5 ln 10) mag. There were 33,962 objects with at least two such measurements, and 389 had a qualifying same-filter time interval, yielding 923 pairs before applying significance, amplitude and slope cuts. All 1,020 distinct time/filter visits used by these 923 pairs were confirmed to be program 1. The forced archive supplied IDs on 885 visits. Original packets supplied them on the remaining 135. Every one of the 18 measurements making up the nine selected pairs is individually identified as ZTF public program 1 in its original alert packet, including four historical rows. The scope is the broker-new object population. An earlier negative detection, an older broker ID associated to a new physical event, a delayed broker ingestion, or a failure of the alert pipeline can remove an event.

The Fink monthly statistics reply contains 2,812,876 raw alerts, 1,580,395 science alerts and 3,064 exposures in the frozen interval, all dated November 2–12 UTC. These counts include established variables and Solar System alerts. They are independent context for coverage. Date-specific broker counts and calendar duration cannot determine effective area-time.

## Follow-up evidence

The nine objects have 35 ALeRCE detection rows, 195 archived ZTF forced measurements and 410 upper limits. The Fink object endpoint also returned light curves and selected image arrays where available. The archive's forced measurements come from alert-packet histories. Fresh forced photometry on all public images at arbitrary positions is a different service. The signal history, context, limits and forced measurements are in data/ztf/*_followup.csv.gz. For all nine positions, the archive cone search across neighboring object IDs returned zero positive S/N ≥ 5 detection rows between 365 and 30 days before catalog discovery. The cone radius is three arcseconds around object means. For the four targets with a pipeline history older than one year an additional search before that year returned 2 and 11 rows for ZTF26acfplkt and ZTF26acheiuo respectively, and zero for the remaining two. Older pipeline history timestamps can include either difference sign.

The 14 selected epochs with original image stamps from ALeRCE are shown in derived/ztf_stamps_1.jpg and derived/ztf_stamps_2.jpg. The other four measurements were available in a later alert's historical array, without that epoch's images. Crosshairs locate stamp centers. PNGs are displayed with the broker's own per-image stretch. PS1 photometry and star/galaxy scores, alert-embedded Gaia and MPC fields, independent Gaia DR3 and SIMBAD cone results and original packet responses are retained. Satellite-orbit clearance and a forced-photometry reconstruction of total outburst duration were not performed. None of the nine had sufficient evidence of an extragalactic origin to promote for multi-messenger interpretation.

## Reproduction

Run scripts/ztf_tap.py, scripts/ztf_screen.py, scripts/ztf_visits.py, scripts/ztf_coverage.py, scripts/ztf_followup.py, scripts/ztf_packets.py, scripts/ztf_contact.py, scripts/ztf_catalogs.py and scripts/ztf_prior.py with Python 3.10 or newer. Only the contact sheet requires Pillow. scripts/ztf_rubin_cone.py checks the separate Rubin follow-up position against ZTF objects, detections, packet forced photometry and limits. The TAP counts, query URLs, retrieval dates, statuses and SHA-256 values of original uncompressed replies are preserved under receipts/ztf/ **inside** ztf_evidence_supplement.tar.gz; receipts/ztf is not a separately tracked directory. The program-visit packet receipts also record their local response paths.
