## Trigger associations

**No optical position has a supported association with a localized event in this checked set.** Two ZTF objects fall in the coarse Fermi/GBM map of the same burst, GRB 261108A. At each optical position the integrated posterior mass in higher-density pixels is about 28%. Their separations from the independent SVOM/ECLAIRs localization are 13.460° and 13.463°, compared with its 0.080° 90% radius. Both lie far outside the SVOM region. The optical pairs start 1.14 and 2.12 days after the burst. [GBM map and report](https://gcn.nasa.gov/circulars/46185) · [SVOM detection](https://gcn.nasa.gov/circulars/46186)

`context_receipts.json` records input sizes and hashes. To regenerate the sky-time comparison with `numpy`, `astropy` and `astropy-healpix`, run `python match_triggers.py PATH_TO_RUBIN_PAIRS_CSV PATH_TO_ZTF_PAIRS_CSV`. The program downloads the referenced GBM maps to `map-cache/`. `gcn_inventory.py` rebuilds the index and `radio_context.py` accepts the same two pair paths to repeat the RACS search. The coverage is conditional on the cited localizations and lag interval. The comparison does not include GBM sub-threshold events, a complete EP notice stream, directional satellite sensitivity or contemporaneous forced radio measurements. It cannot establish the absence of prompt emission or the viewing angle of a jet. A γ-ray, optical, X-ray, radio, neutrino or GW counterpart outside these catalogues or time windows remains untested.
