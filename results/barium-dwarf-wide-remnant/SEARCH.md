# Archival investigation

The 249-day barium binary Gaia DR3 5707071630039205248 has a resolved neighbor, Gaia 5707071698757576576. The primary's AstroSpectroSB1 solution and the faint object's astrometry are consistent with common motion. This experiment tests the tertiary interpretation against the full enriched-star cohort, the white-dwarf classification, the original sample selection and alternate identifications.
