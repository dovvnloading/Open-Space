# Optical shoreline elevations at the Pool Malebo outlet

Sentinel-2B classified the same ground pixels in the Congo reach downstream of the outlet (SWORD 13217000061) as bare/nonvegetated surface on 13 July 2024 and water on 5 January 2024. Within the fixed primary support, 5,289 downstream pixels met this rule, compared with 82 in upstream reach 13217000091. The two scenes show changes in inundation. They do not identify bare rock lithology or the time of peak river stage.

The January and July shoreline positions on the downstream reach bound a median 3.50 m of historic Copernicus DSM relief across 13 bank-side segments (interquartile range 2.53–4.75 m). The upstream reach yielded only one segment and 4.78 m, with zero qualifying patches under the 40 m margin sensitivity. Shifting the DSM by 20 m in cardinal directions gives downstream median relief of 2.69–3.64 m and upstream 3.08–5.60 m. On January 17, a separate Sentinel-2A acquisition yielded three downstream segments with 1.09 m median relief and two upstream with 3.30 m. The primary scenes therefore cannot independently substantiate a larger downstream stage rise. Support is too uneven to infer a falsifying contrast either. Relief within an inundated strip is not a direct water-level difference. The July and January passes also differ from the exact SWOT maximum and minimum dates.

Both scenes were analysed on a 20 m UTM 33S grid within 1 km of the original SWORD v16 line, excluding pixels whose nearest point is an endpoint. To limit the steep along-channel gradient, the line was subdivided into fixed 1 km segments with separate sides assigned from the nearest local centerline tangent. Within each segment-side, only pixels classified as clear (SCL 4, 5, 6) at both times were eligible. A flood strip consists of eight-connected pixels of July SCL 5 (bare/nonvegetated) and January SCL 6 (water), omitting historical JRC Landsat water occurrence ≥90% and nodata. Responding strips are not automatically hydraulic main-channel shorelines. They can include small branches and bars. The lower shoreline comparison uses strip pixels within 20 m of confirmed water at both dates. The upper shoreline comparison uses strip pixels within 20 m of confirmed land at both dates. Pixels belonging to both edges are excluded. A patch must include at least three pixels on each edge. On the same patch, median DSM elevation at the outer edge minus the median at the inner edge gives an estimate of exposed bank relief. First take the median across qualifying patches within each segment-side, then the median across segment-sides with observations. Segment-side counts are spatial support, not independent statistical replicates. Cloud cover, mixed pixels, adjacency, 20 m class boundaries, true channel changes, DSM vegetation, and past hydroflattening can shift the result. The published global point-to-point vertical accuracy is <2 m for slopes ≤20% and <4 m for steeper slopes at 90% confidence. It is not a site-specific uncertainty. The DSM was measured principally from 2011–2015. `grid_*.tif` is generated by the preparation command and contains five aligned bands for local replay. In order these are January SCL, July SCL, GLO-30 EGM2008 surface elevation (metres), JRC water occurrence (%), and segment-side integer identifiers (0 = outside). `shoreline_summary.csv` and `primary_patches.csv` are recomputed by `python shoreline.py analyze` with NumPy, SciPy and Python GDAL. For regeneration from the original sources, save the two primary SCL GeoTIFFs and DEM in `sources.json`. Crop the JRC layer with `gdal_translate -projwin 15.06 -4.19 15.36 -4.43 /vsicurl/JRC_URL occurrence.tif` using the URL therein. Run `python shoreline.py prepare JAN_SCL JUL_SCL DEM occurrence.tif`. `python scene_check.py SCL_DIRECTORY` computes January cloud-coverage and alternate-scene relief against the same July scene. The provided SWORD geometry contains the two original v16 lines. The initial January scene supplies common clear coverage of 61.37% on the downstream corridor and 32.46% on the upstream corridor. Other sampled January scenes provide at most 55.16% coverage on the upstream reach, and the maximum number of qualifying upstream segments on any date was two.
