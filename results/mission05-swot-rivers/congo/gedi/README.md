# GEDI lidar at the Djoué rapids and the Malebo outlet

We tested whether an independent spaceborne laser captured the change in water-level difference inferred from SWOT. NASA's GEDI02_A V002 catalog returned six granules overlapping the two reaches during the SoS V3 observation interval. Five had laser shots within the fixed river corridor. Among 936 shots within 450 m of a centerline, 517 fell on pixels classified as water in both January and July 2024. **All 517 failed GEDI's Level 2 quality flag.** Their waveform lowest-mode elevations spanned 198.87 to 13,228.91 m relative to the ellipsoid. Elevations of high clouds are plausible for some extreme returns. On 9 May the service reported read failures for beams 8 and 11. The remaining five granules returned no beam read error. The result applies to the available observations. GEDI was in storage on the ISS from March 2023 to April 2024, including the January high-water event. [NASA product](https://doi.org/10.5067/GEDI/GEDI02_A.002), [instrument storage history](https://daac.ornl.gov/GEDI/guides/GEDI_L3_LandSurface_Metrics_V3.html).

Earlier good-quality tracks confirm a steep downstream reach at the locations they sampled. On **20 July 2019**, 16 interior-water shots on six beams covered river kilometers 4.35 to 8.78 using beam median positions measured upstream from the lower reach's downstream end. The corresponding endpoint beam median elevations were 246.45 and 250.68 m relative to the ellipsoid. The median of the 15 pairwise slopes through beam medians was 0.96 m/km in the upstream direction. This is a spatial slope over the sampled 4.43 km in ellipsoidal height. Its conversion to a gravity-referenced water-surface slope would require a geoid correction. The individual beam positions, elevations and observation counts are in `accepted-beam-medians.csv`. The within-pass sequence corroborates a downstream fall predating SWOT. [GEDI product and vertical reference](https://doi.org/10.5067/GEDI/GEDI02_A.002).

On **13 February 2020**, two accepted shots on one beam sampled the upstream end of the lower reach at km 11.10. Thirty-seven shots on six beams sampled the upper reach at km 0.81–4.33. Their medians were 262.09 m and 269.52 m on different spatial supports. On 27 June 2020, three shots sampled only the lower reach. None of these observations provides simultaneous high- and low-stage profiles at both 2024 SWOT reach centers. A published hydrometric assessment places the continuous Brazzaville and Kinshasa staff gauges above the Djoué rapids. The public Office Congolais des Eaux demonstration station explicitly uses fictional numbers. We found no open continuous ground-stage series at the lower reach center. [Gauging site description](https://doi.org/10.1080/27678490.2022.2082338), [OCE page](https://oce.cd/donnees-hydrologiques/DEMO-KIN).

## Selection and geolocation

The CMR query spanned 1 April 2019 to 2 May 2025 over 15.14–15.32°E, 4.41–4.27°S. We requested each returned V002 granule separately from SlideRule's public GEDI L2A service, using the same polygon without a server-side quality filter. The inventory contained 54 granules with 74,814 returned shots. Distances and along-channel coordinates come from nearest-point projection onto each segment of the two SWORD v16 centerlines in WGS 84 / UTM zone 33S. We assigned shots to the closer reach and retained 16,468 shots within 1.5 km, preserving their raw quality, beam, solar elevation, lowest/highest return heights and exact times. Centerlines are the source shapefile for Africa hydrobasin 13 subset to the two stated reaches. [SWORD v16](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10013982), [SlideRule GEDI](https://docs.slideruleearth.io/api-reference/gedi).

The primary channel screen uses a 450 m centerline radius. For each shot we sampled the Sentinel-2B Level-2A scene-classification band at the native 20 m grid on 5 January and 13 July 2024. Both central pixels must be water, code 6. The final historical profile further requires a full 3×3 square of water on both dates, reducing potential land mixtures across a nominal 25 m GEDI footprint. The historical classification is a fixed dual-season water mask from 2024. Earlier channel migration and water level remain sources of uncertainty. GPS geolocation, cross-channel differences, optical classification errors and vegetation/whitewater are additional limitations. We require GEDI `surface_flag=1`, `quality_flag=1`, `degrade_flag=0` (SlideRule packed flags exactly 130). Surface screening uses a DEM consistency test; quality filtering over water can discard valid specular returns. We did not select points for agreement with SWOT. Of the 1,874 central-water shots inside 450 m over the full search, 62 passed the packed flags and 58 also passed the 3×3 interior-water requirement. [GEDI product guide](https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/988/GEDI02_User_Guide_V2.pdf), [packed flags](https://github.com/SlideRuleEarth/sliderule/blob/v5.8.1/apps/node/datasets/gedi/package/GediParameters.h), [Level 2A reader](https://github.com/SlideRuleEarth/sliderule/blob/v5.8.1/apps/node/datasets/gedi/package/Gedi02aReader.cpp).

## Files and reproduction

- `cmr-granules.json` preserves the full CMR catalog response with query URL, timestamps, granule IDs and original NASA HDF5 URLs. `granule-manifest.csv` links each record to the local SlideRule response hash, counts and beam-read failures. The server attaches per-request messages to its streamed output, so raw byte hashes can differ on refetch. A date with no nearby shots has no row in `screen.csv` and still appears in the manifest.
- `gedi-corridor-shots.csv.gz` contains all corridor measurements at up to 1.5 km, including exclusions, the optical labels and original GEDI shot numbers. The SHA-256 is `cf3dbb45f6c18a1db3e5dfbed75477f966d5bf5fa12afe0126a51d07391b8716`. The full service binary responses and NASA HDF5 orbits can be fetched with the archived identifiers. The EPSG:7912/ITRF2014 GEDI horizontal coordinates are used as WGS 84 geographic coordinates for meter-scale reach assignment. All reported GEDI elevations use the native ellipsoid. No absolute GEDI–SWOT elevation subtraction was performed.
- `optical-sources.json` records both full source-asset URLs, exact acquisition times and original file hashes. `centerlines.geojson`, `sliderule-version.json` and `sliderule-record-layout.json` pin the geometry, service build and binary schema.
- `python summarize.py` rebuilds `screen.csv` and `accepted-beam-medians.csv` directly from the archived extract with the standard library. `python prepare.py cache --fetch` retrieves inputs and rebuilds the extract with Python, NumPy and GDAL. SlideRule endpoint requests are defined in `prepare.py`. The code records beam read failures. Re-running after transient HTTP errors will reuse previously downloaded files. The analyses assign dates from each shot's UTC timestamp.
