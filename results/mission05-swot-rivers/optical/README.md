# Upper Zambezi optical water extent

We measured open water in the 20 m Sentinel-2 Level-2A scene classification layer on two SWORD v16 gauge reaches. Within each reach, each image uses the same centerline and buffer. A pixel classified as water (SCL 6) contributes 400 m². Vegetation and bare land (classes 4 and 5) also count toward clear coverage. The extent interval assigns every other pixel first to land, then to water. It measures the effect of missing coverage. Misclassification on clear pixels and inundation under vegetation remain unresolved. This corridor area has different spatial support from the RiverSP equivalent river width. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data, 2023–2025. [Scene classification layer](https://s2.pages.eopf.copernicus.eu/msi/s2msi/1.3.0/PDFS_ADFS/L2/PDFS_S2_MSI_L2.html)

We selected the two dates with least tile-level cloud cover in each calendar month from July 2023 through April 2025, then screened cloud locally. All 88 scenes, asset URLs, source raster SHA-256, acquisition identifiers, processing baselines, clear fractions and pixel counts are retained. A local threshold of 98% clear admitted 19 of 44 scenes at Chavuma and 22 of 44 at Nana’s Farm. Dates were selected using tile metadata before inspecting local water classes. [Scene inventory and measurements](scenes.csv)

At Chavuma both April 2024 scenes passed. The 22 April interval was 4.483–4.955 km². April 2025 had no primary pass. On 27 April 2025, a sensitivity observation with 97.67% clear coverage gave 5.158–5.749 km². Even allocating every unclear 2024 pixel to water leaves at least 0.204 km² more classified open water on the later date. The two dates have different processing baselines and a five-day calendar offset. This comparison supports the independently gauged seasonal recovery. At Nana’s Farm the strictly accepted 22 March 2024 and 22 March 2025 intervals overlap (6.857–7.502 and 7.254–7.866 km²).

From the mission directory run `python optical/measure.py /path/to/sentinel-cache` with Python, GDAL and NumPy. The cache stores original imagery outside the repository. The Earth Search collection is `sentinel-2-l2a`. [Catalog provider and dataset](https://github.com/Element84/earth-search)
