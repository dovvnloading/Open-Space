# RiverSP processing and location checks

The reach and node CSVs contain the PO.DAAC Hydrocron measurement fields, provider units and original RiverSP granule identifiers. The v2.0 and D files draw on the same satellite observations. Comparisons between releases test sensitivity to processing. Each filename uses the SWORD v16 ID, and its data column gives the ID queried in that release. Eight of the nine reach IDs changed. The official crosswalk maps every target one-to-one. The two measurement manifests retain query URLs, HTTP response and decoded CSV hashes, dates and units.

Run `python compare_releases.py` and `python node_checks.py` in this directory with pandas and NumPy. The scripts use the checked-in measurements offline. `fetch_geom.py` retrieves the reach measurements by default. Provide the v16 node IDs in the node manifest to retrieve the node measurements. `crosswalk.py` verifies the archived Africa translation member CRC and checks the IDs. Provide the same v16 node IDs for the node variant. Site selection used PO.DAAC's v16 Feature Translation Service. The selected provider descriptors and query URLs are in `sword_v16_study_reaches.json`.

For the release comparison we require reach quality ≤1, a complete reach (partial_f=0), finite valid height, width and slope with valid uncertainties, and an observed-node fraction of at least 0.80 or 0.70. Each release has one row per cycle/pass; matched comparisons require eligibility in both. At Diré center the D product commonly reports 46/58 nodes. The 0.80 cutoff removes every central D observation, and the 0.70 sensitivity admits 98. On the 45 co-observed eligible orbits at 0.70, median absolute D minus v2.0 differences are 0.0184 m in height, 25.8 m in width and 0.131 cm/km in slope. The downstream neighbor has a median width difference of −115 m. These comparisons keep the releases separate through time.

Three v16 nodes at quarter, half and three-quarter index positions in each Diré reach check whether changing spatial coverage drives the December 2024 high stage. The center nodes in Mopti and Ké-Macina give additional track diagnostics. All 11 official v16-to-v17b node correspondences have shift and boundary flags of zero. These are distinct nodes on the same instrument. Computed distances from observed node coordinate to the mapped v17b prior use a local equirectangular approximation. The provider also supplies node_dist, cross-track distance, node quality and uncertainties.

Eight of nine Diré nodes had node quality ≤1 on December 8. Median upstream, central and downstream elevations were 262.6303, 262.5027 and 262.4025 m. On December 29 the medians were 262.5682, 262.4909 and 262.4057 m, with eight eligible nodes. December 15 had three eligible nodes. The December 8 maximum observed coordinate displacement from the prior among the eight was 203 m. Local slope differences can remain below resolution despite coherent stage. The tables retain all event flags and the track-specific median node-minus-reach differences.

Products and definitions are documented by [PO.DAAC Hydrocron](https://podaac.github.io/hydrocron/user-guide/versioning/), [SWORD v16](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10013982), and the [official v17b-to-v16 translation](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22259077).
