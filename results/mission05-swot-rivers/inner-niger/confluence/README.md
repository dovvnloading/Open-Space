# Mopti confluence discharge check

RiverSP v2 gives two incoming reaches for the downstream reach 14279000471. These are western arm 14291000011 and eastern arm 14281000011. Each arm independently lists 14279000471 as its sole downstream neighbour. The provider assigns the same river name ("Niger River") to both arms. The calculation uses reach identifiers and arm orientation. The topology extracts and their URLs and hashes are in this directory.

From the principal nine-reach CSV directory, `python closure.py /path/to/csv-directory` verifies the links and reproduces the joined measurements. For each cycle and pass it requires good or suspect reach quality, full reach flag, observed-node fraction at least 0.80, crossover quality at most one and positive L4 consensus for all three reaches. Of 32 common eligible overpasses, 19 have all three discharge estimates, on 13 cycles between August 3, 2023 and September 2, 2024. The three time stamps differ by at most one second within an overpass.

The sum of the two upstream estimates minus the downstream estimate, divided by the downstream estimate, has median −0.110. The median absolute imbalance is 0.322, with individual signed fractions from −0.843 to +1.775. These values describe consistency of the three model estimates. The estimates are reach averages and derive from the same satellite passages. Temporary storage, exchange with side channels and flow outside mapped channel banks can also affect the comparison. Complete triples cease before the 2024 flood crest, so this diagnostic cannot quantify storage losses or flood routing during the crest.
