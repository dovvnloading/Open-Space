# Historical nadir-altimetry comparison

Dated July 22 and January 26 targets match the two SWOT visits in the 2024 flood year. Use the same calendar targets every year and the published observations only. At each date take the nearest observed height within 15 days inclusive, preferring the earlier date in a tie. Require finite height and reported uncertainty of at most 0.30 m. Nominal close-stage matches require absolute January-minus-July stage change at Mopti at most 0.20 m, as in the reach analysis. Report all raw pairings and counts, plus 0.50 m stage-match and 0.15 m uncertainty sensitivity. Retain date mismatch across sites and repeat excluding comparisons whose respective endpoint dates differ by over seven days. An additional contrast uses the four dates' signed differences, downstream minus upstream.

Candidate primary stations are drawn before comparing any seasonal contrasts from the DAHITI Mali virtual station index. Require a public Niger River height series within 60 km of each of the two SWOT central reach centroids, continuous satellite-era record beginning prior to 2019 and ending after January 2026. Rank qualifying sites by distance. Keep every qualifying station pair as a sensitivity, including the previously identified 13763 downstream station. The observation months and stage criteria can leave insufficient close-stage matches to rank the event; report that outcome with denominators. Seasonal samples within a pair are ranked empirically. Different station pairs can share reaches, satellite passes and dates; their results cannot be pooled as independent years.
