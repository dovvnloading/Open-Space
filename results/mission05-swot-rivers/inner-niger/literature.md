# Prior reports and measurement novelty

Publications and operational bulletins were searched in English and French on 16 November 2026. The entries below give representative exact search strings and the retrieved primary references. The search covered public full-text web indexes, publisher records, IRD, PO.DAAC, OPIDIN, Mali Météo and DNH materials. Unindexed hydrometric archives, theses and local bulletins limit any assertion of worldwide priority.
