# Sélingué Reservoir

The NASA/USDA Global Water Measurements series is an independent Sentinel-3A nadir altimeter record at 11.288° N, 8.449° W. Pass 743 samples the reservoir every 27 days. The full provider text includes data definitions, source flags, reference pass, EGM2008 transformation, error estimates and all missing-value codes. EGM2008 heights are in column 15 and the uncertainty of the relative height is in column 7. The site metadata and an SHA-256 digest are in the manifest.

| Calendar year | Highest observed EGM2008 level, m | Observation date | Reported elevation error, m |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| 2023 | 349.26 | 2023-11-18 | 0.043 |
| 2024 | 349.38 | 2024-11-03 | 0.042 |
| 2025 | 349.33 | 2025-12-13 | 0.043 |

Thirteen, fourteen and thirteen passes returned valid heights in these years respectively. The difference between the 2024 and 2023 sampled maxima is 0.12 m. At 27-day cadence a true peak or a short operation can fall between passes. NASA records a narrow crossing far from the dam and compromised DEM-assisted data capture at this target. The levels establish the seasonal filling cycle. Release attribution requires outflow and routing data.

Lake/reservoir surface elevation products are courtesy of NASA and USDA/FAS. [Sélingué product](https://earth.gsfc.nasa.gov/gwm/lake/monthly/535) · [Target-specific advisories](https://earth.gsfc.nasa.gov/gwm/html/WATER-MONITOR.LakesReservoirs.35day.pdf).

The regional hydrological year review records that Sélingué reached its prescribed 349 m IGM filling level on October 24, 2024 and October 30, 2025. These dates corroborate the filling phase observed by Sentinel-3A. This gauge uses a different vertical reference.
