# Independent laser heights at the Malebo Pool outlet

On 13 March 2024 the ICESat-2 ATL13 median water-surface elevations were 269.713 m and 257.771 m near two SWORD nodes, giving a drop of **11.941 m**. The nearest quality-screened SWOT visit, 10 March on pass 111, measured 269.651 m and 257.759 m, a drop of **11.891 m**. The 0.050 m difference is descriptive. The sites sample different footprints, the visits are three days apart and the water level can evolve between them. These two nodes are closer together than the reach centers used for the January–July comparison.

The downstream result uses 19 laser segments from two beams within 1 km of SWOT node 13217000060451, with a median separation of 0.341 km. The upstream result uses 41 segments from two beams at node 13217000090231, with a median separation of 0.860 km. The nearest upstream segment is 0.527 km away, so the two-end comparison disappears with a 0.5 km radius. We also found same-day corroboration at two upstream nodes on 13 December 2023 and one five-day upstream comparison in January 2025. No laser visit directly recovers both endpoints at both 2024 extremes. The reported API elevations offer no segment-level uncertainty or quality flags here. Beam counts and height ranges convey sampling support. [Matched elevations](node_matches.csv)

We searched CMR for ATL03 v007 ground tracks intersecting 15.13–15.32° E and 4.44–4.25° S from 1 October 2018 through 31 October 2026. The local ATL03 file supplies the date and reference ground track. ATL13 granules can package multiple orbits, so their filename reference track can differ from the one crossing this reach. For 85 local track/date combinations we queried NASA OpenAltimetry's ATL13 endpoint. Thirty-three returned beam series, including nine of the 20 queries inside the July 2023–April 2025 SWOT study interval. For each date and each of six SWOT nodes we chose the closest eligible SWOT date within seven days, then required at least three laser segments within the stated radius. Node flags 0/1, positive WSE uncertainty ≤0.30 m and valid WSE were required. The point table uses the API's elevation as delivered. The differential comparison removes any constant datum offset.
