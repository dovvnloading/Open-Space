# The ultraviolet measurement favors a luminous companion in UCAC4 720-033289

GALEX AIS measured **NUV = 19.690 ± 0.134 AB** in 110 seconds. The catalogue supplies zero FUV exposure. The optical-to-infrared fits predicted the observed brightness much more closely when they included one ordinary companion. The single cool giant underpredicted the ultraviolet flux by factors of 12–30 at the model medians. These predictions were obtained without using the UV measurement.

Entries give predicted NUV AB magnitudes, with 16th and 84th percentiles in brackets. All three spectroscopic assumptions use the same photometry, mass function, MIST tracks and GALEX measurement. They probe sensitivity to the giant's atmospheric parameters.

**Extinction controls the interpretation.** Above the atmosphere the MIST companion models have substantially more UV flux, although the hot component also has a higher *effective* NUV attenuation for the same dust column. Matching the measurement at the model medians would require NUV attenuation to decrease by 2.73–3.68 mag for the zero-light models or increase by 0.28–0.50 mag for the one-dwarf models. A sensitivity experiment scaling the modeled effective UV attenuation up or down by 20% shifts the predictions appreciably. Even with attenuation reduced 20%, the zero-light medians are 21.45–22.46. The one-dwarf medians span 17.90–20.70 under the same two perturbations. The perturbation is illustrative. No sightline-specific UV extinction curve or empirical upper limit on an additional UV emitter is available. The GALEX NUV band includes the 2175 Å dust feature, whose strength can vary. A UV-bright unresolved source, chromospheric emission, or a different spectral model can affect this test. [Extinction study](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/449/4/3867/1167095) · [MIST bolometric corrections](https://mist.science/BC_tables/v1/GALEX.txz)

**Association and blending.** The GALEX centroid lies 0.74 arcsec from the approximate epoch-2007 Gaia position. The 60-arcsec Gaia query returns no other source inside 8 arcsec. The closest catalogue neighbor is 19.61 arcsec away with G = 18.08. The GALEX 6-arcmin catalogue search returns 67 AIS detections and only one object ID inside 8 arcsec. There are zero additional artifact-free AIS detections outside that radius at NUV ≤ 19.824. A single-sided 95% Poisson upper bound gives 2.996 sources in that annulus, or 7.36 × 10⁻⁶ sources per square arcsec, conditional on uniform surface density and coverage. Corresponding chance-coincidence upper bounds are 0.0013% within 0.74 arcsec, 0.083% within 6 arcsec, and 0.148% within 8 arcsec. Crowding, unresolved components, position-dependent dust, and the GALEX detector response at 0.488° field radius limit this local estimate. The Gaia neighbor check does not resolve the binary. **Calculation.** We draw 20,000 weighted six-band MIST configurations per spectroscopic-input/companion model from the same evolutionary grid and weights as the Gaia–2MASS analysis. Conditional draws of optical A_V and distance modulus retain their covariance. Stellar current masses, log g, and effective temperatures give bolometric luminosities; MIST GALEX bolometric corrections tabulated at R_V = 3.1 convert each component to the AB system at its inferred A_V. The calculation interpolates each coeval companion along the same hydrogen-burning track as the optical fits and sums fluxes. Calibration and atmosphere systematics in the optical extinction coefficients and NUV corrections are additional to the tabulated intervals. No statistical evidence ratio or unique identification of the UV emitter is claimed. [Prediction quantiles and hashes](nuv_predictions.json)
