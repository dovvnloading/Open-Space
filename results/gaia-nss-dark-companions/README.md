# HD 264291 has a wide companion with a conditional mass near 1.77 solar masses

Gaia DR3 3378588057203660160 (HD 264291) has a joint period of 1,007 days. A fit to the Gaia DR3 orbital covariance and 100 quality-selected LAMOST DR11 medium-resolution exposures on 18 nights gives a companion mass of **1.77 [1.71, 1.84] solar masses** (central 68% conditional interval). The nightly RV residual RMS is 0.69 km/s. The mass assumes that the measured lines trace the primary, adopts the IsocLum primary mass, and identifies its orbit with the photocentre. An unresolved pair of ordinary stars remains a viable explanation. This packet designates the system a follow-up target. Its status as a previously unpublished compact-object candidate remains open.

The initial Gaia screen covered 345,265 stars, including 188,638 with an applicable IsocLum primary mass. It selected 983 unique stars above a nominal 1.4 solar masses, of which 112 exceeded 3 solar masses. LAMOST DR11 v2.0 matched 95 stars in the combined target/control list, and 23 had clean RVs from multiple nights. All five above the 3-solar-mass screen with clean multi-night LAMOST coverage already appeared in the Jayasinghe et al. catalogue. A second pass with FLAME stellar-track masses adds 6,750 stars above 1.4 solar masses and 271 to the above-3 stratum. Survey validation of that extension is in progress.
