# Prior identifications and limits of the novelty check

The exact-ID audit found that 1,627 of 7,733 selected stars occur in at least one of the ingested tables. Some of those tables are parent samples that include rejected targets. Their presence prevents treating the entire intersection as new. Absence from those tables is an unresolved novelty status. [Initial matches](prior_matches.csv) · [Extension matches](../extension/novelty/prior_matches.csv) · [Table counts, hashes and source URLs](inputs.json)

We included the 24 Andrews et al. astrometric candidates, the 177 high-probability Shahaf systems, both high-mass tables in the Gaia DR3 multiplicity paper, the Gaia ellipsoidal-variability table, Jayasinghe et al.'s 234-star SB1 screening table, the 4,641 Andrew et al. systems, El-Badry et al.'s NS sample and follow-up dispositions, Stewart et al.'s observing list, the two full printed El-Badry et al. 2026 candidate lists, and the two published Müller-Horn et al. parent catalogues. We also included the identified tables from the 2026 LAMOST and cluster studies and the printed portion of Li et al.'s field study. The executable table definitions specify the publication version, selector and identifier column. [Crossmatch script](crossmatch.py)

Li et al. (October 2026) jointly fit 1,835 Gaia astrometric binaries using archival RVs. We found five IDs in the printed first table and eight in the printed second table. The arXiv source package contains the manuscript and figures. Searches of the paper, DataCite, Crossref and OpenAlex did not locate the complete machine-readable tables at the time of this analysis. HD 264291 meets its published mass threshold and has archival LAMOST observations, so overlap is a material possibility. Its Gaia DR3 ID, HD, TIC, 2MASS, TYC and LAMOST aliases were also checked against SIMBAD and the published literature. SIMBAD records earlier work on its chemically peculiar spectrum and on time-domain observations. A first compact-object identification for HD 264291 cannot be claimed from this audit. [Li et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34715) · [Target bibliography](../orbit/hd264291_bibliography.csv)

The audit uses DR3 identifiers when the authors publish them. A table limited to printed IDs, sources known under other names, unpublished selections and body text outside the selected tables can escape an exact-ID join. Follow-up of any surviving target requires its aliases, sky position, spectra and the electronic catalogues. [Selected follow-up identifications](../orbit/candidate_identifications.csv)
