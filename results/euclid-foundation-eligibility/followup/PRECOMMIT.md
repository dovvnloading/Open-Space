# Frozen pilot specification

Three mosaic IDs were selected lexically from the earlier SIA science inventory before opening detector layering or pixel products: 102158276_013663548_VIS, 102158276_028106832_VIS, 102158276_343226670_VIS. The first two are development observations; the third is reserved as a holdout and shall not be inspected before a candidate, location and threshold are fixed. The point is (273.1670, 64.3687) degrees ICRS. Query science, RMS and flag cutouts with size 30 arcseconds. Require disjoint input exposure identifiers at the measured position, positive finite RMS, no flag in the source aperture, and sufficient coverage. This location is an extended galaxy in a public tutorial, so the initial question is whether stable aperture fluxes and sky controls are reproducible, not whether a point-source transient exists. A proper source search needs a separate control-calibrated threshold, source definition, masks and spatially distributed controls. Do not promote an anomaly from this test without those controls.
Development eligibility freeze: use the first visit only to select 4-pixel-radius aperture centers from local maxima exceeding 8 times its per-pixel RMS; require 10 pixels from image boundary, zero flag and positive finite RMS throughout the aperture, and at least 10 pixels separation, brightest peaks first. No holdout pixels will be accessed for selection. Count is an observational eligibility denominator, not a claim of astronomical sources: extended galaxies may supply multiple peaks. No science variability threshold or candidate test is authorized by this count.

## Revised development experiment, frozen before expanded cutout access

Use a 60 by 60 arcsecond region centered at the same coordinate, first two ordered visits only. The accepted pixel mask allows only VIS bits STARSIGNAL (18) and OBJECTS (24); all other set bits reject, with finite science and finite positive RMS. Select source centers in visit one at local maxima exceeding 8 times RMS, at least 10 pixels from the edge and each other, with every radius-4 aperture pixel acceptable. Source count and cross-visit aperture recovery are eligibility measures, not a discovery. Initial two-visit variability threshold is |difference minus control median| > 5 times empirical robust dispersion of matched control differences and at least 3 times propagated formal aperture error, with at least 10 eligible matched controls per target. This is a screening threshold, not a calibrated family-wise error rate. Use no third-visit pixels unless a target and expected sign are frozen first. Compare aperture fluxes after a common photometric scale derived from eligible objects, with local sky annulus and explicit PSF caveat; no strong claim without shape matching and blank sky controls.
