# V6 frozen two-visit compact-source development test

Reuse the V5 frozen 269 first-visit center list and identical development images and WCS assertion; third visit remains held out. This is a new development search after observing that V5 selected straight streaks and conditioned on positive visit-two flux, so significance and false-alarm rate are not independently calibrated. Before any V6 measurements, select compact sources using the first visit only: radius-four aperture positive residual moments after local median sky, eigenvalue ratio at most 3 (axis ratio at most sqrt(3)), and positive flux. This morphology threshold is a development choice, not a calibrated point-source classifier. Every source with valid second-visit aperture and annulus is retained regardless of sign of its flux; report missing masks separately.

For each target, comparison population excludes the target and requires first-visit flux within a factor two and first-visit radial size within 0.4 pixel; at least ten controls required. Fit the visit flux scale as the median of control flux2/flux1 only, leaving the target out. The target difference is flux2 minus scale times flux1; subtract median control difference and require absolute residual to exceed 5 times robust MAD control scatter AND 3 times both the propagated per-aperture error and the empirical 0.044814546 difference scatter from V5's 677 fixed-grid control apertures. Those control apertures are not proven empty sky because OBJECTS is allowed, and the constant is not an independent held-out noise estimate. Aperture errors include median local-sky variance approximated with independent pixels, while the empirical floor partly accounts for correlations. Both signs are screened, with no claim of survey-wide significance. Record selected, masked, nonpositive and tested counts; point coverage and PSF-dependent effects must be checked before opening the third visit.
