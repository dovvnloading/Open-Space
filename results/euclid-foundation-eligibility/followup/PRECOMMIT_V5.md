# V5 frozen development protocol

Use the same two development visits, same region using a shifted, tile-contained rectangle and 3000 by 3000 pixels (5 arcminutes) from each calibrated VIS science, RMS and FLAG mosaic. The later disjoint third visit remains reserved. Assert pixel WCS mapping equivalence at the center and corners within 0.05 pixel before pairing indexes. Allow only flag bits 18 and 24, exclude every other flagged pixel, require finite science and positive finite RMS. Identify first-visit local maxima at least 8 local RMS above a 31-by-31 median-filtered sky, with radius-four all-good aperture, 10-pixel edge and separation. For each retained source measure radius-four flux above median of an unmasked 6–8 pixel sky annulus in both visits; require at least 50 valid annulus pixels and positive flux in both. The identical-pixel approximation needs the WCS assertion; changes in PSF and aperture flux remain possible. For a target, controls must be within a factor two in first-visit flux, within 0.4 pixel in first-visit positive-flux radial second moment, and at least 10 in number. Test differences relative to control median: 5 times control MAD-based sigma and 3 times formal propagated error, both strict. The scale is the median of visit-2 / visit-1 flux among paired sources. This is a development screening rule, not family-wise calibrated significance. No holdout screening without frozen target coordinates, direction and sensitivity.

Empirical noise: use a deterministic grid spaced 30 pixels, excluding points within 12 pixels of any source center; require both apertures and at least 50 sky-annulus pixels valid. Report robust sigma of visit difference after scale and median removal, plus median propagated aperture error. This does not remove correlated systematics or prove image PSF matching. All selection, limits and failures must be reported.

The V3 1800-pixel request was not acquired: it would extend 130 pixels beyond the parent tile. This smaller bound was frozen before source screening.

V5 geometry, frozen after the V4 insufficient-control result: parent mosaic x pixels [0,3000) and y pixels [floor(y_center)-1500, floor(y_center)+1500), where the coordinate is fixed above. This includes the original position. Visit IDs, masks, WCS, statistics and holdout rules remain unchanged.
