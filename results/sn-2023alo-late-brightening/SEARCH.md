# Late-time photometry at the position of SN 2023alo

A 141-position ZTF transient screen identified renewed positive difference flux around the position of this Type IIn supernova in 2025. The host plus transient brightened by approximately 0.32 mag in g and 0.16 mag in r relative to the preceding season. Nearby stars were stable at the few-hundredths level. The host center lies close to the supernova, so identifying the component that brightened requires a differential-astrometry check. This checkpoint records the sightline before testing the supernova and nuclear alternatives.
