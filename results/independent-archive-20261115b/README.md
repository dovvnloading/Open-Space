# Independent archival results

- **A K2 microlensing candidate erupted in six observing seasons.** Mount John measured five outbursts from 2007 to 2014 at the position of two OGLE alerts. Kepler and KMTNet recovered the sixth eruption in 2016. The repeated outbursts favor an eruptive variable at the position of K2C9-R-0367. [Measurements, figure and replay](../recurrent-bulge-transient/README.md)
- **Venus is forecast to occult the same radio pulsar from South Africa and India on 2054 March 2.** At least 84 seconds of simultaneous disk coverage survive the intersection of three source positions and two planetary ephemerides. Unknown proper motion limits the timing. The note also documents a separate conditional sightline in February 2027. [Timing windows and astrometric controls](../venus-pulsar-sightlines/README.md)
- **A light curve in the published Galactic bulge microlensing sample brightened again after 399 days.** BLG515.30.119116 and dwarf-nova candidate OGLE-BLG-DN-0513 share a survey identifier. The second episode survives comparison against 64 other light curves in the field. The first event’s physical origin remains open. [Catalogue audit and field controls](../ogle-dn-0513/README.md)
