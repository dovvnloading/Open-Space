# Independent archival results

- **A K2 microlensing-candidate sightline shows at least six outburst-like brightenings.** Mount John measured five brightenings from 2007 to 2014 at the position of two OGLE alerts. Kepler and KMTNet recovered the sixth eruption in 2016. The repeated outbursts favor an eruptive variable at the position of K2C9-R-0367. [Measurements, figure and replay](../recurrent-bulge-transient/README.md)
- **Fixed-position calculations predict a possible Venus occultation of PSR J1944−1750 from South Africa and India on 2054 March 2.** At least 84 seconds of simultaneous disk coverage survive the intersection of three source positions and two planetary ephemerides. Unknown proper motion limits the timing. The note also documents a separate conditional sightline in February 2027. [Timing windows and astrometric controls](../venus-pulsar-sightlines/README.md)
- **A light curve in the published Galactic bulge microlensing sample shows a second brightening after 399 days.** BLG515.30.119116 and dwarf-nova candidate OGLE-BLG-DN-0513 share a survey identifier. The second episode survives comparison against 64 other light curves in the field. The first event’s physical origin remains open. [Catalogue audit and field controls](../ogle-dn-0513/README.md)

## Scientific limits

**The physical nature of both optical transient sightlines is open.** Multiple brightenings favor intrinsic stellar variability for K2C9-R-0367, but the emitting star and any blend are not established; an independent microlensing episode cannot be excluded. BLG515.30.119116 has a verified second brightening, not a demonstrated physical reclassification of its original event. The classifications of neighboring catalog sources likewise remain uncertain.

**The proper motion of pulsar PSR J1944−1750 is unmeasured.** The 2054 Venus occultation is a conditional prediction from fixed-position astrometry and two planetary ephemerides, not an observed or guaranteed future event. Forecast timing and the availability and detectability of observations need fresh checks nearer the date. The separate 2027 event has additional position disagreements.
