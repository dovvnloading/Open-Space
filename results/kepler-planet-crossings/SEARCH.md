# Search record

Search for occultations between two transiting planets during overlapping Kepler prime-mission transits. The observational unit is one double transit in a single target's light curve. A planet crossing the silhouette of another planet restores flux relative to the sum of two independently measured transit deficits.

Before inspecting candidate photometry, measure the individual transit shapes and timing offsets from other epochs. Compare candidate residuals with matched single-transit and off-transit controls. Check quality flags, local variability, crowding, cadence integration and any available short-cadence observations before interpreting a positive residual. Inspect previously published crossings and keep the denominator and rejection reasons. Exclude white-dwarf companions, dwarf–background-star encounters, novae and TESS false positives.
