# A catalogued Swift soft X-ray source at M31N 2021-06b

LSXPS J004258.9+411807 (ID 785562) has ten Swift/XRT soft-band (0.3–1 keV) detections in the UK Swift Science Data Centre’s one-bin-per-observation light curve. Every listed detection in that series falls between **2021 July 14 and September 15**, from 32.56 through 95.83 days after optical discovery (JD 2459377.5). The count rates span 0.00507–0.01391 counts/s, with the largest on August 4. Nine total-band (0.3–10 keV) bins meet the catalogue detection criterion between July 16 and September 15. These two series use overlapping photons. They independently check the aperture analysis method on the same instrument. The source and these measurements already appear in the mission catalogue. The proposed association is with the named optical nova. [UKSSDC source record](https://www.swift.ac.uk/LSXPS/LSXPS%20J004258.9%2B411807) · [Released detections](swift-lsxps-detections.csv)

The catalogue source is at RA 10.74554°, Dec +41.30199°, about 1.4″ from the nova, with a 3.0″ 90% error radius. The catalogue’s master detection flag is zero. Swift’s 18″ half-power diameter at 1.5 keV allows nearby emission into point-source apertures. The source record spans decades and includes stacked datasets. Its aggregate first/last-detection dates of 2006 and 2024 cannot establish individual soft detections at those times. Only the above ten detections appear in the public per-observation soft detection series at the time of retrieval. Other visits have different exposure times, backgrounds and sensitivities. The first and last detections do not measure the onset and end of the soft phase. [Instrument response](https://swift.gsfc.nasa.gov/about_swift/xrt_desc.html) · [Catalogue definitions](https://www.swift.ac.uk/LSXPS/docs.php)

## Light-curve provenance

The CSV preserves all six columns of each archived detection row in the original order, with an added band label. The first three columns after the label are JD in TDB and signed time extents in days. The last three are count rate and signed rate errors in counts/s. To regenerate the table, download both text products, retain all data rows following the QDP header `READ TERR 1 2`, split on whitespace, and prepend the band labels `soft` and `total`, respectively. These URLs select the detection array (dataset 0) of the per-observation light curve. Separate arrays in the source record supply upper limits.
