# Selection frozen before examining the flux

Start at Sector 107 (2026 August 9 to September 7), the newest sector in the requested mission cycles. The observing unit is one distinct TIC in one sector. Rank the distinct positive integer TICs in the official MIT QLP sector target list by ascending SHA-256 of the unpadded base-10 ASCII integer, breaking ties by TIC. Select the first 128. The selection is independent of magnitudes and photometric measurements and inherits the QLP selection function. There is no additional Tmag cut. Keep failures in the denominator without replacements.

If a QLP release cannot be verified, record the archive response and distinguish a product not released from access failure. A substitute source or altered selection must be documented before viewing flux. The intended inference is for the sampled QLP stars and the injected depth, shape and duration; no all-sky or Cycle 6–8 extrapolation. A catalog miss, a single observed dip, or a lack of independent epoch coverage cannot establish novelty.
