# Independent referee check — Sector 105 asymmetric dimming

**Disposition: bounded null.** No deep, aperiodic, target-localized event in the registered sample survived the full contamination and recurrence checks. This review is not a validation of a rare-event population limit beyond one sector.

## Numerical checks on deposited tables (completed)

- The SHA-256-selected roster has 128 pilot and 256 separately screened stars (384 distinct TICs); 252 have Tmag < 13. The raw roster has 399,217 unique TICs and SHA-256 `82a1427e655eaedb551510b09dd4c2a8f56f3fa7220966139f497e36c82fde58`.
- Pilot: 34 event triggers, 3,554.0724 wall-clock star-days; held-out: 65 event triggers, 7,108.1447 wall-clock star-days. All 99 event dispositions are preserved. The 53 triggered stars each have pixel-cutout receipts.
- The held-out 2% / 12 h asymmetric-dip cell recovered 1,149 of 2,048 injections (56.1035%). Effective exposure = 3,987.9191 star-days; the lower 97.5% weighted-exposure bound = 3,774.6031 star-days. The 97.5% Poisson upper count for all 65 triggers = 82.84784 events. Their quotient = **0.0219487545 per star-day**, a conservative 95%-coverage upper rate under the stated arrivals and injection assumptions. None of the 65 events was discarded to obtain a smaller numerator.
- At 1% / 12 h, 466 of 2,048 injections were recovered: **22.7539%**, rounded to 22.8%. The full nine-cell recovery and rate grid is checked by `referee.py`.
- TIC **316937197**: the QLP 24-hour triangular-template fit is 0.1715444335 (17.15%). A target-centered 3 × 3 pixel-aperture template fit on a separate TESS cutout is 0.0070549488 (0.705%). These depths refer to distinct extraction methods and do not measure intrinsic eclipse depth. A least-squares line through 13 independently extracted timing coordinates spanning 2,890.1138 days gives **18.7670535523 d**, with RMS 0.00655048 d (9.43 min). The earlier 2018 observations and consecutive Sectors 103–105 events rule out a solitary aperiodic dip.
- Eleven of 65 confirmation events reach 1% in the separate pixel extraction. Six are in TIC 116658378, a VSX/SIMBAD/Gaia-classified eclipsing binary. The other five have comparison-star or sky-background evidence against clean target attribution.

## Source identification and catalogs (completed)

TIC 316937197 has **no VSX match within the queried 63 arcseconds**, and its Gaia DR3 entry has no ECL variability class or non-single-star solution (`non_single_star=0`, `phot_variable_flag=NOT_AVAILABLE`). SIMBAD describes the bright source simply as a star. A Gaia neighbor is 1.564 arcseconds away, unresolved by a TESS pixel. The eclipsing component is **unidentified**. These finite catalog searches do **not** prove the 18.767-day signal is uncatalogued in all resources or establish novelty.

## Limits and pending extension

This is a **one-sector search** on a SHA-256-selected subset of QLP targets, with a 6/12/24-hour triangular injection model and 30-minute bins. Detection recovery is measured **after QLP flux extraction and detrending**, not from injected raw spacecraft pixels. It **weakly constrains rare multi-day Boyajian-like events** and says nothing conclusive about megastructures or exocomet populations.

The Sector **106 and 107** QLP roster URLs each returned **HTTP 404** at 2026-11-17 05:18:55 UTC, with status and body hashes stored in `availability.json`. They remain **pending release checks**, not negative observations. Ground-survey coverage at the Sector 105 dip epochs and source-resolved eclipse photometry are also pending.
