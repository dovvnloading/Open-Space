Screen native SAP_FLUX on QUALITY=0 cadences. Average in 30-minute bins with at least half nominal coverage; require a complete event plus half its duration of shoulder on each side. Fit a linear local baseline and triangular flux deficit with total duration 6, 12 or 24 hours and ingress fractions 0.2, 0.5 or 0.8. Retain fitted amplitudes at least 1% of the sector median and at least 7 robust standard deviations (1.4826 MAD of same-template amplitudes within the star-sector). Rank overlapping intervals by score. Symmetric templates protect against forcing asymmetry on eclipses. Seven MADs is an empirical screening threshold, not a global significance.

Review each raw trigger in its stellar and detector context. Examine corrected flux, raw brightness on both sides, background, neighboring-star curves, times of flagged cadences, repeated dips and alternative periods. Treat an uncorroborated fit as ambiguous. Follow plausible isolated asymmetric deficits with pixels, catalog/contaminant checks and independent epochs. Recurrence tests exclude only patterns to which observations are sensitive.

Inject one multiplicative triangular deficit at a time into the original usable flux before binning. Sample peaks uniformly over each entire sector light-curve span, including gaps. Depths are 1%, 2%, 5%; durations are 6, 12, 24 hours. Ingress fractions 0.2 and 0.8 are equally probable. Use seed 8462852 with independent per-star random streams and 8 trials per star and cell. Rerun detection, allow peak error up to a quarter duration or one bin, whichever is greater. A match already present before injection counts as missed.

For each cell use sum of star-span times empirical recovery as effective star-days. A weighted Hoeffding one-sided 97.5% lower bound on exposure and an exact one-sided 97.5% Poisson upper bound on mean counts jointly give at least 95% pointwise coverage under a homogeneous independent Poisson event model. Count all unresolved screen events, including symmetric events, for the conservative all-trigger upper limit. Provide the all-trigger bound regardless of follow-up. A validated-class rate would require calibrated survival through the vetting stage. These photometric injections do not model losses upstream of published SAP, dilution of intrinsic dips, flat-bottomed or multi-day events, or a different stellar population.
