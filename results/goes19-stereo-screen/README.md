# GOES-19 optical group decoding and a stereo direction check

An independent native-file reader reproduced all 50,839 group records from six GOES-19 granules spanning 00:00–00:02 UTC on November 16, 2026. Of those, 50,361 have a decoded time within the two-minute window and 478 fall outside it. The quality flags classify 50,759 rows as good and 80 as degraded across all six files. These counts describe file-local groups, including any duplicates across granule boundaries. Physical flash counts and instrument livetime remain undetermined.

The observing unit is a row within a SHA-256-identified NOAA Level 2 LCFA product. The reader applies the product attributes for unsigned packing, scale, time origin and fill values. The independent decoder reproduced times, coordinates, group identifiers, parent identifiers, flags and all counts. Separately, nominal direction arcs computed for two published 2023 bolides from their GOES-16 centroids pass within 4.82 and 17.50 arcseconds of the corresponding GOES-18 centroids. The continuous arcs account for every nested shell between 40 and 120 km in the chosen geometry. Future acceptance thresholds need pointing and timing uncertainties and chance-association controls.
