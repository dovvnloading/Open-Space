# Rapid variability in 2026 Rubin public alerts

## Question and initial selection

Can Rubin’s closely spaced visits resolve a repeatable minutes-scale modulation of a blue star? Such modulation can arise from stellar pulsation, spin, or binary motion. The intended observing unit is one science-image flux measurement per object and visit. A short period alone does not identify the mechanism.

The initial broker query selects Gaia DR3 matches within 0.8 arcsec of Rubin stationary alert objects, with BP−RP < 0.25, G > 16, at least 10 detections and 30 broker-reported forced measurements. The analysis uses individual 2026 observations and reduces time-series selection bias by using forced fluxes. Search individual well-sampled nights first. Test a proposed oscillation on separate visits and other sources observed at the same time. Reject matches to common instrumental variability, literature recoveries and insufficient coverage as discovery claims.

The feasibility audit also examines distinct questions on repeat dimming of catalogue white dwarfs and anomalous moving-source extension.
