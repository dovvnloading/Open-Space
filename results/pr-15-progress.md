## Verification and remaining questions

A separate checkout downloaded the 25 public ESPRESSO inputs again and reproduced the flux-based velocities, line strengths and profiles, including the ±5 m/s injection test. New downloads of all 15 SMC X-1 XRT event lists and maps reproduced the released event table and band counts byte for byte. The six-detector SPHEREx analysis and the five-period comparison replayed from the pinned inputs. Re-extracting the 58 M31 XRT pointings and their exposure maps reproduced the summer timeline table. Local checks included the 113-test repository suite, pilot and 12-seed benchmark. GitHub CI also passed for the SMC X-1 checkpoint. [CI run](https://github.com/dovvnloading/Open-Space/actions/runs/43658945510)

- **HD 32564.** The Doppler shifts recur across wavelength bands. The Hα and Ca I controls and the unreleased ESO visits leave the companion interpretation open.
- **M31.** The source producing the summer soft photons in the Swift aperture is not uniquely resolved. Chandra HRC provides precise October positions but cannot establish the October spectrum. The long delay between the 2017 eruption and the HRC detections does not measure the emission duration.
- **WR 117-2.** The 0.82″ neighbor, an aperture-dependent channel, different instrument bandpasses and multiple viable period aliases limit source and line attribution.
- **SMC X-1.** Subtracting the eclipse-core spectrum weakens the evidence for a change in intrinsic hardness. The brightness changes survive. The original late-November forecast remains a prospective test.
