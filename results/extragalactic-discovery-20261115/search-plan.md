# Independent archival searches, 15 November 2026

At selection, the GitHub open pull request collection was empty. The result directory names and visible research branches were checked. The following questions concern archives and associations outside the ongoing gamma-ray burst, stellar, space-physics, lensed supernova and occultation searches.

* Can published extended radio doubles acquire independently measured physical sizes beyond the giant-radio-galaxy threshold, after checking the host, spectroscopy, angular extent and prior giant compilations?
* Do catalogued extragalactic globular clusters form close pairs in both projected position and measured velocity that survive misidentification, rotation, density and multiple comparisons?
* Can independent planetary-nebula and compact-cluster catalogs identify a previously unreported nebula coincident in both position and velocity with an extragalactic cluster?

A separate planetary-geology search will test whether geographically separate new impact sites on Mars have compatible formation intervals. Repeated detections, search footprints and common image dates can produce spurious associations.

These are research questions. Candidate designations and numerical results require actual source records, reproducible calculations and reference checks.
