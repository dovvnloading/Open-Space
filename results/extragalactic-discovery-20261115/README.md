# Independent archival investigations, 15 November 2026

| Investigation | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Giant radio galaxies | The proposed host of RGZ J000314.9+111544 has a DESI spectroscopic redshift of 0.763424. FIRST and VLASS independently place its two outer radio hotspots 1.13–1.14 Mpc apart. RGZ J085850.1+262321 is a **spectroscopic confirmation of a volunteer's earlier megaparsec estimate (2014)**, not a newly discovered radio galaxy. | [Radio fields, spectra, catalog comparisons and object histories](../rgz-desi-giant-radio-galaxies/README.md) |
| Recent Martian impacts | Across 259 impact fields, the average diameter-rank difference between nearest craters is 8.4% smaller than expected after allowing for broad sorting along each field. A published fragmentation simulation reproduces part of the effect. **Fragmentation physics, terrain, and image completeness remain open explanations. The comparison against the simulation is inconclusive at the 2 m and 3 m diameter cuts.** | [Catalog measurement](../mars-crater-size-correlation/README.md) · [Physical model comparison](../mars-crater-size-correlation/MODEL.md) · [HiRISE image check](../mars-crater-size-correlation/pixel-check/README.md) |
| Fornax3D repeat observations | In four galaxies, 36 **candidate corrections** to published repeat-observation links meet position, brightness and velocity consistency tests. Median separation changes from 15.39″ for the listed links to 0.154″ for the proposed alternatives; raw-cube validation is still needed. | [Full row-by-row audit and conservative association tests](../fornax3d-nebula-identifications/README.md) |
| Nearby galaxy cluster membership | The excess of bright planetary nebulae near compact clusters fails to repeat in a second selection. Among 544 additional nebulae the search found one pair against 5.53 expected. The M31 cluster-pair screen also yields no nominally bound pair with complete measurements. | [PHANGS and M31 controls, images and selection limits](../phangs-planetary-nebula-cluster-tests/README.md) |

The [initial search questions](search-plan.md) were committed before the scientific results. The Mars analysis moved from separate impact-site chronology to within-field diameters after shared source images made close catalog pairs ambiguous. The observation and model data in the linked reports are archival. **Publication priority is unverified.** None of these measurements alone establishes first-publication priority or proves a newly discovered object.
