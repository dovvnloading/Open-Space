# Orbital outburst phases in Be/X-ray binaries

## Search plan

Search the public Swift/BAT transient-monitor 15–50 keV daily rates for trains of orbitally recurring outbursts at a changed orbital phase or cadence. Initial targets are EXO 2030+375, GRO J1008−57, 2S 1417−624, GX 304−1, XTE J1946+274 and Swift J1626.6−5156. These are known binaries. A new result would require a specific previously unreported episode or relationship.

Use published orbital periods, retain source coverage and rejected cases, compare with primary literature, and test interesting sequences against another instrument when available. Recurring outbursts, prior giant outbursts and the known historical phase shifts in EXO 2030+375 are controls. Separate time-of-peak shifts from changes in outburst shape, long-lived bright states and seasonal gaps. No measurement is claimed at this checkpoint.
