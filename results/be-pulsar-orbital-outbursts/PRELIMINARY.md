# First imaging and timing checks

Two of eight passages marked as missing in Figure 2 of Huang, Ji & Yan (2025) show resolved brightenings in the MAXI/GSC daily light curve near the peak of Fermi/GBM's pulsed emission. On 2022 September 28 and 2023 September 26 the local 2–20 keV excesses were 0.0555 ± 0.0059 and 0.0695 ± 0.0057 photons cm⁻² s⁻¹. Neither Cyg X-3 nor Cyg X-1 shows a corresponding positive excess on either date. The June 2023 event fails that particular control.

On 2023 September 26, 4,373 independent MAXI 3–15 keV photons from a 1.25° aperture over six days give fundamental Rayleigh power 114.1 at Fermi's frequency (approximately 0.024336 Hz). The timing test uses the measured frequency as supplied and includes the Earth and binary light-travel corrections. This is evidence of residual pulsed activity on some weak orbits. The previously reported alternation of the bright events persists.

This file is an intermediate checkpoint. A replayable analysis with sidebands, control fields, randomized scans and all eight orbit dispositions follows.
