# SDSS-V DR20 changing-look pilot: calibration-limited development

**The 32-source cohort is frozen, but no changing-look classification has been tested or ruled out.** Development calibration checks show substantial epoch-to-epoch differences. The preregistered broad-Hβ test cannot yet be applied defensibly: host and Fe II modeling, correlated noise and empirical false-change controls remain unqualified. The held-out spectra were opened for identity and exposure metadata only; their flux arrays were not examined.

The field, source ordering and primary threshold were frozen in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) before flux inspection. Of 54 metadata candidates, the first 32 ascending SDSS_IDs were selected without flux-based replacement. Each has baseline MJD 59648, good development MJD 60322 and good holdout MJD 60498. Six bad-field post-DR19 nights were excluded: 60298, 60674, 60675, 60699, 60701 and 60702. The 60674 night defined the original metadata footprint only. All 96 selected spectra were retrieved; coordinates agree within one arcsecond, SPALL identifies all as science objects with ZWARNING=0, and parent TAI sets are pairwise disjoint for each source. Same instrument and field can retain shared systematics. [Selection](good-selection.csv) · [Per-observation hashes and parent lists](manifest.json) · [Configuration](campaign.json)

Among 64 development spectra, 54 meet the prespecified >=80% rest-frame coverage and continuum S/N >=5; 25 of 32 pairs pass in both epochs. Seven pairs are uncovered or too noisy under that rule. The narrow-[O III] diagnostic has nominal >5σ core-only measurements in both epochs for 23 pairs. Their development/baseline ratios have median 1.095 and range 0.660–6.462; uncertainties omit continuum error and correlated noise. These ratios are not a calibrated variability test. [Development QA](development-qa.csv)

Eight same-field standards, selected by ascending ID before inspecting their flux, have median development/baseline ratios 0.846, 0.793, 0.768 and 0.752 at 5500, 6500, 7500 and 8500 Å respectively; robust MAD scales are 0.284, 0.253, 0.259 and 0.267. The 6500 Å individual ratios range 0.526–2.227. These stars participate in calibration and are not independent controls. Field metadata gives median seeing 1.369 and 1.887 arcsec; no cause is assigned. SDSS documents fiber/hour-angle flux-calibration effects and correlated coadd noise in its [DR20 BHM caveats](https://www.sdss.org/dr20/bhm/caveats/). [Control measurements](calibration-stars.csv) · [Input receipts](calibration-receipt.json)

The exploratory fixed-wing/[O III] proxy was frozen after calibration QA but before inspecting its values. All 23 nominally eligible pairs give values; development-minus-baseline range is −0.963112 to +0.566871, median −0.001535. These are not broad-line flux changes or candidate scores: the proxy omits host, Fe II, covariance and calibration uncertainty. It cannot qualify a holdout. [Proxy](wing-proxy.csv)

The separately frozen toy simulation uses Gaussian wings, noise and a noiseless unit normalizer. For 200 null pairs its 5th–95th percentile differences are −36.50 to +31.11; at peak amplitude 3 they are +10.51 to +73.82 in arbitrary units. It does not simulate real host light, masks, Fe II, covariance or calibration and does not qualify the primary 5σ criterion. [Synthetic-only results](synthetic.json)

Next, validate a joint host, Fe II, narrow and broad-line model on development data; obtain disjoint-exposure nulls and an independent comparison population, and propagate empirical calibration uncertainty. Freeze each qualifying prediction before viewing its holdout. No population rate, novel source, or physical null follows here. Individual target literature and aliases have not been searched.

## Development follow-up: a previously known changing-look quasar

The initially frozen joint local model was applied to the 25 pairs passing coverage and S/N. It gave one conditional threshold flag, SDSS_ID 62239511. Its fixed narrow-line model was inadequate: χ²/dof was 2091.5/302 in the baseline and 1079.5/302 in development, so its nominal significance does not qualify a holdout. The [development spectra](outlier-development.png) show a weaker Hβ-region excess in the second epoch and a displacement of [O III] from the baseline pipeline-redshift position. No held-out flux was read.

An exploratory model revision, frozen after inspecting those development spectra, chose a common narrow profile using both development observations. Its local broad-Hβ/[O III] area ratios for the quadratic-continuum, 20 Å broad-sigma model are 0.657 ± 0.047 on 2022-03-10 and 0.043 ± 0.060 on 2024-01-13, where errors are conditional normal-matrix estimates. χ²/dof improves to 469.3/301 and 309.3/301, respectively. Across the four fixed broad-width/continuum alternatives, baseline ratios span 0.657–1.060 and development ratios span approximately 0–0.170. The 20 Å, linear-continuum alternative has a ratio of only 4.84, below the frozen factor-five threshold; the model therefore does not qualify the target under all alternatives. The exploratory profile selection, partial host/Fe II treatment, residual structure and calibration differences remain material. [Initial fits](joint-fits.csv) · [Profile alternatives](profile-fits.csv)

For the selected profile, 200 development-only five-pixel residual-block perturbations all converged. The 2.5th–97.5th percentiles of development-minus-baseline ratio are −0.774 to −0.404. This range is conditional on the selected model and block-resampling assumptions, not a calibrated coverage statement. Adding fixed broad profiles to the actual development spectra tests algorithmic response but does not add independent noise realizations. [Real-background injections](real-injections.csv) · [Residual-block result](residual-blocks.json)

The 22 other development-only wing proxies are a descriptive comparison, not a controlled null population; four have an absolute proxy difference at least as large as this source's 0.3903. Inspection of the two most extreme proxies ([negative](outlier-62237784.png), [positive](outlier-62238906.png)) shows visible noise and narrow-line/continuum differences, not independent classifications. Their signal, calibration and physical nature are not resolved.

The flagged object is **SDSS J141324.27+530526.9, RMID 017**, already studied as a changing-look quasar by [Dexter et al. 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.10138); the authors analyze its earlier 2014–2017 evolution. [Lu and Wang 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06753) also identify RM17 among their He II changing-look sample and describe its prior Hβ status. This object is a post hoc known-object check, not an independently preselected positive control or a newly discovered changing-look AGN. Other target histories remain unsearched.
