The selected observing unit is stable SDSS_ID × disjoint physical exposure sets, verified by full TAI_LIST and parent MJD_LIST, not filename CATALOGID. Use the 2024-12-30 daily field as bounded initial metadata footprint, all candidates joined by SDSS_ID and position to older legacy baseline, earliest good post-DR19 observation after 2023-07-31, and latest separate night thereafter. Lowest 32 SDSS_IDs satisfying metadata-only criteria: 0.10<=baseline z<=0.75, QSO or GALAXY spectral classification, zero redshift warning, valid coordinates, three pairwise disjoint parent exposure lists, and observable Hβ/[O III] range from nominal grid (not measured line flux). Require baseline older than DR19 cutoff, then development new epoch, then strictly later held-out night. Preserve complete enumeration/exclusions and target ID version links. 32 is maximum, not a promise; do not replace failures with candidates chosen by visual flux inspection. DR20 catalogs data through MJD 60708. Required S/N and actual mask coverage are measured after cohort freezing and are screen eligibility, not sample inclusion.

Question: does a broad Hβ component change strongly from the old baseline to the first new epoch, with the predicted change still present in a later observation? Before using flux, fit a common source redshift anchored by narrow lines (within pipeline errors), relative [O III] normalization with uncertainties, host plus power-law continuum, narrow Hβ/[O III] and broad Hβ, masking zero IVAR and AND-mask pixels; compare alternate continuum windows and profile shapes. Require a >=5σ signed change in integrated broad Hβ, >5σ broad emission in the high state, and low-state upper limit less than half the high-state flux, with >=80% usable coverage of prescribed rest 4750–5100 Å and continuum S/N >=5 per pixel in each observation. Calibrate using same-night splits, same-field stable standards, [O III] residuals, synthetic injection/recovery separate from observational output; require calibration scatter small relative to proposed change and correct for 32 tests. This is a screening proposal until fit methodology and false-change control are demonstrated: never assign discovery, density or population limits from underpowered or incomplete data. Freeze the identity, sign, broad-line window and threshold of any qualifying candidate before reading holdout flux. Test against remaining independent exposure night; make lack of sensitivity explicitly inconclusive. Photometric variability is support only; independent telescope remains desirable.

The project DR17 archive adapter and cohort coordinator validate DR17 plate/MJD/fiber normalized spectra and a narrow galaxy cohort, not DR20 FPS multi-epoch identity or AGN profiles. Direct multi-epoch FITS analysis with explicit manifest and byte hashes is needed here; do not invent ledger job IDs or call independent models independent data. Run calibration, synthetic tests and positive control separately. Any target without a later separate good night or disjoint TAI lists is uncovered and retained in denominators. Freeze new manifest after verifying metadata/parent identity and before inspecting any flux. This correction changes only eligibility, not the frozen science threshold or any measured flux.

## Controls-first operational windows

Before examining development flux, evaluate observed COADD arrays transformed to the baseline pipeline rest frame: continuum windows 4750–4770 and 5080–5100 Å; line-domain coverage 4750–5100 Å. An eligible pixel has positive inverse variance, finite flux and zero AND mask. Continuum S/N is the median per-pixel flux×sqrt(ivar) in the two windows, and coverage is the fraction of native pixels in the domain that are eligible. This practical diagnostic applies to both development observations; it does not estimate covariance. For a calibration diagnostic only, estimate narrow [O III] λ5008 via the integrated residual in 4998–5018 Å above a linearly interpolated median continuum in 4975–4988 and 5025–5038 Å; report values and conditional Poisson-like errors, not a broad-line test. Require at least 80% eligible pixels in each window; missing windows are uncovered. The mean pair scale and [O III] noise alone cannot determine calibration error. No holdout arrays will be accessed at this stage. This definition is frozen before running the controls.

## Same-field calibration-star control

Before inspecting any calibrator spectra, use common SDSS_IDs classified `SPECTROPHOTO_STD` in both MJD 59648 and 60322 metadata; select the eight lowest positive IDs. In each spectrum measure median observed-frame flux in 100 Å windows centered at 5500, 6500, 7500 and 8500 Å, with positive inverse variance and clear AND masks and >=80% usable pixels. Report ratios of development to baseline per star and window, median and robust MAD across stars. These stars participate in spectrophotometric calibration and are a diagnostic, not independent calibrators; they cannot capture target-specific aperture, host or systematics. Window scatter and [O III] line ratios are exploratory. No astrophysical change statistic or threshold is tuned from these spectra.

## Exploratory broad-wing diagnostic after calibration QA

The controls found substantial epoch-to-epoch response differences before any broad-line screening, so the prespecified discovery screen is not yet qualified. To quantify—not classify—the development data, freeze these diagnostic windows before inspecting broad Hβ values: mean continuum from 4750–4770 and 5080–5100 Å, linear interpolation; broad-wing rest windows 4805–4840 and 4885–4920 Å, excluding the narrow core; integrate residual and divide by the separately measured narrow [O III] flux in the same epoch. Use only the 25 pairs meeting prespecified domain/SNR thresholds and with positive [O III] residual >5 times formal core-only error in both epochs. Report ratios and raw difference but not significance or physical classifications: fixed wing width omits parts of variable broad profiles, Fe II and host absorption are not modeled, continuum errors/covariance and [O III] response effects are not propagated. These diagnostics cannot qualify a target for the frozen holdout. Resolve calibration and validated host/line model with synthetic and null controls first.

## Synthetic diagnostic qualification

Before synthetic draws, fix a separate toy test: 200 independent pairs per amplitude, Gaussian per-pixel noise σ=1, 1 Å sampling on 4750–5100 Å, flat continuum 10, unit fixed [O III] normalizer (no noise), Gaussian Hβ sigma 20 Å at 4862.68 Å injected only in the second epoch at peak amplitude 0, 1 or 3 flux units, deterministic NumPy seed 20261116. Apply the fixed wing diagnostic above. Report median and 5th–95th percentile of measured differences. This measures only linear proxy response under a simple known spectrum; no host, Fe II, real masks, calibration, resampling covariance or astrophysical variability are simulated, and this toy test cannot validate the primary 5σ discovery criterion.

## Joint development fit and real-background qualification

A fresh method/campaign `dr20-clagn-e657b57-terra61-joint1` is frozen before fitting development spectra. In 4750–5100 Å rest frame use weighted linear regression with zero AND mask, positive inverse variance and finite flux; continuum is a polynomial of degree two in (λ−4925)/175, including curvature as a local nuisance, not a full physical Fe II template. Include a stellar Hβ absorption template Gaussian sigma 7 Å with nonpositive amplitude, narrow Hβ Gaussian sigma 2.5 Å with nonnegative amplitude, and an [O III] doublet Gaussian sigma 2.5 Å with fixed 5008/4960 flux ratio 2.98 and nonnegative amplitude. Include a nonnegative broad Hβ Gaussian with sigma 20 Å; repeat at sigma 35 Å as a prespecified profile alternative. Wavelength center uses the baseline pipeline z (no narrow-line redshift refit); evaluate plus/minus 5 Å center shifts as a conservative model sensitivity diagnostic. These narrow widths are phenomenological; relative model quantities only. Linear least squares with constrained bounds via scipy.optimize.lsq_linear, per-pixel standard deviation multiplied by 1.2, covariance estimated from the weighted unconstrained normal matrix (conservative at a boundary only as a diagnostic, not coverage-guaranteed). Require line-region coverage >=80%, continuum S/N>=5, [O III] positive >=5σ in both epochs, and fit success. For each epoch estimate broad Gaussian area / [O III] doublet area, with both coefficients and their covariance propagated via the delta method. Test a high/low ratio >=5 and difference >=5σ, and require the high state's broad area >=5σ. No discovery or holdout selection is allowed until these criteria survive the model alternatives, empirical calibration and injection controls. The local curvature and constrained absorption bound—but do not fully represent—stellar population and Fe II. Report conditional fixed-profile or wing sensitivity, not a full spectral state classification.

Empirical noise qualification is a separate real-background injection experiment: for each baseline spectrum that passes the frozen coverage/SNR cuts, make one no-injection and two positive broad-line injections in the development copy, fixed observed amplitudes +0.5 and +2.0 times the baseline epoch's fitted broad area, independently at the sigma-20 profile; keep pixel masks and noise unchanged. This is deterministic injection/recovery, not an independent noise sample or a false-alarm probability; report bias and recovery and explicitly preserve negative/zero baseline amplitudes and failures. Also compare degree-two versus degree-one continuum with the same line model, and center shifts ±5 Å; compare local color and [O III] normalization/seeing changes using previously frozen stars. Unresolved host/Fe II and non-simultaneous photometry remain alternatives. Fit only baseline/development; holdout remains unread until prediction is frozen.

## Exploratory narrow-profile revision after inspection

The development outlier's baseline [O III] is visibly offset relative to the pipeline redshift and not described by the fixed 2.5 Å Gaussian, so the first fit is inadequate. Before recalculating, test a new local model in a separate campaign `dr20-clagn-e657b57-terra61-joint2`: common narrow centroid shift in {−4,−2,0,+2,+4} Å and sigma in {2.5,4,6} Å chosen by minimum sum of the two development weighted residual sums; allow a second [O III] doublet at twice the selected sigma with shared centroid and nonnegative amplitude, preserving 2.98 ratio. Apply the same shift and narrow width to Hβ. Fit each epoch separately conditional on the shared chosen profile; broad sigma 20 or 35 and continuum degree 1 or 2 remain alternatives. This is an exploratory post-inspection model; selecting its profile using both development epochs invalidates any naive interpretation of formal p-values as held-out significance. It does not freeze a holdout prediction or claim an identified transition. Compare residual χ², broad areas, local control sensitivity and profile alternatives; host population and Fe II remain incomplete. Keep heldout arrays closed.
