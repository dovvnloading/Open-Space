# Search for very young main-belt asteroid fragments

Frozen before inspecting the ranked pairs, 2026-11-15. The observing unit is a pair of separately tracked main-belt asteroids. The question is whether two distinct bodies occupied a common location recently at a relative speed compatible with fragmentation. The search starts with the Minor Planet Center's public MPCORB osculating elements. It includes the orbital phase and retains observing-arc information.

Close elements alone cannot establish a relationship. The intended tests are a common-epoch comparison, a planetary-ephemeris reconstruction of the closest approach, a check of the orbital uncertainties and of whether the objects were independently resolved, and a search for published identifications under both designations. Known families and duplicate designations are controls. Any result will distinguish the catalog measurement from the inferred physical history.
