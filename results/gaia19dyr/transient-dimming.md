# Gaia19dyr observing record

Gaia19dyr (AT 2019pse, Gaia DR3 4266891305243650560, 2MASS J18542109+0052399) is a previously alerted candidate young stellar object at 283.58793°, +0.87774°. The Science Alerts team reported a 1.6-mag dimming on 2019 September 4. We investigate whether deep dimming repeatedly recurred at a preferred phase and whether the amplitude changed with observing season. The unit of analysis is an observing night. The initial discovery screen used publicly released ZTF and Gaia photometry and therefore subsequent comparisons using these releases are cross-instrument checks within an exploratory study.

The initial ZTF nightly single-season scans peak close to nine days in multiple observing seasons. ZTF r-band excursions exceeded one magnitude on 0 nights in 2018, 23 in 2020 and 40 in 2021. The ZTF 2018 and 2025 seasons bracket the deeper dips. The interpretation as occultation by a particular structure requires additional evidence.
