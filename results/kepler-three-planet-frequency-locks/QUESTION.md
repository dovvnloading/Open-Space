# Three-planet orbital frequency locks

Can a combination of three measured transit frequencies remain phase coherent in compact multi-planet systems? The observing unit is a transiting triplet around one host. This branch tests integer combinations of the inner, middle, and outer planets’ orbital frequencies against individual transit times and alternate published ephemerides. A catalog coincidence can arise from mixed period estimates, a short baseline, or searching many integer coefficients. Report the searched population and controls, and distinguish measured phase behavior from model-dependent libration.

The main-branch result directories and open pull requests were checked before starting. This search uses planet transit epochs and addresses a different question from the repository's projected planet crossings and individual-planet transit searches.
