# Secondary search away from known controls

This follow-up was specified after seeing the primary control-recovery table and field-null summaries. All 99 original paired maxima came from the Boötes field. The secondary search estimates the significance of other stellar overdensities over a domain that excludes both known controls. Its choice was prompted by the primary outcome and it is exploratory.

Use the original DR11 stellar catalogue, filters, grid, 15 distance moduli, three aperture sizes, local background, 0.25-degree shuffle cells and shot-noise score. Remove all centre pixels within 26 arcmin (inclusive), using the original projected distance definition, of the prelisted Boötes I and Coma Berenices coordinates. Every aperture-scale and both observed and shuffled score maps receive the identical exclusion. Keep stars in excluded centres' neighborhoods in all calculations, so extended control outskirts or large background annuli can still influence valid centres. No further positional or score-based cuts will be made.

Draw 199 independent within-cell permutations per field with random seed 20261118, pair the fields by realization index, and take each pair's maximum over both fields, all retained centres and all templates. Retain peaks with (1 + number of paired maxima ≥ observed)/(199 + 1) ≤ 0.05. Apply the original angular peak de-duplication after masking. Report threshold, maxima, peaks and scale-dependent surviving area. Secondary significance is conditional on this specified mask, photometric permutation model and follow-up design. The primary injection campaign does not calibrate recovery at this new threshold.
