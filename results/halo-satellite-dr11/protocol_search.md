# Detection and recovery protocol

Frozen before ranking or examining catalogue rows. Search two ICRS rectangles named Bootes (209–212°, 13.5–15.5°) and Coma (185.75–188.75°, 22.9–24.9°). Retain clean stellar sources with finite dereddened g,r, positive errors below 0.2 mag, 17 ≤ g ≤ 23.5 and 16 ≤ r ≤ 23.5. For each field build a 1.5 arcmin Cartesian grid with RA scaled by the cosine of its central declination. Fit no parameters to the two known satellites.

Use a published old metal-poor g/r track (nearest available to 12 Gyr, [Fe/H] = −2, exact choice and provenance recorded with the input). Slide it through distance modulus 16–23 by 0.5 mag. Form a bounded Gaussian colour weight using photometric uncertainty and a 0.05 mag intrinsic width, for main-sequence and red giant branches; record exact implementation. Compare weighted counts in circular apertures of radius 3, 6 and 12 arcmin against a concentric annulus spanning 2–3 aperture radii. Rank the on-minus-off contrast divided by its estimated shot noise. Require complete geometric aperture and annulus coverage inside the rectangle. Mask, depth and morphology issues are checked independently when interpreting peaks.

For trials control, shuffle complete colour–magnitude/error tuples among retained stellar positions within fixed ~0.25° cells, holding density and approximate depth structure fixed. A field null realization uses the maximum over all valid centres, widths and distance moduli. Use 99 realizations per field and combine paired maxima across both fields. Retain local maxima with global Monte Carlo p ≤ 0.05; also list descriptive top peaks and ranks of controls. Greedily de-duplicate peaks separated by less than the larger aperture diameter. A control is recovered if its centre is within max(6 arcmin, aperture radius) of a retained peak and its modulus agrees within 1 mag. Report spatial and distance recovery separately. Bootes I and Coma Berenices are control regions, irrespective of outcomes.

Inject independent old populations with Plummer projected positions and stochastic IMF sampling normalized by expected V-band luminosity; identify luminosity convention, passband, and any approximation in the output. Use at least two values per dimension of absolute magnitude, projected half-light radius and distance; report binomial intervals. Compare the same detector and global threshold with and without the injected stars at the injected position, requiring a nearby above-threshold centre within 1 mag of the injected modulus; exclude known controls and avoid unusable edges. Report selection-conditional spatial coverage, local field variation, and the absence of any image-level completeness calibration. No empty, failed or control-only footprint yields an astrophysical null.
