# Targeted CMD confirmations

Before evaluating CMD counts, fix the selection to the survey-native pre-helium-flash PARSEC track at 12 Gyr, Z = 0.0002. Retain stars with absolute colour residual ≤ 2 × sqrt(0.05² + σg² + σr²). Compute the model colour at the dereddened g magnitude. No modulus, colour cut, aperture or target centre is optimized in these comparisons. The two controls use the published coordinates, moduli and apertures fixed in protocol.md. The descriptive top off-control peak uses its measured DR11 coordinates, 12′ radius and modulus 20.5. Report it under the same rule irrespective of the sign of the observed excess.

PS1 uses its published DR2 stack selection and uncorrected brightness cuts used during retrieval. Apply per-source SFD98 dust corrections for the CMD, with PS1 coefficients 3.172 and 2.271. DELVE g and r are already corrected. Use all DELVE rows satisfying the archive selection (stellar morphology, useful g/r errors, clean per-band image flags and g₀ ≤ 23.5). DELVE and Legacy have overlapping DECam input exposures.

For each target, use on radius R and off annulus (2R,3R]. In both regions cross-match independent DR11 bright reference points (PSF sources with clean flag, 18 ≤ g₀ ≤ 20.5 and 17 ≤ r₀ ≤ 20.5) against the survey catalogue within 1 arcsec. Record matched/eligible counts and reference recovery fractions. These are evidence of source coverage, including holes and differential completeness, rather than calibrated angular masks. Report nominal uniform-area counts (area ratio ≈ 0.2); for DELVE also provide the signed excess adjusted by the ratio of reference recovery fractions as a diagnostic when measurable. Withhold area-based tail probabilities if either reference fraction is below 0.80 or their absolute difference exceeds 0.10. The one-sided conditional binomial probabilities assume homogeneous surface density and full geometric coverage. For CMD enrichment among selected local stars, also report a one-sided Fisher exact probability comparing CMD vs non-CMD in the two regions, when both have sources. None of these conditional p-values calibrates a blind survey discovery.

Show all on/off CMD points for the controls, their native tracks and selections. Counts and figures retain surveyed populations even if the area-based comparison is unusable. The exploratory off-control peak has no assigned discovery significance from these follow-up comparisons.
