# Search and archive receipts

Searches made 2026-11-17 UTC, before and during analysis. Results are not exhaustive; ADS and unpublished studies not searched directly.

- Web query ["NGC 7172" "XRISM" 2026 iron 2025](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22NGC+7172%22+%22XRISM%22+2026+iron+2025) and ["202074010" XRISM NGC7172 paper](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22202074010%22+XRISM+NGC7172+paper): DARTS mission quick look / proposal https://data.darts.isas.jaxa.jp/pub/xrism/browse/quick_look/resolve/html/202074010.html; no exact-ID paper surfaced in this search. Proposal explicitly seeks Fe-line width and blue shifts; absence of indexed results does not establish novelty.
- Web query ["NGC 7172" iron K alpha line variability XMM Chandra absorption](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22NGC+7172%22+iron+K+alpha+line+variability+XMM+Chandra+absorption) returned Dadina et al. 2001 A&A 368, 73, https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0101324, describing approximate constancy within errors; Vasylenko 2018, https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.08457, discusses prior variability and distant torus. They concern different epochs and instruments.
- HEASARC TAP https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/xamin/vo/tap/sync, `release-query.adql`, raw binary VOTable `release-query.xml`, response SHA-256 e81308e2f64f5856562b18bbeb8f7167622749e706591c2eb60812e0339412be. Row 202074010 has time MJD 60969.7299074074, exposure 215347.02904 s, public_date MJD 61348 = 2026-11-04. Retrieved 2026-11-17 UTC. Response retains nine matches, cap 200; no overflow.
- `response_listing.html.gz` is the 2026-11-17 UTC direct archive directory https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/FTP/xrism/data/obs/2/202074010/resolve/products/; it lists no RMF or ARF. A generic mission response is not the matching excluded-pixel, Hp-only response, and the latter must be made from the observation. Archive energy-scale report and GTIs are cited in the result README.
- `nxb_recipe.html.gz` preserves https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/xrism/analysis/nxb/resolve_nxb_db.html retrieved 2026-11-17 UTC. The public https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/FTP/xrism/postlaunch/nxb/provisional/resolve/ lists v3 night-Earth event 27 MB, EHK 1.2 GB and NXB RMF 25 MB (archive rounded sizes). Its recipe matches science/sky filtering and CORTIME distribution using rslnxbgen, recommends CORTIME >=6; source EHK is ~73 MB. These matching calculations were not executed; NXB counts and relative half coverage are not established.
