# Frozen test design: NGC 7172 Resolve 202074010

Frozen before inspecting any event energies or test counts, 2026-11-17 UTC, code base faa0667. Observing unit is one 2025-10-21–27 spacecraft visit; chronological halves share detector, response and sky and are not independent observations. This tests whether the source-frame Fe Kα region changes relative to local continuum within the visit, not a claim of first variability or a measurement of intrinsic width.

Input: HEASARC cleaned p0px1000 event file SHA-256 74230a4c915f9fa7d7ff54bb1d09e242766e1dd1f5af67db3b6d0bf0ad26224c; URL https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/FTP/xrism/data/obs/2/202074010/resolve/event_cl/xa202074010rsl_p0px1000_cl.evt.gz. Apply mission standard clean screening already in file; Hp grade only, exclude calibration pixel 12 and known problematic pixel 27, require per-pixel GTI and record other quality flags. Split at the time corresponding to half of the union exposure of the global GTIs, not the median photon time. Document if pixel GTIs differ or time standard is ambiguous. No barycentric correction is necessary for this day-scale split.

Preselected observed-frame bands: 6.31–6.38 keV (line region), 6.20–6.27 and 6.42–6.49 keV (sidebands). The line band covers the redshifted neutral Fe doublet near 6.35 keV; these are broad count windows, not a fit to one Gaussian or a velocity determination. Both sidebands combined have twice the line bandwidth. For each half, count L in line region and C in combined sides, and estimate continuum-subtracted line rate per good-time exposure with (L-C/2)/good_time_exposure. The single primary null: equal ratios L/C across the two halves; equivalently, given fixed row and column totals, the line and side photons have the same half membership. Use exact two-sided Fisher conditional p-value (ordering by probability), one test, alpha 0.05; report counts and uncertainty, not merely significance. This ratio comparison is differential and not a calibrated line-flux measurement. Test assumes the continuum shape and background fractions near the line do not change; inspect broad-band hardness and standard background/gain information as separate diagnostics, not additional discoveries. If response, screening, time/GTI, or low sensitivity prevent defensible inference, mark inconclusive. Quantify low-count sensitivity and avoid treating a non-rejection as evidence of constancy.

Calibration controls: retrieve archival standard RMF/ARF and energy-scale report where available, verify file metadata/grade, detector gain and energy scale, GTIs, and non-X-ray background recipe. Compare counts by pixel and live exposure. Never infer intrinsic width without response-convolved doublet including natural widths and Compton shoulder. No same-observation pixel region is a clean local background for a point source; use mission night-Earth NXB control if available and otherwise state background unmeasured. Precedent BeppoSAX https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0101324 and NuSTAR https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.08457 reported long-term evidence / limits on line variability. This new visit tests a different time and spectral response; not an independent discovery claim.

## Frozen diagnostic design

After the primary result and before inspecting control counts: separately count the two 70 eV sidebands and compare their relative proportion between halves with Fisher's exact test as exploratory continuum-shape control. Compare broad observed 3–5 and 7–9 keV Hp counts as a second exploratory hardness diagnostic. Divide eligible pixels into inner versus outer using the instrument's central 2x2 detector coordinates (DETX 3–4 and DETY 3–4), compare primary bands and Hp/(Hp+Mp) grade fractions by half; counts are not independent astrophysical observations. Bin global GTIs into equal cumulative-good-time-exposure deciles and record 3–9 keV rate, line and side counts. Recompute the same line/side statistic after shifting every fixed energy boundary simultaneously by ±5 eV, conservative relative to full-array 5.9 keV gain shift ±0.01 eV in the gain report; this is a stress test, not a measured per-half drift bound. No significance claims from these unadjusted multiple diagnostics. Save event TIME, PIXEL, ITYPE, PI, DETX, DETY and the original GTIs in deterministic compact files for offline replay; ensure selected primary counts agree exactly with the original bytes.

Terminology correction after review: the original frozen protocol used “livetime” for summed GTI duration. This is good-time exposure, without an independent deadtime correction. The test, energy bands, split and counts are unchanged. Deadtime and grade energy dependence have not been measured.
