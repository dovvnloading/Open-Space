# Can lunar impact flashes independently date LROC craters?

The observational unit is a crater in the LROC new-crater catalogue paired with an Earth-based optical flash. A possible common impact must satisfy a before/after image time bracket and a selenographic angular separation compatible with the flash localization. Check known flash/crater pairs as positive controls, rank all remaining matches, and compare to displaced or permuted flashes before interpreting coincidences. The craters and flashes were measured by different instruments. A result needs the exact records, catalogue versions, competing associations and prior identifications.

The default-branch result names and open PRs were checked before selection. At selection PR #84 covered S Virginis and WD 0140+130. This search uses neither target.
