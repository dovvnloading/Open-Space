## Ground-based localization of the 2023 December eruption

ZTF public difference images independently identify which object brightened in the TESS aperture. Across 18 distinct exposures on six nights, the median difference centroid is at ICRS (89.6894685°, +39.25916485°). Gaia DR3 3458275544681382912, the MASTER OT counterpart, lies **0.0064 arcsec** away after propagating its proper motion from J2016.0 to the median ZTF epoch (MJD 60294.3373). The closest alternative in the Gaia DR3 cone lies 3.819 arcsec away. The eclipsing-binary catalog source Gaia DR3 3458275643465151744 lies 20.915 arcsec away. The median radial scatter of the individual centroids is 0.037 arcsec and the maximum is 0.137 arcsec. The 0.0064-arcsec residual describes the coordinate agreement. It is smaller than the single-exposure scatter and the measurement does not establish absolute astrometry at that precision.
