# Disposition of persistent narrow residuals

The search preserves the initial threshold crossings. A wavelength coincidence with a catalogued stellar transition or a terrestrial line excludes that window from the clean-line sample. It does not establish which process contributed all of its photons. A line left after the measured vetoes requires a separately documented astrophysical and instrumental review before it qualifies for follow-up.

## Coordinates and controls

DESI DR1 uses vacuum wavelengths in the Solar System barycentric frame. For each camera/exposure, divide the measured centroid by the native cframe header's multiplicative `HELIOCOR` to compare with sky and lamp atlases. Divide instead by the Doppler factor of that exposure's stellar fit for stellar transitions. The conservative rest-frame window has half-width equal to one local instrumental FWHM combined in quadrature with three formal stellar RV errors mapped into Å. The sky window has half-width one local instrumental FWHM in the observatory frame. Directly adjacent fibre numbers on the same 500-trace spectrograph test slit contamination; the sky controls are the three nearest usable sky assignments by great-circle distance on the focal plane. These selections can overlap. [DESI DR1](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/releases/dr1/) · [desispec frame convention](https://desispec.readthedocs.io/en/latest/_modules/desispec/heliocentric.html) · [DESI slit numbering](https://desimodel.readthedocs.io/en/0.18.0/_modules/desimodel/inputs/fiberpos.html)

The cframe check uses each fibre's own resolution column at the detected subpixel wavelength. It fits calibrated flux with an integrated line plus an affine continuum and retains its formal standard error. It skips unusable sky controls, records their separation, flags and identity, and continues outward to three usable sky fibres on the same camera. A wavelength bin of zero inverse variance or nonzero mask in the profile core makes the forced measurement unavailable. A neighbouring trace with a nonzero FIBERSTATUS remains flagged. Each frame JSON contains the source URL, file size, survey-published SHA-256 and checksum-manifest hash. A raw-spectrum line fit is local spectrophotometry on the reduced spectrum. The separate stellar-residual fit also subtracts a model.

## False-positive catalogue

| Mechanism | Measured screen and further discriminants |
|---|---|
| Cosmic ray, charge trap, bad column, persistence | Track masks, fibre status, EXPID and repeat nights. Compare the narrow line shape against both an instrumental LSF and a one-pixel spike. For any remaining feature examine preprocessed 2-D CCD cutouts, trace illumination and unrelated targets on the same detector coordinate. A repeated extraction of one exposure supplies one trial. |
| OH airglow, [O I], Na, Hg street lamps, telluric residuals | Compare exposure-specific topocentric wavelengths against 274 bright empirical Kitt Peak lines and 7 lamp transitions. Test independent local sky residuals, including both signs, per exposure. At a preselected centroid, any absolute sky S/N ≥4 is a shared-residual veto. If the sky's absolute fitted flux plus three errors can reach the stellar residual, the sky check is underpowered. Review sky models and telluric bands for anything surviving. The empirical list omits weak lines. |
| Cross-talk and fibre leakage | Force the same line on both physically adjacent traces within the same spectrograph, save their fibre IDs, fluxes and S/N, and retain unavailable traces as such. Use trace separation and an exposure-specific upper bound on bright-neighbour coupling if a possible contaminant remains. Sky-coordinate distance cannot establish slit adjacency. |
| Template mismatch or stellar activity | Compare observed and model spectra separately with the local continuum. A shallower stellar absorption line than the fitted model generates a positive residual. Compare atomic transitions and controls of similar spectral type. Check Hα/Hβ, higher Balmer lines, Ca II H/K and IR triplet, He I, flare colours and visit-to-visit profiles for active late-type stars. Discuss molecular bandheads and activity where appropriate. |
| H II, planetary nebulae, Be stars, Wolf–Rayet stars, symbiotics | Check Hα, [O III], [N II], [S II] and companion lines at the stellar or local gas velocity; the velocities can differ. Look for extended emission across nearby fibres and in images. Emission-star checks include Balmer wings, He I/II and C/N wind complexes, as well as high-ionization and Raman bands with a cool stellar continuum for symbiotics. A class label alone cannot exclude a secondary emitter. |
| Background galaxy or QSO | For [O II] components and their nominal blend, [O III], Hβ, Hα and Lyα, solve for redshift, project companion wavelengths, and match against shape-compatible positive triggers in accessible good bins. `adjudicate_hits.py` records each possible redshift and matching ≥7σ companions. Follow up viable cases with measured upper limits, doublet ratio/shape, imaging/aperture alignment and Lyα asymmetry or dropout. A missing companion beyond coverage or below the local limit cannot clear a one-line galaxy. |
| Steady terrestrial line | Compare stellar-rest, barycentric and topocentric scatter and contemporaneous unrelated fibres. Close visits can have indistinguishable barycentric corrections. Report the leverage and retain ambiguity where the change is sub-resolution. |

DESI documents its sky subtraction, cosmic-ray masks, cross-talk and CCD calibration in the pipeline and release papers. [Guy et al.](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/acb212) · [DESI DR1](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/ae4c43) · [Bit masks](https://desidatamodel.readthedocs.io/en/stable/bitmasks.html)

## Reference lines

- `kpno_sky_vacuum.txt` contains the 274 topocentric vacuum centroids returned by DESI's `get_sky_lines`. The copied numeric centroids agree with the Git blob `8bb54c2b508dc82b0c532aee371ff252fd7a5606`. The range is 4359.55–9819.99 Å. The supplement for individual lamp lines uses the NIST neutral mercury and sodium tables. [desispec](https://desispec.readthedocs.io/en/latest/_modules/desispec/sky.html) · [NIST Hg](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/mercurytable2_a.htm) · [NIST Na](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/sodiumtable2_a.htm)
- `rest_lines.csv` is the initial screening list of 49 laboratory or representative blend centroids. Entries tagged fastspecfit come from the DESI FastSpecFit vacuum-rest catalogue, Git blob `94e9843412cd7d3af6c84737433b514dccaac226`, deduplicating the broad-line components. SDSS supplies Ca II, and NIST supplies additional Na I and He I references. For our optical NIST entries, `air_to_vacuum` inverts the vacuum-to-air formula tabulated by SDSS. A small reference list cannot exclude all natural transitions. [FastSpecFit](https://github.com/desihub/fastspecfit/blob/main/py/fastspecfit/data/emlines.ecsv) · [SDSS reference table](https://www.sdss2.org/dr3/algorithms/linestable.php) · [SDSS air conversion](https://classic.sdss.org/dr5/products/spectra/vacwavelength.php) · [NIST He](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/heliumtable3.htm)
- The Li I doublet emerged in the discovery data. Its 6707.775 and 6707.926 Å air components convert to 6709.6302 and 6709.7812 Å vacuum. They are stored separately in `POST_INSPECTION`; the original candidate list records the search before this identification. `reference_mask(..., include_post_inspection=False)` recovers the original line selection. [NIST Li](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/lithiumtable3.htm)

The DESI DR1 MWS value-added catalogue lists 4,586,247 unique spectroscopically classified stars and 1,718,305 Gaia sources with multiple RV/stellar parameter measurements. Report actual searched stars, distinct exposures and accessible wavelength intervals separately. DESI spectroscopy integrates photons across an exposure; a recovered signal constrains its exposure-averaged flux. A pulse must contribute sufficient fluence in every required exposure for this persistence selection. [DESI MWS catalogue](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/releases/dr1/vac/mws/)

## Reproduction

Run `python observe_controls.py SCAN_DIR FRAME_OUTPUT --reuse` following the native spectral scan, then `python adjudicate_hits.py SCAN_DIR FRAME_OUTPUT DESI_INPUT_CACHE candidates.jsonl`. Keep the raw `rvtab_spectra-*.fits` files in the input cache for the formal per-visit velocity errors. The compact adjudication lists each positive's redshift alternatives, sky and rest windows, shape statistic and unavailable checks. Per-frame JSON files contain full sky and slit measurements. No stronger astrophysical disposition follows from a match alone. Additional review of a surviving candidate can be recorded by `vetting.py` with status, reason and a relative evidence-file path and SHA-256 for each review. Reports without a verifiable evidence file remain pending. `python -m unittest test_vetting` checks masking, independent controls, redshift projection and missing evidence on synthetic fixtures.
