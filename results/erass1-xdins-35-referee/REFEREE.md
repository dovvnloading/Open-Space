# Independent evidence-level referee — eRASS1 35-source image follow-up

**Verdict (19 November 2026 UTC): accept only as a reproducible, incomplete follow-up record; reject discovery, negative-population, and complete physical-classification claims.** This is a separate adversarial reading of the committed source tables and requests, not independent new telescope data. I recomputed the 35-object and 6/29 partitions from [source_evidence.json](output/source_evidence.json), compared the photographic receipts and per-source optical ratios with [classifications.csv](output/classifications.csv), and counted archive matches directly from each HEASARC search response.

## Checks against the discovery hypothesis

1. **Frozen, identical targets.** [summary.json](output/summary.json) verifies 35 unique UIDs from [PR #204](../erass1-xdins-search/README.md). Its SHA-256 receipts identify `official_top60.csv` as `5092c7f0f14d87b494ceb94673eb621486e3ed2345d7456becaf5ea08f69ca6b` and `screened_main.csv` as `a4d3810b0ac4c367e08442a5f64733b5c5929dfa2f67d59d07e797fbfea0f9c2`. The requirement of "no secure association" uses a statistical NWAY threshold; it is **not** a 5σ blank-sky observation.
2. **Six known INS, not six new XDINS.** The original four Magnificent Seven in this subset (RX J0720, RX J0806, RX J1308, RX J1856) are all known, while the downloaded [ATNF](output/atnf_receipts.json) source file (SHA-256 `55daa29f71f344c8de4064969b5812d6cb3a9b851efae0bef004ac9d5f6740c0`, **4,262** parseable positions) identifies **PSR J0659+1414** at 2.13 arcsec and **PSR J1057−5226** at 2.57 arcsec. The latter two are established rotation-powered radio pulsars, not classic X-ray-dim neutron-star discoveries; [SIMBAD B0656+14](https://simbad.harvard.edu/simbad/sim-id?Ident=PSR+B0656%2B14) and [B1055−52](https://simbad.harvard.edu/simbad/sim-id?Ident=PSR+B1055-52) independently list their old names. The other 29 are unknown; no credible AGN/star/cluster/spurious class is justified merely by eROSITA softness.
3. **Position/extent.** The published eROSITA POS_ERR is **0.944–4.447 arcsec**. Catalogue EXT=0 and EXT_LIKE≤6 come from PR #204's fixed point-like gate; this result did not fit eROSITA photon images or independently reject source splitting, diffuse emission and spurious PSF detections. The very soft band count ratio is **not** a 40–120 eV response-folded blackbody fit or likelihood comparison to absorbed AGN/coronal models.
4. **Direct-image evidence and limits.** All **35** received native unWISE W1/W2 cutouts with image-data SHA-256 and position-forced aperture data. Only **10** have interpretable optical image-position photometry from LS/PS1 and only **7** give provisional g 5σ nondetection limits. DES SIA queries returned no usable image for these positions; PS1 stacks are unavailable south of -30° for 31 of 35; multiple LS viewer calls failed HTTP 500/429 or lacked interpretable pixels. A failure is **not** a non-detection. All unWISE apertures are marked for possible source crowding, and the optical aperture noise/background/zeropoint treatment is insufficient to certify deep completeness.
5. **f_X/fopt gate fails.** Independently reading the seven numerical source records gives provisional observed-band ratios `1480, 845.8, 243.4, 162.8, 87.3, 21.4, 6.5`, with maximum **1480**, not ≥10⁴. Optical-band νfν is calculated from the image AB limit; this is not a g=V Maccacaro substitution. However, the eRASS catalogue f_X uses a nominal spectral conversion, and optical PSF, blended wings, Galactic reddening and depth calibration are not independently validated. **Zero physically certified ratio lower bounds >10⁴** (not a demonstration that all 29 fail).
6. **Archived detections are only associations.** The official NASA HEASARC XMMSSC 4XMM-DR14 response returned positional matches for **20** of 35 source coordinates, Chandra CSC **8**, and Swift 2SXPS **7**, using up to 12 arcsec matching radii. Their source identifiers, separations and response hashes are in [archive_receipts.json](output/archive_receipts.json); these archives are not negative when no entry is returned. No pointed-epoch event lists, spectra, flux histories or long-term stability statistics were reduced, and chance/confused positional links remain possible. A catalogue match is not a period or variability detection.
7. **Extinction versus distance.** All **35** [IRSA reddening](output/image_receipts.json) queries now have pixel-value E(B−V) estimates in **0.0241–3.4908 mag**. The `8.8e21 * E(B−V)` neutral-hydrogen proxy is a *modelled* full-column gas estimate, not a spectrum-fitted N_H; high dust towards Magellanic fields may bias this interpretation. The per-source observed f_X-derived L_X at 0.3, 1 and 50 kpc are **hypotheses**, not measured distances. A background LMC association must not be assumed merely from coordinates.
8. **Novelty unresolved.** A current ATNF position check excludes obvious named known pulsars. The full [Kurpas et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202450248) table of **33 previously reported candidates** could not be retrieved (HTTP 404; [receipt](output/literature_receipts.json)); the 2025–26 literature search is not exhaustively matched to all 29. No novelty certificate can issue. The [2026 reported XDINS](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202659673) is already published and cannot count as a present new discovery.

## Source-level decision and disposition

Exactly **six "known INS"** (four classic XDINS plus two radio pulsars); **29 "unclassified pending physical vetting"**; **zero supported new INS candidates**, and **zero evidence-backed new AGN/star/cluster/spurious labels** for those 29. See [all 35 CSV rows](output/classifications.csv). This is a bounded lack of confirmed discoveries, **not** a complete negative survey. Native blackbody/power-law/coronal spectral response fitting, background/event-image QC, extinction-corrected PSF limits, X-ray variability, distance constraints and full prior-publication comparison remain necessary before promoting any new XDINS.
