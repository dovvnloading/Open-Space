# IMAP magnetic depletion and proton pressure: pre-science protocol

Status: synthetic and metadata only. No IMAP magnetic-field or SWAPI physical values have been read for this protocol. Question: for the first eligible five-minute magnetic-strength depletion in the first 12 UTC hours on 2026-07-31, is proton scalar thermal pressure higher during the same interval than in its two adjacent five-minute controls? The observing unit is one prespecified five-minute event plus ten minutes of bracketing controls, not individual samples. A single result does not establish a population, a novel phenomenon, total pressure balance, mirror instability or reconnection. Solar-wind magnetic holes and pressure responses have longstanding literature, e.g. https://doi.org/10.1029/2006JA012116 . Review target-specific prior work before any interpretation.

The 1 nT and 20% gates are deliberately large engineering thresholds, not false-alarm probabilities or certified accuracy bounds.
