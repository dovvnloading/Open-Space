These are observed 0.5–7 keV aperture and annulus counts. The gross hard fraction conditions on the detected counts in each region; its two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson interval uses the binomial counts. Intervals are per bin. Empty bins have no fraction or interval. The fractions include background and energy-dependent detector response. They cannot establish the source distance, absorption or a Galactic origin. The chosen field and binning are follow-up characterization; original instrument, 24-control and held-out results are unchanged.
