# Conditional VAST DR1 repeatability check for three TDE-candidate associations

**AT2020nov did not cross the frozen two-session rise screen.** Its two held-out session comparisons were −0.705 and −0.057 model standard deviations relative to its development baseline. The known radio-variable control AT2018hyz crossed the same screen (3.342 and 4.111); the other known control, ASASSN-15oi, was below its baseline (−2.637 and −6.065). These are conditional comparisons of catalog peak fluxes, not calibrated physical significances or evidence of a new source.

The 134-row TDECat literature compilation provided 103 events discovered before 2023-06-01; it is heterogeneous and mostly optical, not a complete uniform spectroscopic sample. Three positions have catalog associations within the broad search radius. The other 100 are catalog nonmatches, not measured nondetections; coverage, classification and source ownership remain unresolved. AT2018hyz and ASASSN-15oi are previously reported radio-variable TDE controls. AT2020nov already has VLA radio detections, which Cendes et al. interpret as likely host star formation; Anumarlapudi et al. discuss a competing AGN-like interpretation. The same source has previously appeared in RACS, VAST and VLASS context. The 2025 VAST epochs are not assumed independent of any published measurements without an observation-level crossmatch. [TDECat](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202554882) · [VLA study](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10563410) · [RACS TDE study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12097) · [Literature receipt and limits](evidence/at2020nov-literature.md)

For each source the 2023–2025 VAST DR1 measurements were split by a fixed 24-hour session rule before flux access; the final two distinct sessions were held out. In particular, AT2020nov's last four images form two pairs, not four independent epochs. The statistic for session j is `(flux_j − baseline) / sqrt(variance_j + baseline_variance)`, with a crossing only if both z values are ≥3. The future flux and its variance were not fixed before holdout. [Frozen development protocol and metadata](../vast-tde-expanded/CONDITIONAL-DESIGN.md) · [Holdout protocol and exact baselines](../vast-tde-holdout/README.md)

Controls were selected from fixed local metadata pools using development observations only. The released quality selections, finite/error/RMS/beam validity checks, 20-control per-image minimum and 3% gain floor were fixed before processing. A session with multiple overlapping images does not gain independent-sample precision. The known controls strongly violate a constant model (χ²/dof 385.6/8 and 264.9/8); the model variance was inflated as specified. The 3% floor, cross-session calibration and control-selection covariance, forced-fit behavior and Gaussian tails remain uncalibrated. Positive finite beam axes do not establish PSF matching. No source-resolved radio image or independent instrument was used to establish ownership or a physical origin.

A predeclared development-only pseudo-test used 20 SHA-ranked control positions per field. Only 1/20 in AT2020nov's field had both pseudo-test sessions; 19/20 and 20/20 did in the two control fields. At +0, +0.5, +1 and +2 mJy/beam the crossings were 0/1, 0/1, 0/1, 0/1; 0/19, 2/19, 9/19, 17/19; and 0/20, 4/20, 16/20, 18/20. These share the data and calibration and do not calibrate an independent false-alarm rate. An initial implementation passed extra control rows and was rejected by the phase guard; that failed run is preserved alongside the corrected run. An earlier pre-flux manifest also failed byte-identity review because `.gitattributes` had different checkout line endings; the packet was corrected and re-reviewed before any flux was returned. [Pseudo-test history](../vast-tde-holdout/README.md) · [Initial failure](evidence/pseudo-initial-failed.json) · [Corrected tests](evidence/pseudo-final.json) · [Failed manifest](evidence/failed-preflight-manifest.json.gz)

The protocol freezes, review manifests and authorizations were local workspace records. They were not externally registered or cryptographically timestamped, so their hashes establish byte identity and recorded local sequence rather than independent proof of when a prediction was made.

## Offline evidence

From the repository root, `python results/vast-tde-repeatability/replay.py` runs the original frozen development and holdout arithmetic against the deposited compact rows with NumPy; it verifies input projection and baseline hashes, row selection, drops, frozen controls, gains and outputs to 1e−10 relative/absolute tolerance. [Projection manifest](evidence/projection-manifest.json) records six compressed row subsets. They contain all metadata-pool rows from the authorized development images and only frozen-control/target rows from the authorized holdout images, including rejected rows and their reasons. These projections were created **after unblinding** as a reproduction aid, not as an independent observation or a preregistered input. No network or native Parquet is needed for replay.

An independent native-input audit compared all 5,618 projected rows with the hash-verified Parquet and image metadata. Separate development and holdout implementations independently checked the same observations; they are arithmetic and lineage checks, not independent astronomical measurements. [Projection audit](evidence/independent-projection-check.json) · [Audit code](evidence/independent-projection-check.py) · [Independent development](evidence/independent-development.json) · [Development code](evidence/independent-development.py) · [Independent holdout](evidence/independent-holdout.json) · [Holdout code](evidence/independent-holdout.py)

The offline replay also decompresses and verifies the original failed-preflight manifest hash and head. It checks the old Git blob and a reconstructed checkout whose final line alone has CRLF: that reconstructed byte sequence matches the old recorded checkout hash and normalizes to the old blob. This documents the historical EOL-only mismatch; the failed manifest is not a current checksum assertion.

The projections derive from the public VAST Extragalactic DR1 measurement Parquet SHA-256 `a3af873784b4b3d5a727d6d50890a48a5fd1bbc41ebe035106ce83d47cfb9ca6` and image table SHA-256 `70ce692b3c8d067d53e248b6265420a26880147bd7bc15327930880bd8395195`. Native bytes, relevant file/observation identity and calibrated imaging must be independently checked to verify provenance and source association. The archive access and filtering can physically decode full Parquet column chunks; image and source predicates controlled returned rows, not physical bytes. [Public VAST release](https://doi.org/10.25919/nh9d-t846) · [Release paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22739)

The complete stage outputs, authorizations and manifests are in [evidence](evidence/); the original f323 development and 2ca holdout code is unchanged in the linked stage directories. This is a bounded, conditional result. It does not yield a population rate, a physical nonvariability limit or a new TDE association.
