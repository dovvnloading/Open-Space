# AT2020nov radio literature check — 2026-11-17

Scope: published primary sources and fixed development baseline; no VAST heldout rows or native pixels. AT2020nov = ZTF20abisysx; frozen VAST association source 14043503, separation 0.7237248 arcsec. Association is positional, not proof of ownership.

Cendes et al. 2024 https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10563410 (download cendes2024.pdf SHA256 4486db0c61c10c1faabf2cdb9e78678a6e89e3a14e5a8f98be5ff8057a9db677). Their all-other-radio-observations table records VLA 20A-372: 2020-10-16, 15 GHz, 0.224 ± 0.009 mJy; 2021-02-10, 6 GHz, 0.376 ± 0.010 mJy; 2021-02-28, 6 GHz, 0.370 ± 0.010 mJy. VLA 22B-205: 2022-11-13, 6 GHz, 0.366 ± 0.019 mJy. Their text instead reports 0.39 ± 0.02 mJy for the latter epoch; this internal mismatch is unresolved. Per-epoch restoring beams and exact component positions were not established from this article, so cannot claim matched resolution or identify the source centroid precisely. They attribute the 6 GHz near-constancy through 2022 to likely non-TDE host emission such as star formation.

Earl et al. 2025 https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/501422/1/2412.12991v2.pdf (earl2025.pdf SHA256 3f37d3190133561593d3de11bd70fedf3ed3c09d852feacf066a72ef69cb1eb1) repeats Cendes 2024 radio history and the host-star-formation interpretation; this is not an independent radio measurement. Optical/X-ray classification still admits caveats.

Anumarlapudi et al. 2024 https://inspirehep.net/files/a7ab0e07bf1497396408827056c5ad7d, ApJ 974 241, https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ad64d3: Fig. 8 already shows AT2020nov with RACS, VAST, VLASS; authors report little variability, with first RACS-low ~400 days before optical discovery, and signed spectral index −0.64 ± 0.04 from RACS/VLASS/Cendes. Their printed convention S_nu proportional to nu^(-alpha) conflicts in sign with their quoted negative value; do not silently resolve. They favor AGN-like emission; this differs from Cendes/Earl host-star-formation attribution. Their survey table quotes typical RACS-low ~15 arcsec, mid ~10 arcsec, high ~8 arcsec, VLASS ~2.5 arcsec; those are survey-level figures, not per-observation VLA beams. The detailed dates and source ownership of plotted VAST points were not verified; a 2024 publication cannot settle 2025 coverage. Do not assume plotted epochs are independent of current products.

Development 0.888 GHz source peak mean 1.847493996 mJy/beam is not directly comparable to integrated 6 GHz flux: spectra, beam, extraction, epochs, blends and calibration differ. Purely illustrative same-component and same-epoch power-law alpha = log(1.84749/0.366)/log(0.888/6) = −0.847, with peak/integrated equivalence assumed; it is not a measured spectral index or evidence for a rise. The known radio source and non-TDE host alternatives suffice to make this value non-surprising; they do not quantify the host contribution.

Needed to assess physical association: a pre-specified same-frequency, same-component, beam-matched historical versus later measurement with local stable controls, full covariance and realistic background/beam systematics; inspect morphology and registration against host nucleus and neighboring radio components; simultaneous or well-controlled multifrequency spectrum; independent higher-resolution radio epoch and host/star-formation/AGN counterfactual. A radio rise alone establishes neither TDE ownership nor physical class.
