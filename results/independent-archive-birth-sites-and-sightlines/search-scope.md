# Independent archive search

The initial inventory used main at `ebbe3f8488467b270b69d8c1fe93f9c516674f3f`, its results directories, active branches and open PR #84. The 16-hour variable and Mira investigations are reserved by other threads.

Primary search: can a young pulsar’s measured proper motion trace it to a shell-type supernova remnant? Seek a close passage at a physically plausible epoch. Test competing distances, astrometric errors, shuffled directions, and previously reported associations. Catalog centers and characteristic ages are proxies.

Independent screens cover intervening quasar absorbers across adjacent lines of sight, binary-star information from historical asteroid occultations, and multispacecraft solar ejecta. Each requires actual observations, a counterexample or control, and an explicit prior-report check before attributing novelty. This checkpoint records the search space before selecting candidates.
