# Independent archival findings

| Investigation | Measured result | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [J0108 foreground absorption](../mgii-j0108-environment/README.md) | Three strong Mg II doublets span 2.544 proper Mpc and 192.7 km/s at z ≈ 0.960. An earlier SDSS plate reproduces all six lines. Five galaxies occupy the same redshift slice. | **Exploratory selection**, not a confirmed common gas structure: 49 of 5,000 regional sky shuffles produced a comparable triplet. Separate galaxy halos could produce the pattern; host assignments and gas connectivity are unresolved. |
| [TYC 557-1335-1](../tyc-557-1335-1/README.md) | The projected separation resolved in a 2022 occultation is at least 4.70 au at nominal parallax. Producing it with the published 59.392-day orbit at measured eccentricity requires at least 3,080 solar masses. | **Unconfirmed third-star candidate.** The weak secondary occultation dip was recorded at only one station (two full video exposures), and the 59-day Gaia spectroscopic orbit rests on 11 usable radial velocities. Neither the third star nor a physical hierarchy is established. |
| [Solar-wind polarity, 31 January 2019](../l1-lunar-polarity-split/README.md) | ACE and DSCOVR saw northward field while Wind saw southward field for a full hour. Two lunar probes measured the northward state in the subsequent hour. | Five-spacecraft local-field measurement. The lunar probes were on the **dawn side**, not at Earth's bow-shock nose; the actual nose field is **undetermined**. Wind-derived OMNI is a propagated reconstruction. |
| [Pulsar shell back-traces](../pulsar-shell-backtraces/README.md) | Four exterior tracks passed the geometric cuts. Two recover known associations, one conflicts with independent distances and another is sensitive to motion errors. | No new birthplace established. |
