# Independent archival findings

| Investigation | Measured result | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [J0108 foreground absorption](../mgii-j0108-environment/README.md) | Three strong Mg II doublets span 2.544 proper Mpc and 192.7 km/s at z ≈ 0.960. An earlier SDSS plate reproduces all six lines. Five galaxies occupy the same redshift slice. | Candidate shared galaxy environment. The measurements leave the connectivity and host galaxies of the gas open. |
| [TYC 557-1335-1](../tyc-557-1335-1/README.md) | The projected separation resolved in a 2022 occultation is at least 4.70 au at nominal parallax. Producing it with the published 59.392-day orbit at measured eccentricity requires at least 3,080 solar masses. | Candidate third stellar component, conditional on the occultation geometry and Gaia radial-velocity orbit. |
| [Solar-wind polarity, 31 January 2019](../l1-lunar-polarity-split/README.md) | ACE and DSCOVR saw northward field while Wind saw southward field for a full hour. Two lunar probes measured the northward state in the subsequent hour. | Five-spacecraft measurement. Field at the terrestrial bow-shock nose remains undetermined. |
| [Pulsar shell back-traces](../pulsar-shell-backtraces/README.md) | Four exterior tracks passed the geometric cuts. Two recover known associations, one conflicts with independent distances and another is sensitive to motion errors. | No new birthplace established. |
