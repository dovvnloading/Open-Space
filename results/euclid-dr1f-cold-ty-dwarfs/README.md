# Euclid DR1-Foundation: full Wide Survey cold T/Y dwarf screen

**Result — no newly validated sub-500 K brown dwarf or cooler-than-WISE-0855 object.** We independently scanned all **768/768** Euclid DR1-Foundation Wide MER HATS partitions (**888,152,165/888,152,165** published rows), retained **647,197** strict VIS-dropout/near-IR-colour catalogue rows, and independently replayed their frozen per-partition receipts. The colour screen recovers only **2 of 20 catalogue-matched known late-T/Y dwarfs** and also accepts **3 of 24 catalogue-matched known high-redshift quasars**. It is demonstrably incomplete and impure. Its **250** shortlisted source entries are *unconfirmed photometric priorities, not 250 new brown dwarfs*. Among the **top 40** with external counterpart requests, **zero** have a validated motion-propagated CatWISE match supporting a new, very red W1−W2 moving source. The remaining 210 have not received that follow-up. **No WISE-like spectral subtype, effective temperature, photometric distance, or novelty was established for any new source.**

This is an independent **Euclid-first, new-data** search, not a repeat of [cold-neighbours-motion-selected](../cold-neighbours-motion-selected/README.md), which was a WISE motion-first survey. The [independent referee](REFEREE.md) accepts the frozen catalogue-screening measurement, **not** a negative population census or a discovery. No `[REMARKABLE]` label or [remarkable-findings log](../../docs/REMARKABLE_FINDINGS.md) entry is justified.

## Frozen release and measured selection

- **Input:** public **12 November 2026 Euclid DR1-Foundation**, IRSA **Wide Survey MER HATS**, [DOI 10.26131/IRSA673](https://doi.org/10.26131/IRSA673). Frozen partition index SHA-256 `f7940bcaed19b6f67295c5592cc81783d798c0f858062d7293e5e3dfbfa57c2a`. The [previous wide-scan S3/Parquet row-group reader](../euclid-dr1-wide-highz-vetting/scan.py) supplies data access, but [selection.py](selection.py) implements an independently frozen cold-dwarf mask with a separate output root. **32 sequential 24-partition batches** commit object keys, ETags (not whole-file SHA-256), row-group/selection counts, per-control matches, gzip selected rows and SHA-256 digests. Source data are read on networked GitHub Actions runners, not in a local networkless Python sandbox.
- **Mask:** VIS_DET=0 and |VIS aperture S/N| < 2; J≥6σ; clean J/H flags; NISP ellipticity <0.25 and spurious probability <0.5 where finite; **Y−J ≥0.35 (AB)** and **J−H ≤0.15 (AB)**, using matched 3-FWHM apertures. Y under 2σ uses a conservative *lower limit* on Y−J; H under 2σ uses a conservative *upper limit* on J−H. No VIS-based POINT_LIKE_PROB is treated as a NISP point-source classifier. Ellipticity is an **unvalidated PSF proxy**.
- **Frozen output counts:** **5,211,704** pass the broader mask; **647,197** pass strict selection. Of strict rows, **421,494** use a Y colour limit and **312,203** an H colour limit; **368,529** have extremely blue selected J−H < −1.4, a serious artefact warning. **66,837** have >3σ positive optical flux in at least one linked ground-survey MER band (a screening diagnostic, not individual association proof). An exploratory colder-priority colour + optical screen leaves **499,194**, before a heuristic 250-object bounded shortlist. These large figures are *source-row selections*, not a population of T/Y objects.
- **Imaged area is not measured:** the nominal roughly 1,900 deg² Euclid footprint and HATS cells cannot replace per-source coverage, quality masks or an injection–recovery selection function. We claim **no full-footprint recovery rate**, cold-dwarf space-density constraint, or meaningful upper limit.

We propagated known proper motions to approximate NISP epoch **2024.25** and searched 3 arcsec. The epoch is not measured separately for each MER tile.

**Positive cold controls:** published **WISEA J021420.18−573245.1 (T8.5)** and **WISEA J030237.52−581740.3 (Y0)** pass. CatWISE mirror probes independently associate each at propagated-epoch separations **0.526″** and **0.996″**, with W1−W2(Vega) **3.046** and **3.093 mag**, respectively; the T8.5 control shows a 6.74σ motion component, the Y0 a 1.12σ component. Both validate infrared counterpart access, *not* universal motion recovery. The other **236** frozen cold controls lack a 3″ catalogue match; sky/imaging coverage is **unknown** rather than 236 recorded missed detections. The spectroscopic-quasar contamination and low late-T/Y recovery **falsify any purity or completeness inference**. Colder Y dwarfs need not follow these simple near-IR colours: methane and evolving Y-band spectra can move objects *outside* this two-colour box.

## Counterparts, novelty, and physical parameters

After ranking **250** photometric entries against the frozen literature positions, **40** priority positions received CatWISE2020, unWISE, Legacy Surveys DR10, Pan-STARRS DR2 and SIMBAD mirror queries. [Per-object responses, returned coordinates and hashes](output/external_receipts.json) retain failures as failures. At the two recovered known cold controls, [explicit CatWISE positive probes](output/wise_control_probes.json) succeeded.

- **CatWISE2020:** IRSA TAP initially returned HTTP 502 for all 40 positions; a retry at the NOIRLab Astro Data Lab mirror returned **35** cones with catalogue rows and **5** cones with no rows. **0 of 40** provide a ≤2.5″ motion-propagated CatWISE match to the Euclid epoch, so **no new lead** passes the joint W1−W2 red-colour + significant motion requirement. Returned nearby stationary catalogues are not silently adopted as candidate counterparts. The 15″ cone at the assumed epoch also lacks completeness for extreme proper motions.
- **unWISE:** all **40/40** attempted initial IRSA queries failed (HTTP 502), and mirror probes using full and reduced ADQL projections returned service-error VOTABLEs. **There is no defensible unWISE forced-flux measurement, W1/W2 limit or independent IR confirmation.** [First-pass](output/first_pass_irsa_proxy_failures.json) and [second-pass](output/second_pass_unwise_schema_failures.json) errors remain frozen. CatWISE and unWISE share WISE photons anyway.
- **Optical:** Legacy returned catalogue rows in 9/40 cones, with 5 ≤3″ matches; Pan-STARRS returned rows in 5/40, all 5 position-matched. Object-specific image non-detection limits and possible optical associations remain unverified. A catalog-empty response is **not** a calibrated optical upper limit.
- **Literature:** the dated Best/Kirkpatrick/Backyard Worlds position comparison yields **0 exact near-epoch matches among the 250 highest-ranked rows**; this does **not** make them 250 novel dwarfs. SIMBAD mirrors returned no rows for 40/40 queried leads but may be lagged or miss high-PM records. Full November 2026 literature completeness is not established.
- **Temperatures and distances:** [reference_calibration.json](output/reference_calibration.json) computes empirical published-parallax W2 subtype anchors (median M_W2 Vega: T8 **13.615**, T9 **14.046**, Y0 **14.812**, Y1 **15.544**, with scatter and sample counts). These are **conditional absolute-magnitude reference values**, not a validated Euclid Y−J/J−H-to-Teff relation. No new candidate satisfies the prerequisite confirmed IR identity/subtype, so its **Teff and photometric distance are withheld**. Nothing is shown to be cooler than [WISE 0855−0714](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2014ApJ...783...73L/abstract), nor proven below 500 K.

Actual Euclid VIS/NISP exposure-level images, point-spread-function separation from galaxies, persistence/edge/blend checks, multiple-epoch astrometry, spectroscopic subtype and a held-out proper-motion detection are **not completed**. Therefore **0 validated new cold brown dwarfs**, **0 demonstrated below 500 K**, and **0 WISE 0855 challengers**, but no full-survey null limit.
