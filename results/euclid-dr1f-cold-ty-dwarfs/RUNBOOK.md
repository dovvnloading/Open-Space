# Reproduction and checkpoint contract

Branch: `research/euclid-dr1f-cold-ty-dwarfs`. One push-triggered, branch-scoped GitHub Actions workflow: `.github/workflows/euclid-dr1f-cold-ty-dwarfs.yml`. Path filter only `results/euclid-dr1f-cold-ty-dwarfs/**`, excluding `output/**`, documentation-only edits. No repository-wide PR-triggered data jobs were added.

1. The networked Actions runner runs `bootstrap.py` to freeze three published literature lists and their SHA-256 digests, plus spectroscopic quasar controls. It imports the older Euclid reader for the frozen wide S3 index; all science selection and object classification are new.
2. Every `selection.py` call streams **24** complete HATS leaf partitions, preserving the publisher object key, ETag, index digest, Parquet row-group count, per-partition control matches, selected gzip JSONL rows and a packet SHA-256. It checks selection source fingerprints on resume. 32 batches cover 768 HATS partitions. No partial-run denominator may be called the full release.
3. The one job commits checkpoints in batches from the runner, using `github-actions[bot]`; `output/**` is excluded from its own push trigger. If the 300-minute budget is reached, it tries a sequential workflow dispatch; if dispatch is unavailable, a *new operator dispatch* is needed. A failed partition fails closed, retaining the partial checkpoint.
4. Upon full completion, `aggregate.py` replays all 32 SHA-256 packet receipts, computes full-row and control denominators, and stores a bounded follow-up shortlist. `vet.py` queries a maximum of 40 objects through CatWISE2020/unWISE, Legacy, Pan-STARRS, and SIMBAD; service failures are never treated as upper limits. `referee.py` independently rechecks every selected row, frozen packet hash, partition uniqueness, quasar-control contamination, and the claim ceiling.
5. Read `output/summary.json`, `output/controls_recovery.json`, `output/vetting_status.json`, `output/referee.json`; inspect `output/external_receipts.json` for each returned coordinate, response digest and error. No image-pixel provenance or effective-area measurement is inferred from catalogue coverage.

Scientific limitations: the Euclid epoch 2024.25 is an approximation rather than each tile's observation date; CatWISE positions are at epoch 2015.405 and the 15-arcsec query radius misses extremely high-proper-motion objects. Literature compilations are dated and not exhaustive through November 2026. The NISP ellipticity proxy is not PSF-based point-source classification. CatWISE/unWISE share WISE photons, not independent observations. Temperature and distance brackets are conditional on published-parallax subtype anchors and cannot establish subtype for an unclassified source. A candidate needs Euclid VIS/NISP pixel/multi-visit validation and spectral/motion followup before discovery.
