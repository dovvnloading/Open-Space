# Archival investigation

WD J230752.92−000207.45 was identified in DESI as a candidate with Ca II triplet emission. An earlier BOSS observation contains emission at all three predicted wavelengths. This experiment tests for independent epochs of emission against blank-sky fibers, neighboring fibers, the wavelength mask and the sensitivity to continuum and velocity windows.
