# Source membership checkpoint

The primary STREAMFINDER DR3 member table (Ibata et al., 2024, stream 14) lists 70 NGC1261b candidate stars. The 12 stars with line-of-sight velocities match stars observed spectroscopically in Reticulum II by Simon et al. (2015). Removing this dwarf-galaxy sightline leaves 58 entries, none with a velocity in the stream table. The next checks are the individual membership flags, source identifiers, coordinate epochs, a control crossmatch, and what the discovery and Galactic-potential papers already discuss.
