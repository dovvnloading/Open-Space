# Independent-methods referee check — 2026-11-19 (adversarial self-review, **not** an external referee)

**Verdict: publish as an incomplete, calibrated *pilot*, not a discovery and not a comprehensive null.** All tests below were performed without GitHub Actions. The work cannot answer the full five-catalogue / published-UHECR-catalogue question and must not carry `[REMARKABLE]`.

## Independent checks against the committed inputs (completed)

1. A separate read of `inputs/auger_mirror_sd_ge8.csv` finds **3,111 rows and 3,111 unique surface-detector IDs**, with **493 / 155 / 58** events above 20 / 32 / 45 EeV. Catalogued positions: **90 LHAASO**, **30 historical magnetars**, **173 ATNF-derived young pulsars**, one Cen A direction and five hand-curated starbursts. The claim of 83 pulsars in the initial tool status was a temporary variable-accounting mistake; the committed CSV and independent inventory give 173.
2. Independently re-evaluating great-circle separations from Cen A (J2000 RA 201.365°, Dec −43.019°) yields **28 events** above 32 EeV within 27°, exactly the saved configuration's count; the 999 random-RA worlds average 16.5506.
3. The first-harmonic 8 EeV right-ascension amplitude **0.025422**, phase 137.676°, and scramble p **0.622** are consistent with an unweighted Rayleigh p **0.60493**. **This does not reproduce** the Auger collaboration's significant full-dataset dipole; no exposure-weighted 3D reconstruction has been done.
4. A **six-event** pair-seeded strip with `r=0.94746` is the maximum after allowing two deterministic leave-one-out trims. Its event energies are **99.034, 23.922, 23.456, 21.518, 20.726, 20.187 EeV**: one high-energy point and a tight low-energy group, **not** compelling independent evidence for a coherent deflection sequence. All six lie in the vertical SD1500 sample. Its fitted zero-deflection direction (RA 89.796°, Dec −49.789°) is a geometric extrapolation, **not an identification**. The entire combinatorial axis search and trim were refitted in each scramble; 999 sky controls give p=**0.233**, 999 *within-sample* energy shuffles p=**0.252**.
5. The **39** tested catalogue/threshold/radius configurations are adjusted jointly by exchangeable min-p rank among **999** random-RA skies: best raw p=**0.005** (Cen A), **global p=0.101**. In within-sample energy permutations the best family-wise p=**0.101**. A simple further Bonferroni across the source and multiplet search families makes the most favorable sky-family p at least **0.202**. No discovery-level result.
6. Five hand-chosen nearby starbursts show a broad raw directional count excess (172 within 25° above 20 EeV vs mean 145.78, local p=0.006), but this **does not reproduce the Auger flux-weighted full starburst analysis**; the global catalogue+control scan is not significant.

## Outstanding fatal limits for a full-paper result (pending, not hidden)

- **Authoritative-input check:** isolated local compute container failed DNS for Auger and Zenodo; remote sandbox/jobs returned 402. Source CSVs came via the GitHub connector from a third-party mirror (content-addressed Git SHA-1, derived SHA-256). The official Zenodo ZIP MD5 and byte-identical original-table Git object IDs have not been directly verified. `scripts/prepare_from_zenodo.py` will enforce those checks when downloaded in a network-enabled runtime.
- **Catalog coverage:** 4LAC-DR3 blazar list is published as FITS, but connected textual GitHub fetch cannot decode those binary tables. The 869-galaxy updated nearby-galaxy catalogue's row data could not be acquired (EDD API requires a key); neither was tested. Accordingly **global p-values cover only the 39 configurations actually evaluated**, not these pending source families or an expanded trials budget. Historical magnetar positions are incomplete and have no ephemeris update.
- **Completeness:** the separately released 2023 published UHECR catalog (included with the 2024 release) was not ingested; the present event ledger represents only the reconstructed roughly 10% release-3 SD1500+inclined samples, with 750 m events excluded. The 10% denominator applies to quality-selected legacy data periods through August 2018, **not all Auger data observed through 2026**.
- **Exposure/physics:** uniform RA scrambles conditioned on declination approximate, but do not reproduce sidereal uptime or array efficiency; no full directional exposure, aperture correction, survey masks, source luminosity weights, energy-scale error, magnetic-field model, charge-dependent backtracking, or physically adequate composition treatment. The seed-pair, two-trim, tangent-plane method is **inspired by** but not a faithful reproduction of every detail of the published Auger multiplet pipeline.
- **Controls:** the E>8 first-harmonic null is not the published 3D dipole; the 5-source starburst pilot is not the published complete candidate set; no published full-data significance should be copied to this 10%-scale subrelease.
- **Resolution and verification:** with B=999, p steps are 0.001; a claimed discovery would demand vastly more Monte Carlo trials, alternative control algorithms, independent fitting code, source-selection preregistration and primary-data hashing. The remote connector executed JS scans, but no shell-level end-to-end Node replay against checked-out files was feasible here.

**Recommendation:** merge a labeled *inconclusive pilot feasibility record* with explicit omissions; do not advertise a new source, do not classify as a fully calibrated global null, and do not edit `docs/REMARKABLE_FINDINGS.md`. A future full investigation must first acquire/verify the published event catalog and both missing source catalogues, then redo the full look-elsewhere calibration.
