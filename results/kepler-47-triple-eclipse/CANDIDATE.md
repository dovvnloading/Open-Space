# Candidate at early checkpoint

A nominal forward integration of the Orosz et al. five-body solution predicts Kepler-47 d overlapping the foreground M dwarf during primary eclipse around BJD 2462322.22 (2029 July 4). This is a candidate shadow overlap. Test the stellar eclipse clock with independent TESS data, test the planet ephemeris against published times, and vary its timing and projected position. Later calculations in the study report supersede these provisional numbers. A new middle-planet transit time or a joint posterior propagated to 2029 is needed to quantify event probability.
