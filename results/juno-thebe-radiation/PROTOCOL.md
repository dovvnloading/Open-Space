# Juno release 35 radiation around the Thebe encounter

Frozen on 18 November 2026 from main 3fed9e09ebf9235cd3aff9d1b1aa8c7122d5137e before reading radiation values. Timestamp-only and reconstructed-orbit preflight established the source intervals and dates. On 1 May, Juno came closest to Thebe at 13:20:05.282 UTC, near one independently commanded SRU image. The question is whether that encounter is accompanied by a short, coincident deficit in an independent detector sensitive to penetrating particles. The measurement unit is a JEDI spin-length time bin or one SRU image. The alternate explanations include a radial or magnetic-latitude gradient, spacecraft viewing geometry and a detector mode change. Direct interception of Thebe and magnetic connection require separate geometry. The latter is outside this experiment. ## Fixed inputs and geometry

- Use only the public 29 March and 1 May 2026 JEDI-A180 HIERSISP CDR version 04 record ranges, their labels and the version-02 column format. Preserve exact byte extents, UTC brackets and range receipt digests. Analyze plus or minus 45 minutes around the March Jupiter and May Thebe closest approaches. Use JEDI ETLookDirection, with exposure duration and instrument quality from the same record. March is a near-Jupiter comparison centered on perijove with ASC coverage. It is not an independently matched Thebe encounter.
- Use NASA NAIF reconstructed Juno trajectory and the Jupiter satellite ephemeris, with geometric position in J2000 relative to Jupiter or Thebe. Minimize center distance using a coarse one-minute grid and a bounded scalar fit around the best point. Compute sampling offsets separately from midpoint times in the PUB SRU count-rate tables for PJ81 through PJ83. Compare declared row counts with decoded SCET rows. Record the closest SRU time and adjacent temporal gaps without inferring uninterrupted coverage.
- Use ASC CHUC release-35 radiation tables on PJ81 and PJ82. No equivalent ASC table covers the May encounter in this release. At each observed SRU midpoint on PJ81/PJ82, compare the median shutter-compensated radiation count from ASC in a symmetric 120-second window, using BBO_FLAG = 0 and positive finite counts, requiring at least 10 contributing ASC rows. Do not extrapolate outside the native timestamp extent. Report SRU-derived rates and rank agreement per pass only if there are at least five matched images. Show all excluded SRU images. These rates have different responses and units.

## Thebe falsification test

- Require JEDI LargePixel=False and Quality=0, positive finite duration and finite, nonnegative counters in energy channels 02–23. Calculate the counts divided by accumulated duration for each pixel separately. T1 and T3 are the shielded witness pixels. T2, T4 and T5 are bare pixel comparisons. These are instrument response proxies. The witness can admit some penetrating foreground and scattering. Group by fixed 30-second bins centered on the encounter, using the same good rows for every pixel. Require at least four distinct sectors and at least 5 seconds of accumulated duration in each qualifying bin. Report support for all bins and sensitivity with a cut at SectorDeadTime/Duration ≤ 0.1 where the recorded dead time is nonnegative.
- Primary window is plus or minus 60 seconds around closest Thebe approach. Compare medians of qualifying spin bins with a local baseline. The baseline at the event is the geometric mean of the separate median rates from 8–20 minutes before and 8–20 minutes after. Require at least three qualifying central bins and at least eight bins on each baseline side. Zero or unavailable baselines are inconclusive. The gate is a relative deficit of at least 30% in each of T1 and T3 on both quality selections. Apply the same calculation to time offsets of −20, −15, −10, +10, +15 and +20 minutes. Require both observed deficits to exceed the deficits for every covered control in the respective channel and at least four covered controls. Report the largest control deficits and results for the bare channels. Repeat the same calculation at March perijove as a geometry comparison. This local screen has no calibrated false-alarm probability.
- On May 1, compare the nearest SRU exposure with the immediately preceding and following acquisitions. Use the derived rate only when the three rows have finite positive derived rates and the same SRU identifier and valid low-flux-cluster versus high-flux-noise method indicators. A consistent SRU deficit is at least 30% below both adjacent images. Sparse SRU measurements cannot establish onset or duration. A JEDI deficit without valid SRU corroboration remains a single-instrument candidate. A passed gate would establish a coincident local dip in the instrument proxies within these exact intervals; physical association with the moon still requires magnetic mapping and another geometry.
