# Early candidate checkpoint

The reconnaissance screen of nearby ultracool dwarfs identifies two Gaia DR3 sources along the path of WISE J200050.19+362950.1. Their source IDs are 2060116591502525952 and 2060116595791115776. Preliminary encounter years are 2034 and 2039. Source astrometry, position conventions, uncertainties, alternate lens measurements and prior work require checks. This checkpoint records the leads before those checks. Cushing et al. (2014) already suggested this brown dwarf as a possible lens.
