## Proposed fixed selection and estimand

Use DU1 and DU3 as the primary and DU2 separately as a diagnostic. All detectors use the same sky basis and response-matched energy and region selection. Adopt ICRS source center RA 253.46756951679163 degrees, Dec +39.76016913205556 degrees, source circle radius 60 arcsec; local background annulus 120–270 arcsec centered at the same position, with cataloged unrelated sources removed according to a fixed pre-acquisition catalog/mask. This geometry is provisional: verify target coordinates from authoritative metadata and that the annulus is on every usable detector, has acceptable exposure and no strong contaminants using independent catalog/metadata; if not, STOP and amend/review before reading the holdout. Do not optimize regions using holdout results. Check exposure maps and PSF leakage, vignetting and background Stokes; simple geometric area subtraction is insufficient for unequal exposure. Record background contamination/systematic limitations.

Primary proposed estimator is **unweighted** (weights unity) and acceptance-corrected source-minus-background Stokes I,Q,U, with per-event energy-dependent effective area A(E) and modulation factor mu(E); I=sum(1/A), Q=sum(Q_event/(A mu)), U=sum(U_event/(A mu)); subtract exposure-scaled background components and only then calculate Q/I and U/I. Use calibrated event Q/U, not rederived angles. These are broadband photon-spectrum-weighted estimates evaluated at measured energy; finite energy dispersion and differing spectra can bias comparisons. Validate against a forward-folded I/Q/U spectral model if feasible, and explicitly limit interpretation if not. Q/U covariance including I correlations, off-region noise and DU exposure must be propagated; reject configurations whose covariance or response/exposure calculation cannot be justified. Weighted alpha075/W_MOM analysis is a secondary sensitivity check and requires matching weighted responses; it must not silently replace the primary. Programmatically predeclare exact PI inclusion and FITS time conventions after checking official headers, before holdout access.

## Conditional repeatability packet — pending root review

Operating scope is the conditional repeatability of the standard L2 and packaged-IRF measured-energy, photon-weighted normalized Stokes estimators between the selected baseline and a single later visit. It does not establish a physical jet rotation, an unbiased true-energy 2–8 keV polarization, or a population discovery. The deterministic XY-only recentered extraction is a mandatory sensitivity, not an independent observation. The continuous-nuisance `prospective.py` is optional and cannot establish a physical claim until all bounds are validated; it does not gate this narrower conditional screen.

Primary predeclared configuration: fixed SIMBAD ICRS center, 60-arcsec source circle, 120–270-arcsec background annulus, DU1+3, 2≤channel-center energy<8 keV, particle STATUS array index 7 clear, and unweighted responses selected by UTC validity. The second configuration applies the five-iteration XY-only centroid rule. Both use separate source/background event sums and the full statistical I/Q/U ratio covariance. The main statistic is delta=(q_hold−q_base,u_hold−u_base); chi2=deltaᵀ(C_base+C_hold)^−1 delta; p=exp(−chi2/2), contingent on an approximate 2D Gaussian and fixed calibration/background model. Predeclared p<5.7e−7 is a **screening lead only**: shared calibration, background systematics, adaptive-centroid selection and independent confirmation are unresolved. Report both alternatives regardless of outcome. The forward-folded shared-vector fit is a diagnostic with a different estimand.
