# Spectroscopic observations

The primary input is the CDS correction of 20 April 2026 to Table 4 of Perdelwitz et al. (2024). The catalogue describes 252,615 exposures of 5,239 targets observed before January 2022. Those are publication totals. The search coverage is the set of targets and epochs recorded by the run. The correction date refers to publication of the table, not the date of the last observation. [CDS catalogue and byte definitions](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/J/A+A/683/A125) · [Perdelwitz et al.](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202348263) · [Trifonov et al.](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201936686)

The reader tries the CDS compressed table, uncompressed table and VizieR TAP representation in that order. It requires the published row count before accepting a transfer. The input manifest records the URL, SHA-256, byte count and retrieval time for each download. Checksums of TAP CSV exports apply to the serialized response and may change with row ordering on a future server release.

The primary velocities are differential SERVAL RVs corrected for barycentric motion, secular acceleration, instrumental drift and nightly zero points. The reader uses DRVmlcnzp and e_DRVmlcnzp, both in m/s, and requires a finite BJD, finite RV, positive finite error and a zero SERVAL quality flag. It retains one measurement per target and BJD, choosing the smallest reported error if a pair repeats. The fibre split uses BJD 2457169, in the intervention gap identified by Trifonov et al. Separate zero points are essential because the upstream templates and corrections were derived separately. FWHM and BIS have units of km/s, CRX has units of m/s, and dLW is in (m/s)(km/s). H-alpha, S and R′HK are dimensionless. The time-resolved R′HK column and S-index column remain separate.

The S-index supplement uses Harada et al. (2025), SPORES-HWO II, Table 5. The reader retains positive S-indices on HARPS, HARPS-pre and HARPS-post exposures with matching HD identifiers and BJDs within two seconds. It rejects ambiguous matches. Component suffixes are retained, so aliases and alternate component designations may have no match. The run reports the number of epochs with S-indices. [SPORES-HWO II tables](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/J/AJ/170/343)

If all primary retrieval routes fail, the survey uses the ESO Phase 3 HARPS RV catalogue DR1. This independent DRS reduction contains 289,843 archive records through May 2023. The reader identifies the documented table by its columns and row count. It uses the SIMBAD designation, drift-corrected CCF RV in km/s converted to m/s, and drs_dvrms in m/s. It selects high-accuracy mode and each star's most frequent CCF mask among valid exposures. It retains BIS and FWHM from the DRS. This release lacks the SERVAL activity products and the RVBank nightly zero-point correction. These differences limit activity and trend interpretation for a fallback run. Based on data obtained from the ESO Science Archive Facility with DOI [10.18727/archive/33](https://doi.org/10.18727/archive/33). [ESO column definitions](https://www.eso.org/qi/catalog/show/416)
