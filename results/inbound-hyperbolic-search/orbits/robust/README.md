# Force-law and arc checks for seventeen formal gravity flags

None of the seventeen dense follow-ups establishes an unrecognized interstellar object. The several-sigma full-arc negative energies that survived radial-plus-transverse water-ice recoil depend on the selected arc, comet activity or additional recoil components. A separate eighteenth flag was the post-break fragment of Evans–Drinkwater. The original-labelled pre-split positions put the parent within 0.49σ of parabolic. [Parent refit](../fragment/README.md)

## Completed

The two disjoint ground censuses covered 2,193 catalogue IDs. This follow-up includes all actual 250 au crossings at least three formal errors below zero, plus any crossing with nominal excess speed ≥1 km/s at least 1.5 errors below zero. The independently tested Evans–Drinkwater parent was handled separately. Each selected object received a new fit using all supported ground positions up to 1,200, with deterministic per-station/night thinning for denser arcs. The table gives signed barycentric 1/a ± formal 1σ at the actual incoming 250 au crossing, in units of 10⁻⁶ au⁻¹.

The code fits six unrestricted initial-state coordinates. Its additional models allow signed radial inverse-square recoil and radial-plus-transverse symmetric water-ice sublimation, with a nonnegative radial coefficient in the latter. Gravity fits have first-night, last-night and leading-observatory holdouts. A separate preperihelion fit required at least 12 such observations over 30 days and three postperihelion positions to leave out. Historical data also received a 10-arcsec gravity fit, doubling the default five-arcsec error floor. The [63 model rows](evidence/summary.csv) include each force-law outcome, boundary energy, retained sample size and RMS. A [separate 18-ID table](evidence/dispositions.csv) gives the physical or statistical reason each branch failed the discovery bar.

- **C/2004 H6 (SWAN).** All 307 selected ground positions are postperihelion. The two-component model places the energy 3.61σ on the unbound side. Adding free normal recoil yields −4,356.8 ± 1,618.4 × 10⁻⁶ au⁻¹ (2.69σ) and shifts the inferred speed from 0.54 to 1.97 km/s. The near-parabolic three-component solution reached −0.67 × 10⁻⁶ au⁻¹ at 50 au for a sky-only Δχ² of 7.24. The fitted transverse and normal accelerations are large relative to the radial term. Marsden already reported an original 1/a of −124 ± 48 × 10⁻⁶ au⁻¹ on a shorter arc in 2004. [MPEC 2004-N33](https://www.minorplanetcenter.net/mpec/K04/K04N33.html)
- **C/2002 O4 (Hönig).** The full-arc two-component model remains negative at 3.35σ. Mass loss during the observing arc was independently reported. Published fits to the shortened arc give a closed original orbit, whereas the complete arc can give a negative original 1/a. [CODE solution history](https://apollo.astro.amu.edu.pl/CODE/orbit.php?int=2002o4n1&orb=original)
- **C/1886 J1 (Brooks).** Its 23/25-position gravity fit gives −0.00796 ± 0.00216 au⁻¹. Doubling the historical error floor gives −0.00571 ± 0.00421 au⁻¹. The signed radial model reaches only 175 au in the integration period. An earlier barycentric integration reported a hyperbolic original orbit for this same comet, designated 1886 III. [Brady 1965, Table II](https://adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1965AJ.....70..279B)
- **C/1917 H1 (Schaumasse).** The signed radial model reverses the apparent gravity-only excess. The 10-arcsec gravity result is 2.85σ. A published, more extensive astrometric re-reduction found a positive original 1/a of 1,629 ± 1,203 × 10⁻⁶ au⁻¹. [Published comparison, Table 1](https://academic.oup.com/view-large/130412893)
- **C/2021 O3, C/1991 Y1 and C/2023 A3.** Their preperihelion-only gravity solutions are all nominally bound. For C/2023 A3 this contrasts with a full-arc gravity result 89 formal errors below zero, illustrating the sensitivity of a small energy to activity and the chosen observing interval.

## Limits and replay

Each force law clips residuals independently. Different retained samples can therefore contribute to differences between models. The model-specific formal uncertainties omit unknown astrometric systematics and activity outside the observed arc. The optional 50 au near-parabolic profile is a soft constraint with a scale of 2 × 10⁻⁵ au⁻¹. Its achieved residual is recorded with its Δχ² and is essential when the fitted energy itself is that small. No candidate probability is inferred from a Gaussian tail.

The six compressed [evidence shards](evidence/) contain original sky positions, sample hashes, fitted states, covariances, holdouts and crossing diagnostics. To regenerate the ground and model tables, run `python results/inbound-hyperbolic-search/orbits/summarize_census.py`. The [fit script](replay.py) reads the frozen target manifests. The two workflow runs used separate manifests; the second also tests normal acceleration for SWAN.
