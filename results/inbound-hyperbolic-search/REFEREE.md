# Independent referee review, 17 November 2026

**Decision under review:** this is a bounded, survey-conditional null, not a discovery. No [REMARKABLE] label is warranted. Exact source snapshots and unchanged pending checks matter more than an apparent high-speed osculating eccentricity.

## Numerical replay from the submitted inputs

- [Independent full replay](https://github.com/dovvnloading/Open-Space/actions/runs/44041019344): the eight jobs succeeded. We checked SHA-256 for the three committed ITF subsets and nine compressed MPC observation arcs, 67 priority-object inputs, four additional sungrazers, three known-ISO alias controls and five rover/site-vector pairs. We reran the catalogue aggregator, the deterministic F51 linker, 20,000 geometry-supported injections, four parabolic SOHO fits and four separate barycentric orbit refits from the compressed MPC positions and frozen initial states. The new JSON and trials compared equal to the committed files.
- The F51 29-day sample retains 7,170 MPC observation rows and 2,131 submitted station/name/date groups. After motion and linearity cuts, 2,130 groups on 17 nights remain. Zero real high-speed three-night links survived. Forced injected astrometry recovered 99/100, with exact 95% lower binomial bound 0.9455406146; 51/52 incoming were recovered. Five independently rotated-night controls accepted zero.
- The independently seeded geometry trial found 37 of 20,000 paths within the 1.5° three-night sky/time proxy, recovered **35/37** (95% exact 81.805–99.339%) and 14/15 incoming. The 0.5° proxy supported 1 path and the 0.1° proxy none. None of the accepted associations mixed real and synthetic identities. These are conditional tests, not detector-footprint or flux-completeness measurements.
- Applying a Bonferroni allocation of 0.025 to the zero-count Poisson tail and 0.025 to the measured recovery error gives **−ln(0.025) / 0.9455406146 = 3.9013443** expected already-three-tracklet objects, rounded to **3.90**, as a conservative joint 95% upper bound. This is **not** an interstellar-object encounter rate or density bound.
- A fresh independent join reproduces all **2,290** distinct JPL IDs with e ≥ 1; **603** have a numerical far-inbound independent fit and **416** have an actual inbound 250 au crossing with a formal energy uncertainty. **1,687 lack a numerical far-inbound solution and remain pending**, not ruled out. The source JPL query JSON digest matched its archived receipt.
- Rerunning the 1I, 2I and 3I full optical-astrometry refits reproduces **26.3383906356**, **32.2839194503** and **57.9684617570 km/s** at 250 au inbound. C/2025 V1 refit to 117 positions gave gravity z = **5.0515162**, radial-plus-transverse Marsden z = **1.0243737** in original reciprocal semimajor axis. The latter has no validated interstellar claim, and omitting ZTF observations or forcing near-parabolic energy is documented in the original result. The 119,799,808-byte DE440 kernel and observatory-code table were re-fetched, and both exactly matched their acquisition SHA-256.
- Four SOHO Kreutz objects were refitted at fixed e=1 from their complete 49, 21, 34 and 19 spacecraft-tied positions: per-coordinate residuals **22.47291″, 28.17196″, 35.48582″ and 37.60544″**, respectively. Their apparently extreme three-second automatic hyperbolas are not credible incoming-energy determinations.

## Housekeeping and explicit limits

The initial research subtree comprised 286 files and 5.66 MB of Git blobs. The largest was the intentional 580,770-byte F51 MPC frozen slice; nothing resembled a temporary log, object file, cached ephemeris, image cube, or scratch notebook. Scripts and checksummed compressed excerpts remain under `results/inbound-hyperbolic-search`. Reusable Actions recipes are archived under `reproduction/workflows` rather than leaving research-only batch jobs active on every push. The root catalog incorporates this result as one *Null result* without losing unrelated additions on `main`.

**Pending or uncalibrated:** the 1,687 catalogue objects without numerical far-inbound estimates; alternate nongravitational and astrometric-weight models outside the selected follow-ups; photometric, chip, trailing, and ITF ingestion completeness; historical NEOCP outside the sampled departures; and the conversion from conditional three-tracklet efficiency to a sky-density or encounter-rate limit. Earlier ZTF images were analysed by the research scripts, but the original pixel frames are not committed and were not independently replayed by the referee. The paper-level validation of C/2004 H6 remains limited by all-ground postperihelion observations and uncertain cometary recoil.

**Still in progress in this checkpoint:** targeted independent re-fit of C/2004 H6 recoil models from its deposited 307-position extract. The archived model outcomes and formal significances are checked from `orbits/robust/evidence/summary.csv`; that alone is not a new physical validation.
