# Subsequent 2026 fixed-track visit check

**A subsequent fixed-track check found two 20-pixel-long trails in visit 005 at about −19.6° relative to nominal Perdita, consistent with the source in visits 006 and 008.** All visits are from JWST/NIRCam on the same day. The crossing list and check outputs were committed together; prospective freeze chronology cannot be independently established. The two full native-trail fits at the −19.8° visit-006/008 track prediction give longitude residuals of −19.74° and −19.42° relative to nominal Perdita, fitted PSF sigmas of 0.74 and 0.84 native pixel, and formal amplitude signal-to-error ratios of 14.82 and 15.25. The visit 007 offset stack scores −5.14, and the two native fits with detector coverage give formal ratios of 0.96 and 0.02. The noisier visit 007 supplies no positive detection. Four other exposures across these two visits placed the predicted position off chip.
