# Complementary NIRCam channels and the 2026 Perdita detection

**The supplemental screen found one source in three independent visits. Its motion, orbital radius, and longitude relative to predicted Perdita agree with the continuing drift of Perdita.** Six neighboring templates in the 2026 October 8 F140M short visits at first cleared the nominal known-moon masks. The source lies 60.7 and 61.9 native pixels from the URA184 position of Perdita. At the Uranian equator its two independently measured positions have inferred radii of 76,365 and 76,340 km and longitude residuals of −19.67° and −19.94°. These are deprojections of instantaneous sky positions. An independent long visit on the same day confirms the frozen position. Two native long-exposure trail fits gave −19.74° and −19.42° at amplitude-to-formal-error ratios of 14.8 and 15.3. The source is undetected in the other long visit. [Frozen long-visit test](../jwst8975/long_holdout/README.md) [Source centroids](f140m_sources.csv) · [Comparison with every cataloged moon](f140m_catalog.csv) · [All first-stage crossings](crossings.csv)

Two different native detectors, NRCA3 and NRCB1, recorded the short-visit images. A trailed Gaussian fit with a planar background yields widths of 0.85 and 0.91 native pixel and peak amplitudes of 21 and 28 times the scaled local pixel MAD. The formal within-fit x/y uncertainties are (0.26, 0.23) and (0.24, 0.12) pixel. A sky position fixed in the opposite visit misses by 295 and 308 native pixels. A location fixed relative to the planet misses by 176 and 182 pixels. The underlying ephemeris is JPL URA184. The source that motivated the 2025 cross-visit investigation was independently identified in the observing team's labeled image. [Native fits and alternate motion hypotheses](f140m_followup.py) · [2025 identification](../jwst6379_9482/identity/IDENTIFICATION.md)

The supplemental search used 122 calibrated detector tiles containing 29 visit/filter combinations, grouped into 17 planet/filter observing epochs. It processed NIRCam long-wave channels, the 6379 F070W/F115W pairs, the short complementary 8975 visits, and Uranus 2739 F210M. The separate program 2739 reduction analyzes the Uranus F300M frames. Masking removes all known moon trails, the projected ring envelopes, disk and immediate glare, bad pixels and detector edges. The search evaluates a 1,000-km radial grid and 1° phase grid with nine light-time-corrected samples per exposure. Uranus radii run from 52,000–160,000 km and Neptune from 35,000–160,000 km. Across these epochs the screen evaluated 679,320 correlated template/epoch cells, of which 49,789 had sufficient support in two visits. The six F140M cells were the only positive two-visit crossings. There were no two-visit sign-inverted crossings. [Epoch totals](epochs.csv) · [All single-visit positive and inverted counts](visits.csv) · [Registration and ephemeris selection](registrations.csv)

Time-coincident detector tiles contribute one sample. Each visit needs a normalized sum of 6 and two samples of at least 2.5 if two exposures are available. Short visits sometimes consist of a single exposure so a compact source, a stable sky position veto and confirmation in other visits are essential. Single-visit crossings are reported for reuse and provide no independent discovery evidence. This supplemental grid has no measured completeness limit. Deeper, independently calibrated population limits are in the [main report](../README.md).

## Reproduction

Read the MAST exposure inventory in the parent directory. Restore the input ephemerides from the published observables using `restore_ephemeris.py`. Include the Neptune 2739 and Uranus 2739 grids, Uranus 2768, both 8975 epochs, 6379, 9482, the separate October 2025 short-visit grid, and the [extra 6379](extra6379_observables.csv) and [extra 8975](extra8975_observables.csv) grids. The native cutouts preserve SCI, ERR and DQ plus their WCS offsets. For partial FITS downloads the SHA-256 applies to the recorded byte ranges and the resulting cutout separately. [Main cutouts](cutouts.csv) · [Main range hashes](ranges.csv) · [Uranus 2739 F210M cutouts](u2739_cutouts.csv) · [Uranus 2739 F210M range hashes](u2739_ranges.csv)

```sh
python nircam_supplement/fetch.py /data/nircam-channels
python nircam_supplement/fetch.py /data/nircam-channels --uranus-f210m
python nircam_supplement/screen.py /data/nircam-channels /data/observables/*.npz
python nircam_supplement/f140m_followup.py /data/nircam-channels /data/observables/f140m_8975.npz /data/observables/extra8975.npz
```
