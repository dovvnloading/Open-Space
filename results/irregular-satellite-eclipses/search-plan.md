# Planetary eclipses of distant irregular moons

Search question: when do the bright irregular satellites of Jupiter and Saturn cross their planet's shadow, how deeply, and can the event be observed from Earth? A deep eclipse of a distant moon can occur well away from the planet on the sky.

Freeze the primary targets before ranking: Himalia, Elara, Pasiphae, Sinope, Lysithea, Carme, Ananke, Leda and Phoebe. Search calendar years 2020–2040 inclusive using JPL trajectories. Require an actual crossing of the finite solar disc as seen from the moon. Check oblate planet geometry, light propagation and sky visibility before proposing an observing window. Look for independent ephemeris or archive checks. A forecast alone is not an observed eclipse.

This is the initial search specification. The measured results and reproducible analysis will follow on this branch.
