# Archival investigation

CHIME detected FRB 20221129A and FRB 20221129B from the same repeater approximately 216 seconds apart. The middle of three fitted components peaks at the highest frequency in both. This experiment tests the recurrence against the measured frequency-time intensities, instrumental effects and the population eligible for selection.
