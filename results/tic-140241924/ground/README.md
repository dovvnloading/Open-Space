# Independent ground photometry of TIC 140241924

ASAS-SN Sky Patrol object 429497355165 has 1,415 measurements between 2016 and 2025. We compared 938 clean epochs in the TESS time span against the eclipse widths, periods and approximate timings derived from TESS. Sky Patrol blends the cluster field. The combined light nevertheless shows both known eclipse periods. The fitted A primary, A secondary and B primary dips have amplitudes of 0.20, 0.08 and 0.11 mag under separate linear ephemerides.

The ground data alone give little leverage on **curvature** once each binary can independently adjust its epoch and period. The robust loss changes from 1157.33 (two linear clocks) to 1155.78 (both TESS timing curves, with linear adjustments still free). Omitting a camera or a 365-day block can reverse the sign of the small difference. The study of opposing timing variations rests on the TESS visits. [All camera and calendar omissions](fits.csv) · [Original ASAS-SN client](https://github.com/asas-sn/skypatrol)

Run `python check.py` from this directory with NumPy, pandas, SciPy and Astropy installed. The replay converts the archived JD(UTC) to BJD(TDB) using Earth’s barycentric position; the neglected observatory displacement contributes at most 0.022 seconds. It keeps quality G with magnitude errors from 0.001 to 0.2, applies a 0.02-mag error floor in quadrature, allows camera-by-365-day zero points, and fits the three eclipse depths. A common phase offset and drift for A’s two minima and separate offset and drift for B are free in every fit. Shape and depth can vary between instruments; here the Gaussian widths are fixed to TESS. The TESS shifts between measured seasons are interpolated linearly. One free scaling per binary is not included in the primary comparison. The loss measures weighted Huber fit quality and carries no calibrated significance.

The archive input came from the Sky Patrol v2 `master_list` cone at 126.771492°, −50.765804° with radius 0.0015°. The original returned index and photometry parquet SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the manifest. The compressed CSV retains all original rows and fields.
