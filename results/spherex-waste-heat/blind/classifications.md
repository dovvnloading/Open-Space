# Published classifications for the frozen blind cohort

This supplementary screen applies to every original WISE-tested star and identifies the overlapping subset that passed DR1 spectral coverage and control requirements. It preserves the measured 404/55 samples and their flux-injection grids.

Secure catalogue vetoes require an exact Gaia identifier for an optical young star in the Konkoly compilation (KYSO), a non-single-star Gaia DR3 solution, or an exact Gaia DR3/EDR3 identifier linking a SIMBAD object with an established young-star, external-galaxy/AGN or multiple-star type. A recorded multiple does not establish the origin of infrared light. Sources selected by the Gaia variability classifier (including YSO/AGN), positional IR-excess-list counterparts and possible line-of-sight blends are placed in a separate review set. A SIMBAD object within 12″ of the Gaia position propagated to J2000 can affect a W4 beam. An unidentified close positional match alone cannot establish a physical companion or a galaxy superposition. An archived IR-excess report is insufficient to prove a debris disk.

`classifications/stars.csv` has one row per original WISE-tested source. `in_strict_dr1` identifies the original 55-star spectroscopic subset. Secure reasons take precedence in the unique counts, and overlaps are reported explicitly in `counts.json`. A star with no database match is unclassified. Targets with no listed exclusion remain in the denominator, subject to catalog selection effects and to unresolved objects too faint to be catalogued. The survey does not supply a census of companions or circumstellar matter.

`classify.py` reads the fixed measured products (`stars.csv`, `screen.csv`, `dr1_screen.csv`). With NumPy, Astropy and Requests installed, run `python classify.py .` from the blind directory. The script verifies cached archive responses by SHA-256 on replay; delete only the classifications output directory to reacquire. `classifications/receipts.json` records full archive requests, input digests, response digests and timestamps. `classifications/raw/` retains the responses. SIMBAD tables use the primary object type and the full published type list. The published-source cross-checks use the complete KYSO table and the Cotten–Song prime and reserved stellar tables; independent catalogs have incomplete coverage and uncertain entries.
