# NEOWISE scatter within a survey visit

Candidate C varied more from exposure to exposure in W1 than predicted by the quoted errors and an added 0.02 mag single-exposure noise floor. A constant-magnitude fit gives χ² = 77.25 for 37 degrees of freedom (38 accepted detections spanning 4.50 days). Its formal p-value is 0.000117 and 0.00257 after a Bonferroni correction over the 22 qualifying target-band visits. The corresponding W2 fit gives χ² = 38.96 for 37 degrees of freedom (p = 0.382). C is the only corrected p-value below 0.05.

Four of C's eight same-scan W1 exposure pairs separated by approximately 11 s differ by 0.30–0.31 mag. In the W1 constant fit, raising the illustrative single-exposure floor from 0.02 to 0.06 mag reduces χ² to 53.04. The tests use pipeline errors and assume independent exposures. Blending, centroid shifts, short-exposure calibration and underestimated measurement errors remain plausible. Ren et al. identified a near-infrared neighbor 3.75 arcsec away. This is excess **photometric scatter** in an unresolved field. Independent epochs and frame-level differential photometry would be needed to test intrinsic variability. [Archival imaging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03619) · [NEOWISE single-exposure caveats](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/WISE/docs/release/NEOWISE/expsup/sec2_2.html)

These calculations use the accepted exposures from `measure.py`, group visits at gaps longer than 20 days and require at least three points. From this directory, extract `archive_inputs.zip` to an empty folder, then run `python within_visit.py /path/to/extracted /path/to/within_visit.csv` (SciPy required).
