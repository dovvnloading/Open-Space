# Explicit follow-up dispositions for the 53 Contardo–Hogg targets

The [source-by-source ledger](fgk_dispositions.csv) lists **every one of the 53 published FGK source IDs**, the published 100–600 K or other temperature, its DR1 spectral classification, the applicable prior references and the uncompleted physical-exclusion tests. It joins the frozen [Contardo & Hogg (2024) catalog](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/ad6b90) to the [two-mask DR1 table](../fgk_spectra/all_53.csv). An unresolved row is not a claim that no previous paper exists, nor that an artificial emitter is needed. Red continua cannot by themselves exclude natural dust, unresolved companions or galaxies.

**Two known natural counterparts inside 300 pc.** FGK4 / TIC 43488669 has published optical dips and debris-disk interpretations ([Tajiri et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4365/abbc17); [Greenberg 2026](https://doi.org/10.3847/2515-5172/ae7322)). FGK5 / TYC 5940-1510-1 is an extreme debris disk with reported 10 µm silicate-like emission ([Moór et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/abdc26); [Moór et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stae155)). Their published 815 K and 835 K fits are outside the stipulated 100–600 K search interval.

**Two unresolved warm fields outside 300 pc.** FGK18, Gaia DR3 3017430205815280384 (708 pc; 550 K published fit), has W3 light 5.07 times its W1/W2 single-blackbody extrapolation. FGK31, Gaia DR3 6359523376149191936 (529 pc; 530 K published fit), has a photosphere-dependent factor of 1.88, a **0.665 arcsec 12 µm centroid offset** and two nearby Gaia sources in a 19″ SPHEREx aperture. These residuals are not identified as technology: multiple dust temperatures and unresolved blends remain. Each has **only one acceptable final-year NEOWISE visit per band**, and SPHEREx DR1 uses scan-coadded cubes, so this work cannot demonstrate mid-infrared stability between visits. SkyMapper sampled eight and twelve optical nights without a 0.2-mag dip but with long gaps. **All 53 measured classifications:** 5 red in both masks, 13 below threshold and 35 indeterminate. The additional 49 rows are assigned their own spectral dispositions, with the primary W1/W2 selection cited; dedicated source-resolved physical identification is still pending. Only a finite set of prior identifications was reviewed. Neither the selected FGK sample nor the 300 pc field supports a population technosignature rate.
