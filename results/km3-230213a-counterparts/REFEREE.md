## Major scientific qualifications
- The post-trial 0.26% probability that would support association of PMN J0606−0724 **does not exist in the literature**: 0.26% is pre-trial for radio flare timing. It is not a corrected 8-family counterpart p-value. No new source-specific p-value can be inferred from zero matches.
- Flux/energy production for 220 PeV neutrinos is physically nontrivial. No survivor has a neutrino emission spectrum and time-resolved multimessenger exposure validation; IceCube reported no excess in 15 years of steady/flaring/time-window data.
- The most important unperformed tests are explicitly listed in [COVERAGE_AND_LIMITS.md](COVERAGE_AND_LIMITS.md): revised GCN/GBM localization maps, Swift/MAXI X-rays, forced ZTF/ATLAS, complete Fermi/OVRO parent monitoring, VLASS/RACS/NEOWISE epoch coverage, clusters and nearby galaxies, exposure-preserving matched Galactic controls. Those have **not** been ruled out.
- TNS discovery date is not explosion/onset/peak. RA-shifted random skies do not have identical survey exposure, Galactic dust or detector pointing; a count of 0 among 2,938 catalogued transients cannot constrain an unobserved source.
- The circular KM3NeT containment radii are published summary contours, not a pixel-level likelihood map; CHIME centroid locations and their potentially wide error regions cannot be reliably combined by a 3° distance cut alone.
