## Specific code and provenance review findings

1. **Do not equate range hashes with product-level SHA-256.** `science-pilot-receipt.json` identifies ordered fetched row ranges and FITS headers for real inputs; full original FITS bytes were not hashed. The cached 188 out-of-footprint exclusions are header-WCS claims from the prior run, not contemporaneous re-reads. Provenance has a nonzero remote mutation risk.
2. **Diagonal ivar propagation is improved, not complete.** The new bilinear `1/sum(w_i^2/ivar_i)` for independent neighbor variance is more defensible than interpolating ivars directly, but real map interpolation introduces neighboring-pixel covariance; it excludes sparse zero-ivar patches and does not address original ACT scan covariance.
3. **Reference disjointness is provenance-based.** Six other nights and exclusion of the full selected test date remove the obvious leakage. Independent-night noise is *not* assured; a shared coadd also causes different test observations to correlate. Do not count repeated positions as independent corroborations.
4. **The beam fallback is not a measured beam.** Published coadd harmonic kernels, nominal instrument angular resolution and a depth-1 effective scan PSF are different. The assumed FWHM width bracket should not be advertised as instrument uncertainty measurement or survey calibration.
5. **True timestamp recovery is now checked but failed.** Parsing MapCat `ctime` as ISO (instead of float) plus reading a time-map FITS pixel gives timestamped screening on the two usable nights. It cannot establish the source peak time or validate unobserved intervals. Positive-control recovery remains open.
6. **Counterpart attempts are not astrophysical vetos.** Missing/failed JPL small-body, radio, GCN and TNS responses and uninterpreted Gaia results leave *all* peaks unresolved, regardless of the strong empirical noise veto. No ad-hoc source labels or “new event” classifications are defensible.
7. **False-discovery null fails dramatically.** Treating `SNR_white` as a normal deviate would produce false positives across nearly the whole ROI. Any paper-quality analysis must estimate a position/scan-specific empirical noise power and trial distribution, inspect cutout morphology, verify independent arrays/visits and rerun positive controls before any event/rate statement.
8. **Full-sky catalog != exposure.** 27,512 entries include repeats, paths outside this ROI, map start/stop intervals far wider than actual per-pixel dwell. Neither event rate, completeness vs Jy, effective area×days nor 95% upper limits can be deduced.

## Disposition

**Accept for merge:** frozen inventory plus actual in-field FITS feasibility checks, explicit protocol deviations, reproducible raw evidence in code-produced JSON, 9/9 external-service attempts with failures preserved, improved date and time handling, Gaussian width-sensitivity tests, sign-flip and split-night nulls, and real-pixel injections — **strictly marked inconclusive** in both result and root catalog.

**Do not accept:** new astrophysical event, validated null detection, completeness surface, published-event recovery, rate bound, `[REMARKABLE]`, or an entry in `docs/REMARKABLE_FINDINGS.md`. A future *new* pre-registered science study needs measured effective PSF, independent positive-control success, proper correlated-noise null, full catalog crossmatches and validated sky/time exposure. The earlier [PR #192](https://github.com/dovvnloading/Open-Space/pull/192) remains inventory-only.

This review is independent **in its audit method**, not independent as a separate human author/institution.
