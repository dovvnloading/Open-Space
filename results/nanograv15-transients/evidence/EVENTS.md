# Review of surviving timing fits

Each array row is a fit at a selected epoch. Several shapes and sky directions can describe one excursion. The tables give every fit that passed the original internal checks. The complete participant lists, amplitude estimates and uncertainties are linked below.

## Common timing patterns

The empirical p uses the maximum over the entire bank after randomizing pulsar signs. EPTA p values use Holm adjustment across all internally surviving common fits. The DM column gives the coefficient of the achromatic signal on the discovery axis after fitting an independent dispersion delay on each usable telescope-day. For a negative clock signal, positive alignment in this control means agreement with that negative discovery sign. A dash means the fit was unavailable.
