# Referee decision — Euclid DR1-Foundation control-first reselection

**Decision: PASS as a measured, reproducible but INCONCLUSIVE survey-catalogue correction; FAIL any discovery, quasar purity, field completeness, redshift or source artefact-clearance claim.** This contribution can be archived/merged only with the photometric-only caveats in the scientific README. It is **not** an independently established new z>7 quasar and does not belong in the remarkable-findings log.

## Reproducibility and control gate passed

- Independent replay of the ETag-identified original source bytes for **24 matched spectroscopic z>6.5 quasars and 14 labelled dwarfs**, covering **35 source partitions**, reproduces **3/24 old strict**, and the relaxed rule's **21/24 (7/8 z>7) known-Q** and **4/14 dwarf** hits. All 24 control failures are in the [per-object table](output/control_failures.tsv) and [full audit](output/control_audit.json). Old Y−J and VIS S/N losses are overlapping, not exclusive.
- **768/768 HATS source partitions and 888,152,165/888,152,165 advertised publisher rows**, 32 sequential immutable hash-checked batch receipts; **1,959,717** eligibility rows. New top 200 globally by the *frozen photometric heuristic* is reconstructible from 200 retained per partition; not a sampled 2-million-object quasar distribution. [Machine replay](output/referee.json) passes.
- Original 3/24 recovery to 21/24 is real **within the matched, label-tuned control cohort**. Its Wilson 95% interval is 0.690–0.957, but is not a held-out imaging/flux completeness function. **4/14** dwarf retention (CI 0.117–0.546) does not establish field contaminant fraction; galaxies, blends, morphology systematics and other dwarfs are unrepresented. No clean effective sky area.
- Conditional Q-vs-dwarf KDE uses **24 Q +14 dwarf** labelled objects and an arbitrary 0.1% Q prior, has leave-one-out Brier **0.629861** and withholds numerical likelihoods for **46/100** top photometric ranks outside empirical colour support. The numeric scores (54/100 supported) are **not field astrophysical probabilities**.
- Actual public Euclid S3 FITS WCS cutouts successfully supplied **VIS/Y/J/H 251×251 science pixels for all top eight**; image-level provenance is SHA-verified. A qualitative preview review flags rank 2 near a bright streak, rank 5 unusual central PSF/ringing and rank 7 a dark NISP stripe. **These are *visual risks*, not a quantified per-exposure detector diagnosis**. 0/8 are cleared for persistence, ghosts, edge masks, deblending and forced photometry.

## Outstanding scientific gates

**CatWISE/unWISE, motion and public spectra are not cleared.** The 16 leading candidates were queried through IRSA and alternate CatWISE/unWISE routes; CatWISE and unWISE had zero matched W1/W2/forced-flux measurements and service errors remain. Gaia absent rows are not zero proper motion. DESI/SDSS/ESO/MAST/SIMBAD requests returned a mix of missing associations, archive errors and metadata. No actual new spectrum was reviewed and no new redshift was confirmed; no object-specific clean archival-negative claim is warranted.

**Abundance comparison is illustrative only.** ~7.6–18.71 z>7 quasars is an expectation under two different idealized assumptions for a hypothetical complete ~1,900 deg² release. The 1,959,717 eligible MER rows cannot be converted into a yield, purity or absence constraint without an effective area/selection-function and representative control tests.

**Publication label:** *Measured, inconclusive control recovery + unvalidated photometric follow-up*. A ranked source, even with actual mosaic pixels, remains non-discovery-level. A new quasar designation requires new, independently verified spectroscopy. The following work is left explicit: per-exposure artefact masks and image review; robust CatWISE/unWISE forced photometry and motion; comprehensive literature/public spectra crossmatches; injection-recovery with held-out controls.
