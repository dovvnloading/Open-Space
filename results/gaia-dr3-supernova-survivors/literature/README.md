# Object identity and publication checks

The three Gaia sources clearing the raw transverse-speed selection that match the class-control list are Shen's D6-1, D6-2 and D6-3. The other nine had no match in our SIMBAD exact-DR3-ID and 5-arcsec positional queries, or to exact IDs in the 92 targets of El-Badry et al. (2026). The highest-speed DESI white dwarf with a five-sigma parallax is SDSS J125834.93−005946.1, listed in Igoshev et al. and classified DA in the 2026 follow-up survey. Its measured transverse speed already has a published counterpart. The DESI chemistry branch independently resolves its three sources to LP 93−21, LSPM J1240+6710 and 2QZ J110907.9+000130. A dated arXiv search returned 32 records from 2026 through retrieval on November 17. The preserved query searches abstract terms including hypervelocity, LP 40−365, US 708, partially burned and supernova/runaway/white dwarf. Relevant current studies include the 2026 Bhat et al. runaway and the El-Badry et al. survey. We compared full, integer Gaia IDs against ten primary PDFs including Shen, Raddi, El-Badry, Werner, Igoshev, Hollands and Bhat. Papers can use older IDs or aliases. We also joined the earlier repository's 21-source high-velocity index on release-qualified DR3 IDs. A positional/alias miss or an unpublished spectrum remains possible. These checks support the named recoveries. No source qualifies for a discovery-priority claim.
