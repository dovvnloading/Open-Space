# All-sky fast transverse-velocity branch

Twelve Gaia DR3 sources meet the raw observed cuts. Two have one-sided 95% lower transverse speeds above 1,000 km/s under the stated model, Shen's D6-1 and D6-3. D6-2 also passes the raw cuts. For the other nine, the largest lower bound is 811 km/s. This census establishes the yield of an astrometric detection rule. Fast stars can be missed because they move predominantly along the line of sight, have weak parallaxes, lie outside the color/magnitude cuts, or lack a Gaia solution.

## Census and evidence for the threshold

We required total proper motion above 35 mas/yr, observed BP−RP below 1.2, parallax S/N above 3, uncorrected heliocentric transverse speed above 1,000 km/s, and absolute magnitude proxy G + 5 log₁₀(parallax in mas) − 10 above 3.5. The last cut uses apparent G without an extinction correction. Before applying the speed cut, 1,023,709 DR3 sources met the other four criteria. The CSV and VOTable responses agree on that total. A separate count query and ID-only VOTable query returned 12 after the speed cut, with final QUERY_STATUS=OK. [Pre-speed count and receipt](eligible_count_receipt.json) We recomputed all five cuts locally on all 12. An initial wide CSV request for the entire selection ended after the header despite HTTP 200. The enumerated ID set was used to retrieve full records in a separate request. The parent_validation XML and JSON files preserve the independent enumeration.

The kinematics table contains the uncorrected, corrected and lower-bound speeds for each source. We evaluated the individual five-parameter Lindegren correction using the gaiadr3-zeropoint coefficients. At a trial speed v we computed D(v) = 4.74047 μ / v − (π − ZP), in mas. Its variance is the projection of the 3×3 Gaia covariance for parallax and both proper-motion components along the gradient of D, plus (0.020 mas)² as a calibration sensitivity allowance. The lower bound solves D/σ = 1.644854. This is a local Gaussian measurement test, with excellent linearization for the measured high-S/N proper motions. The nominal confidence level applies to a specified source before selection. Tail selection, non-Gaussian blends and spatially correlated Gaia systematics limit its population interpretation. Raw inverse parallax is used in the trigger. The Bailer-Jones geometric distance quantiles give a separate prior-sensitive summary. Columns starting with vt_r_lo, vt_r_med and vt_r_hi are speeds at the catalogue's 16th, 50th and 84th percentile distances with the observed proper motion held fixed. These checks share Gaia observations. The photogeometric prior and the GSP-Phot ordinary-atmosphere fits can be inappropriate for supernova survivors.

Fidelity v2 is 0.9980–1.0000 for all 12. Eleven pass RUWE <1.4, at least 9 visibility periods, no duplicated-source flag, IPD multi-peak fraction ≤2%, and IPD harmonic amplitude <0.1. Gaia 2574148589698264448 has a 6% multi-peak fraction. Direct 3″ cones around each source return 13 rows counting the targets. The extra row is 6094201393488527744, 1.349″ from 6094201393481158656 and 2.506 G magnitudes fainter. This pair has a flux ratio of about 0.099. Its scan-direction-dependent contamination is unknown. The source's high catalogue fidelity illustrates the need to inspect the local scene. Its corrected nominal speed is 1,117 km/s with lower bound 811 km/s. A mirror query with parallax S/N <−3 and |π| in the other cuts returned zero. This tests one route through which spurious solutions enter the sample. A contamination fraction for sources with underestimated positive parallaxes remains unknown.

The DR2-to-DR3 neighbourhood query finds a unique close, flux-consistent counterpart for each source and an additional, fainter DR2 neighbour for the same close pair. DR2 shares observations with DR3. The GSP-Phot model parameters for the other nine sources cover 4,933–5,946 K and log g of 4.54–4.75. The Gaia classifier also labels D6-3 a star with high probability. A catalogued atmosphere fit or the absence of a SIMBAD entry is insufficient to establish the physical nature of a candidate. SIMBAD identified the three Shen objects by exact Gaia DR3 identifier and in 5″ cones around the observed 2016.0 positions. Those cones encompass the offset between 2000 and 2016 for all 12, based on their proper motions. Neither query returned a match for the other nine. All radial velocities in these 12 Gaia source records are null.

## Conditional injection recovery

The 744 templates comprise Gaia DR3 random_index 0 through 4,999 with 16 ≤ G < 20.5 and observed BP−RP < 1.2. Each template supplies its actual solution type, parallax/proper-motion covariance, magnitude, color and quality flags. At each of 1, 2, 4 and 8 kpc and heliocentric 3D speeds of 500, 1,000, 1,500 and 2,500 km/s, we inject 256 isotropic orientations and Gaussian measurement-error triples per template. Observed parallax includes the individual zero point. The 22 off-calibration six-parameter templates use −0.017 mas in the raw simulation and cannot pass the strong gate. The 11 two-parameter templates cannot trigger. The strong gate requires the raw selection, valid zero point, five quality conditions and D(1000)/σ > 1.644854. The calibration allowance enters the significance statistic. Synthetic noise follows the template's reported covariance. The injection table includes standard errors treating templates as the independent sampling units.

For 17 ≤ G < 18 and a 1,500 km/s isotropic speed, strong recovery is 56.2%, 36.3% and 1.89% at 1, 2 and 4 kpc. The corresponding template standard errors are 1.66, 1.62 and 0.41 percentage points. At 19 ≤ G < 20.5 and 2 kpc, none of 87,552 simulated draws pass the strong gate. These efficiencies are conditional on the observed Gaia color/magnitude distribution in each bin. They include projection, distance truncation, ordinary field astrometric errors and measured quality flags, with fixed color and G. Missing photometry, survey sky completeness, extinction, survivor luminosities, non-Gaussian astrometric failures and follow-up completeness remain unmodelled. Estimating a Galactic rate or space density would require these additional inputs.

## Reproduction and sources

From this directory run `python analysis.py` and `python inject.py` on the archived CSVs. Re-fetch with `python fetch.py` and `python verify_parent.py`. These latter commands update dated receipts. Each raw CSV response has an exact ADQL file and a JSON receipt containing its SHA-256. HTTP row order can vary. The nonlinear statistical calculation requires NumPy, SciPy and gaiadr3-zeropoint 0.1.0. Source IDs are read as strings.
