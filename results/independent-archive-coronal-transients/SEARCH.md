# Independent archival measurements, 15 November 2026

This search began after checking the default-branch results directory and open pull requests #88, #90, #91 and #92 on 15 November. The first commit recorded the questions and falsification tests before the analyses finished.

| Investigation | Measured result | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [J1013−0148](../xmm-lss-quasar-quartet/j1013-triplet/README.md) | Three DESI quasar spectra at z ≈ 1.51 form a triangle 199–220 proper kpc across. One Mg II line is more than three times as broad as the other two. | A fourth source has an ambiguous redshift. Physical membership and lensing remain open. |
| [GRB 250902A](../mars-odyssey-grb-timing/README.md) | A pulse matching Fermi reached Mars Odyssey 832.99 s earlier. Its timing annulus misses GECAM-B’s centroid by 3.92°, corresponding to 50.39 s. | The annulus depends on pulse identification and clock calibration. A complex comparison burst missed by 23.75 s. A second March 2026 counterpart is marginal. |
| [Io's southern eruption](../io-2024-eruption-sodium/README.md) | During the eruption onset interval the sulfur torus brightened on both sides. Sodium stayed near its previous brightness, then fell by 28.4% over 25 later observing nights. | The onset is uncertain, the torus samples multiple sources, and a 14-day gap could hide a brief sodium pulse. |
| [XMM-LSS J0225−0447](../xmm-lss-quasar-quartet/README.md) | Four individually cataloged quasars lie within 966.5 proper kpc, with a DESI redshift span of 453 km/s. | Preserving real triplets in randomized data yields a qualifying quartet somewhere in 8.09% of trials. The surrounding galaxy excess is modest. |
| [SDSS J151158.42+344735.8](../class-j151158-detector-hit/README.md) | Two raw detector pixels contain 72.7% of a cataloged [Fe VII] line's local signal. In sixteen calibration lines they contain at most 26.8%. | A detector hit explains the apparent iron line. A weaker line underneath it remains possible. |

The search also recovered the previously published Mars detection of GRB 250309B and the previously published coronal-line fading of SDSS J1342+0530. These served as checks. The linked reports document selection, source attribution, individual controls and prior reports.
