# Independent archival measurements, 15 November 2026

This search began after checking the default-branch results directory and open pull requests #88, #90, #91 and #92 on 15 November. The first commit recorded the questions and falsification tests before the analyses finished. **The quasar groups are candidate associations, the Mars GRB ring is conditional on the pulse match, the Io sulfur brightening has no established cause, and the detector-hit finding is a proposed catalogue correction.**

| Investigation | Measured result | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [J1013−0148](../xmm-lss-quasar-quartet/j1013-triplet/README.md) | Three DESI quasar spectra at z ≈ 1.51 form a triangle 199–220 proper kpc across. One Mg II line is more than three times as broad as the other two. | **Candidate association only.** A fourth source has an ambiguous redshift; a pair-preserving randomized test yielded a chance-completed triangle somewhere in 102/1,000 surveys. Physical membership and lensing remain open. |
| [GRB 250902A](../mars-odyssey-grb-timing/README.md) | A pulse matching Fermi reached Mars Odyssey 832.99 s earlier. Its timing annulus misses GECAM-B’s centroid by 3.92°, corresponding to 50.39 s. | The annulus **depends on correctly identifying the matching pulse** and on an empirical clock calibration; one complex comparison burst missed by 23.75 s. A second March 2026 counterpart is marginal. |
| [Io's southern eruption](../io-2024-eruption-sodium/README.md) | During the eruption onset interval the sulfur torus brightened on both sides. Sodium stayed near its previous brightness, then fell by 28.4% over 25 later observing nights. | **The cause of the sulfur brightening is open**, the eruption onset is uncertain, the torus samples multiple sources, and a **14-day sodium-observation gap could hide a brief sodium pulse**. |
| [XMM-LSS J0225−0447](../xmm-lss-quasar-quartet/README.md) | Four individually cataloged quasars lie within 966.5 proper kpc, with a DESI redshift span of 453 km/s. | **Candidate association only:** preserving real triplets, **809 of 10,000 randomized surveys** (8.09%) yielded at least one comparable quartet somewhere; the surrounding galaxy excess is modest. This is not a binding probability. |
| [SDSS J151158.42+344735.8](../class-j151158-detector-hit/README.md) | Two raw detector pixels contain 72.7% of a cataloged [Fe VII] line's local signal. In sixteen calibration lines they contain at most 26.8%. | **Proposed catalogue correction** to one [Fe VII] identification, best explained by a localized detector hit; a weaker underlying line remains possible. This is not a confirmed astrophysical transient. |

The search also recovered the previously published Mars detection of GRB 250309B and the previously published coronal-line fading of SDSS J1342+0530. These served as checks. The linked reports document selection, source attribution, individual controls and prior reports.
